# Major Histocompatibility Complex
Encoded by HLA genes. Present antigen fragments to T-cell Receptors(TCRs)

| *Trait*             | *MHC-I*                                           | *MHC-II*                                |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| *Loci*                | HLA A,B, and C                                    | HLA DP, DQ, and DR                      |
| *Structure*           | 1 long and 1 short chain                          | 2 equal lenght chains                   |
| *Binding*             | TCR and CD8                                       | TCR and CD4                             |
| *Expressed on*        | All nucleated cells, APC, Platelets               | APCs                                    |
| *Function*            | Endogenous antigens (viral or cytosolic proteins) | Exogenous antigens (Bacterial proteins) |
| *ANtigen loading*     | Loaded from RER after delivery by TAP             | Loaded by invariant chain               |
| *Associated proteins* | Beta-Microglobulin                                | Invariant chain                         |

## Associated Diseases
[[Bare lymphocyte syndrome\|Bare lymphocyte syndrome]] autosomal recessive deficiency oh MHC expression

*HLA-B27* - [[Psoriatic Arthritis\|Psoriatic Arthritis]], [[Ankylosing Spondylitis\|Ankylosing Spondylitis]], [[IBD\|IBD]]-associated, Reactive Arthritis

*HLA-DQ2/DQ8* - [[Celiac disease\|Celiac disease]], [[DM type 1\|DM type 1]]
*HLA-DQB1-0602* - Narcolepsy

*HLA-DR3* - [[DM type 1\|DM type 1]], [[SLE\|SLE]], [[Graves disease\|Graves disease]], [[Hashimotos Thyroiditis\|Hashimotos Thyroiditis]], [[Addison disease\|Addison disease]]
*HLA-DR4* - [[Rheumatoid Arthritis\|Rheumatoid Arthritis]], [[DM type 1\|DM type 1]], [[Addison disease\|Addison disease]]
*HLA-DR5* - [[Pernicious anemia\|Pernicious anemia]], [[Hashimotos Thyroiditis\|Hashimotos Thyroiditis]]

HLA-DRB1\*15:01 - &uarr; Risk of [[Multiple Sclerosis\|Multiple Sclerosis]]
HLA-B\*58:01 - Allopurinol hypersensitivity
HLA-DRB1\*01:03 - Increased risk of [[Ulcerative Colitis\|Ulcerative Colitis]]

MHC II involved in Superantigen presentation in [[Toxic Shock Syndrome\|Toxic Shock Syndrome]]
