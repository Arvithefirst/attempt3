# Duchenne Muscle Dystrophy
Frameshift mutation of the Dystrophin gene(DMD)
>[!info] Dystophin gene
>the DMD gene is the largest coding gene and therefore have &uarr; chances of mutation. Dystrophin he;ps anchor muscles to the ECM. Loss of it leads to myonecrosis, &uarr; CK and aldolase

## Clinical Features
1. Weakness beginning from pelvic girdle ascending
2. Pseudohypertrophy of calf muscles with waddling gait
3. Cardiomyopathy
