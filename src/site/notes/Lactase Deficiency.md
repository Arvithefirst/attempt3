# Lactase Deficiency

## Etiolopathology
1. *Primary* - Age-dependant decline idue to abssence of lactase-persistent allele, more common in Asian, African, and Native american populations
2. *Secondary* - Loss of Intestinal brush border (eg: Rotavirus/autoimmune)
3. *Congenital* - Rare

## Clinical Features
1. Bloating,  cramps, flatulence
2. Osmotic diarrhoea


## Treatment
1. Avoid
