## Pathophysiology
- Defect in CD18 (LFA-1 Integrin) on Phagocytes
- Impaired action of [[Complement\|Complement]] C5b, [[Interleukins#IL-8\|IL-8]], and Leukotriene B4

## Clinical Features
1. Late separation of Umbilical cord
2. Absent Pus
3. Dysfunctional Neutrophils (Recurrent infections with Neutrophilia but no neutrophils at infection site)
