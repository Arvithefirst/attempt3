# Hyperammonemia
Can be acquired (Liver cirrhosis) or Hereditary ([[Urea cycle\|Urea cycle]] enzyme deficiencies) 

## Pathophysiology
1. Increased Ammonia is CNS toxic
2. Increased GABA and Glutamine
3. Decreased Glutamate
4. Decreased $\alpha$-ketoglutarate leads to [[TCA cycle\|TCA cycle]] Inhibition

## Clinical Features
1. Flapping tremors, slurring of speech
2. Somnolence
3. Vomiting, blurring of vision
4. Cerebral edema

## Treatment
- Protein limited diet
- Lactulose to acidify GI tract to help Ammonia excretion
- Antibiotics to kill ammoniagenic bacteria (Rifaxamin)
- Benzoate, Phenylbutyrate, or phenylacetate to react with glycine/glutamine to be excreted
