# Mitochondrial Diseases
Rare disorders primarily affecting tissues with increased energy requirements

### Mitochondrial Myopathies
1. MELAS (Mitochondrial encephalomyopathy with Lactic Acidosis and Strokelike episodes)
2. MERRF (Myoclonic Epilepsy with Ragged Red Fibres)
// diagnosis with microscopy


### Leber Hereditary Optic Neuropathy
Mutations of Complex I of ETC -> Neuronal death of optic nerve and retina -> Subacute blindness. Usually accompanied by neurological symptoms like ataxia, MS-like illness
