# Classic Galactosemia
$$ \text{Galactose-1-Phosphate Uridyltranferase deficiency}$$
## Pathophysiology
Cellular damage by build up of galactitol

## Clinical Features
Starts with breast feeding
1. Failure to thrive
2. Jaundice, hepatomegaly
3. Infantile cataracts
4. Intellectual disabliity

// May predispose to E.coli sepsis


## Treatment
1. Avoid
