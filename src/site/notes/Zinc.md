# Zinc
Cofactor required for 100+ enzymes. Important for the formation fo Zinc fingers

## Deficiency
1. Delayed wound healing, suppressed immunity
2. Male hypogonadism
3. Decreased adult hair
4. Dysgeusia
5. Anosmia

>[!info] Acrodermatitis Enteropathica
>Congenital defect in zinc absorption with classic triad of
>- Hair loss
>- Diarrhoea
>- Inflammatory rash over skin openings and tips of fingers
>
>May predispose to alcoholic cirrhosis
