# Hereditary Fructose Intolerance
$$ \text{Aldolase B Deficiency}$$

## Pathophysiology
Disorder of [[Fructose Metabolism\|Fructose Metabolism]]
1. Build up of Fructose-1-Phosphate
2. &darr; available phosphate
3. Inhibition of gluconeogenesis and glycogenolysis

## Clinical Features
*Present only after eating lots of fructose (Fruits, juices, honey)*
1. Hypoglycaemia
2. Jaundice, cirrhosis, vomiting

## Treatment
1. &darr; decreased intake of fructose, sucrose, and sorbitol
