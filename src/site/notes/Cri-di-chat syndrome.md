# Cri-di-chat Syndrome

"Cry of the cat" syndrome -> Congenital deletion of *short arm of Chromosome 5*

## Clinical Features
1. Microcephaly
2. Moderate to severe intellectual disability
3. *High pitched cry*
4. VSD
