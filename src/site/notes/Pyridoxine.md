# Pyridoxine

## Vitamin $B_6$ Pyridoxine
Converted to Pyridoxal phosphate(PLP) a cofactor for 
- Transamination of ALT/AST
- Glycogen phosphorylase
- Synthesis of  Glutathione, Cystathione, Heme, [[Niacin\|Niacin]], Histamine (see [[Cobalamin#Methylation cycle and Fatty acid oxidation\|here]])
- Synthesis of Neurotransmitters(Epi, Norad, Dopamine, Serotonin, GABA)

### Deficiency
Usually precipitated by Isoniazid or OC pills
- Convulsions
- Hyperirritability
- *Peripheral neuropathy*
- Sideroblastic anemia
