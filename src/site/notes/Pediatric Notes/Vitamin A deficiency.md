Also called Xerophthalmia
## Clinical features
1. Defective Dark adaptation
2. Xerophthalmia
3. Conjunctival ulcer
4. Hyperkeratosis
5. Growth failure

## Classification
X1A - Xerophthalmia
X1B - Bitot Spots
X2 - Corneal Xerosis
X2A - Corneal ulcer <1/3rd
X2B - Corneal ulcer >1/3rd

XN - Night blindness
XF - Fundal changes
XS - Corneal scarring

## Treatment
50, 000 IU <6months
100,000 IU <1year
200,000 IU >1year
