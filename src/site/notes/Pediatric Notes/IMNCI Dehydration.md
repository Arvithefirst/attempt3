//If the child has low weight or another severe condition then urgent referral while giving [[Pediatric Notes/WHO ORS\|ORS]] and keeping warm

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Severe Dehydration</mark>
Any 2 of 
- Lethargic/Unconscious
- Sunken eyes 
- Very slow skin pinch
- Not able to drink(Only for >2months)

Treat with
- Prophylactic Gentamycin/Ampicillin (Only for newborns)
- [[Pediatric Notes/Dehydration plan C\|Dehydration plan C]] + Referral to hospital 

<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Some dehydration</mark>
Any two of
- Iriitable, Restless
- Sunken eyes
- Slow skin pinch
- Drinks eagerly(only for >2months)

Treat with
- Prophylactic Gentamycin/Ampicillin (Only for newborns)
- [[Pediatric Notes/Dehydration Plan B\|Dehydration Plan B]] + Follow up in 5 days (2 for newborns)

<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">No dehydration</mark>
Not enough signs 
Treat with 
- [[Pediatric Notes/Dehydration Plan A\|Dehydration Plan A]] + Follow up in 5 days




<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Severe persistant diarrhoea</mark>
- Lasting more than 14 days
- Dehydration present

Treat with 
- IM Ampicillin/Gentamycin
- Keep warm
- Prevent Hypoglycemia
- Refer to hospital (only step >2months)

<mark class="hltr-yellow">Persistant diarrhoea</mark>
- Only for >2months
- Lasting more than 14 days
- Dehydration Absent

Treat with 
- Proper feeding
- Zinc supplement
- Vitamin A dose


<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Severe Dysentry</mark> (Yellow in >2years)
- Blood in stool

Treat with 
- IM Ampicillin/Gentamycin
- Keep warm
- Prevent Hypoglycemia
- Refer to hospital
