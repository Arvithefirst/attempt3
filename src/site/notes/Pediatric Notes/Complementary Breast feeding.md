Done after 6 months. Involves adding foods to the diet in addition to breast milk

## Counselling advise
Upto 6 months
- Only breastfeeding. No water / fluids / medicines
6 months to 1 years
- Breastfeed as demanded
- Feed mashed simple carbs with ghee, undiluted milk, Simple sugars from fruits
12 months to 2 years
- BNreastfeed on demand
- More simple carbs with ghee/oil
- Cooked vegetables, dals, halwa, postatoes, milk, cereals
Ovewr 2 years
- Normal food
