Height below third centile or >-2SD

## Etiology
Familial short stature can be either familial of constitutional
Pathological short stature
1. Undernutrition, Malabsorption syndromes
2. Chornic systemic illness
3. Cerebral palsy
4. Congenital heart disease, [[Medicine notes/Cystic Fibrosis\|Cystic Fibrosis]]
5. Endocrine disorders
	1. Growth hormone deficiency/resisteance
	2. Hypothyroidism
	3. Cushings
	4. Pseudohypoparathyroidism
6. Skeletal dysplasia
7. Genetic syndromees - Downs turners

## Clinical Investigations
1. Mid parental height(MPH)
2. Body proportionality (US:LS ratio)
3. SMR Staging
4. Other etiology specific findings

## Investigations
1. CBC, ESR
2. Bone age
3. Urinalysis, Stool examination
4. LFT, RFT, BSL
5. TFT
6. Celiac serology
7. Groewth hormone
8. Serum insulin like growth factors 1 and 3
9. MRI Brain
