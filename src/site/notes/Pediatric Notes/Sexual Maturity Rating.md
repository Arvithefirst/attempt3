## Girls
1. Prepubertal, no hair
2. Appearance of breasdt bud, Sparse hair along labia
3. Generalised breast enlargement, Course pubic hair
4. Nipple and areola mound, Hair over Mons Pubis
5. Adult breast, Adult pubic hair spreading to medial thighs

## Boys
1. Prepubertal, Testicular volume <3ml
2. Reddening of scrotum, testicular volume <4, Scanty hair at base of penis
3. Increase in penis lenght, testicular volume <10ml, Curling and darkening of hair
4. Increase of girth of penis, Darkening of scrotum, Abundant hair
5. Adult penis, Adult hair spreading to medial thighs
