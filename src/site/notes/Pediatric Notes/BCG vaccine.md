Bacillus Calmette guerin Vaccine for TB using bovine bacilli
- *Live attenuated* lysophilised
- 0.05ml / 0.1ml ID at birth / >1 year in Left Deltiod
- Photosensitive, store at 2-8°C

*Prevents only 50% of primary infections* but 80% of incidence of severe disease

>[!bug] BCG scar
>immediate - 5mm wheal
>3 weeks. - 2-3mm Papule
>6 weeks - 6mm papule
>12 weeks - Papule ulcerates and heals
