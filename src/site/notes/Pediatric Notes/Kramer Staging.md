clinical estimation of Serum Bilirubin

*Zone 1* - Above neck - *4-6mg/dl*
*Zone 2* - Over trunk above umbilicus - *6-8mg/dl*
*Zone 3* - Above knees - *8-12mg/dl*
*Zone 4* - Over shins, forearms - *12-14mg/dl*
*Zone 5* - Over palms soles - *>15mg/dl*
