Measles Mumps Rubella vaccine, part of [[Pediatric Notes/National Immunisation Schedule\|NIS]]
- Live attenuated
- 0.5ml SC in Right deltoid or anterolateral thigh at 9-12 and 16-24 months
- Photosensitive store at 4-8°C

#### Adverse reactions
- 'Measles like' reaction
- Aseptic meningitis (mumps)
- Lymphadenopathy 

#### Contraindications
1. Immunosuppressed
2. Untreated TB


Mumps vaccination is recommended also for young adults as it may cause Oophoritis. 

Rubella vaccination is mainly for prevention of [[Pediatric Notes/Congenital Rubella\|Congenital Rubella]] and not Primary rubella infection
