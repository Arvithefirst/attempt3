Refers to both under nutrition and over nutrition. HOwever since over nutrition isnt really a. problem im writing the rest of this as if im talking about under nutrition

## Definitions
Stunting - Low Height for age
Wasting - Low Weight for Height
Underweight - Low weight for age

## Severe Acute Malnutrition
Defined as 
1. Weight for height >-3sd from mean
2. Visible severe wasting
3. MUAC <11.5cm
4. Bipedal pitting edema

Can present clinically as Marasmus or kwashiorkar

## Clinical features
1. Deranged [[Pediatric Notes/Anthropometry\|Anthropometry]]
2. Signs of dehydration
3. Shock
4. Pallor
5. Vitamin A deficiency
6. Local infectins
7. Hypothermia

## Treatment
Broadly aims to treat 
1. Treat Hypoglycemia
2. Treat Hypiothermia
3. Treat Dehydration
4. Correct Electrolytes
5. Prevent Infection
6. Supplement Micronutrients
7. Initiate feeding
8. Catch up groweth
9. Sensory stimulation
10. Prepare for follow up
