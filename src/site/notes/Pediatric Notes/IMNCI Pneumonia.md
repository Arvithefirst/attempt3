>[!faq] Fast breathing
0-2 months - >60/min
2-12 months - >50/min
1-5 years - >40/min
#### General danger signs
- Convulsions
- Decreased Consciousness
- Inability to feed

<mark class="hltr-red">Severe pneumonia</mark>
- Chest Indrawing
- Stridor in calm child
- General danger signs
Treat with
- Chloramphenicol(40mg/kg) or Amoxicillin(250g TDS x 5 days)
- URGENT hospitalisation

<mark class="hltr-yellow">Pneumonia</mark>
- Fast breathing
Treat with
- Amoxicillin(250mg TDS x 5 days)
- Treat cough, soothe throat
- Follow up in 2 days

<mark class="hltr-green">No Pneumonia</mark>
- uwu
Treat with
- If longer than 30 days then refer
- Soothe throat
- Follow up in 5 days
