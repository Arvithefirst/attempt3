Oral [[Pediatric Notes/WHO ORS\|WHO ORS]]
#### Daily requirement
$100ml/kg$ for the first 10 kg
$50ml/kg$ for the next 10 kg
$20ml/kg$ for each subsequent kg

$$15kg = (10kg*100ml/kg) + (5kg*50ml/kg) = 1250ml$$
#### +Rehydration
75ml/kg over 4 hours or as per WHO Recommended table

#### +Maintenence to replace losses
After signs of dehydration disappear
10ml/kg per stool
