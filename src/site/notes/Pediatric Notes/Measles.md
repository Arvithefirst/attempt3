Common and serious exanthemous illness

#### Etiopathogenesis
1. Paramyxovirus infection. Droplet transmission
2. Incubation period 10 days
3. 90% secondary attack rate

## Clinical features
Common in preschool kids (after plqacental antibodies wear out)
### Prodromal stage
1. Fever, rhinorrhoea. Dry cough, Conjunctival congestion
2. Koplicks spots*
\* *Grey or white lesions resembling sand grains surrounded by erythema appearing on the buccal mucosa opposite to the lower second molars* 
### Eruptive stage
1. Maculopapular dusky red rash appearing on 4th day usually behind the ear
2. Spreads along hairline and eventually to face, neck, truck(*centripetal*)
3. Disappears in the same order. Leaves behing brown discoloration
#### Complications
1. Secondary bacterial infection (otitis media, pneumonia, TB, laryngitis)
2. Persistant diarrhoea, hepatitis, Malnutrition
3. Noma (gangrene of cheeks)
4. Acute encephalitis, Subacute sclerosing panencephalitis(fatal)

## Investiagtions
1. Chracteristic rash and koplick spots is pathognomic
2. IgM anti-measles antibody test positive after 3 days of fever

## Treatment
1. Symptomatic treatment
2. 200,000 IU Vitamin A (100,000 IU below 1 year)
