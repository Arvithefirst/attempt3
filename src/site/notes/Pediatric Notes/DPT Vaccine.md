Diphtheria toxoid, Pertussis whole cell/acellular and Tetanus toxoid. Given in [[Pediatric Notes/National Immunisation Schedule\|NIS]]
- 0.5ml IM Anterolateral thigh at 6, 10, and 14 weeks booster at 16-24 months
- Photosensitive, store at 2-8°C

### Diphtheria
Toxoid is inactivated with formalin and adsorbed onto Aluminum hydroxide. Quantity is expresed in limit of flocculation(Lf)

Given as part of Reduced Antigen Diphtheria Vaccine (Td) and Reduced Antigen Acellular Pertussis Vaccine (Tdap)

### Pertussis (whooping cough)
Whole cell pertussis in DTwP is responsible for the majority of the side effects(local pain and fever.) 

Acellular pertussis shows less side effects but is more costly

Pertussis immunity wears of by adolescence necessitating booster Tdap at 10-12 years

### Tetanus
Also given in 2nd trimester 1 month apart to prevent neonatal tetany

Toxoid is inactivated in formalin and Adsorbed to Aluminum Hydroxide. Each dose contains 5 Lf

Always given as post-exposure prophylaxis for wounds if immunisation status is unknown, >10 years ago. Given along with immunoglobulin if wound is not clean and incised
