Usually affects children 1-4 years old
1. Fat suggar baby appearance
2. Mild to gross bipedal edema
3. Severe muscle wasting
4. Flaky paint/enamel spots skin dyspigmentations
5. Petechiae and ulceraation over abdomen
6. Cheilosis, angular stomatitis
7. Dyspigmentation of hair. Flag sign
8. No hunger, irritable mood
9. Anorexia, vomitting, distension, watery stools
10. Anaaemia
11. Cold pale extremities. Hypotension
12. Diminished renal function
