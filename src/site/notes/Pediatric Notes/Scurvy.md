Vitamin C(Ascorbic acid) deficiency - RDA 30-40mg for children, 40-70mg in adults

## Clinical features
1. Diarrhoea, Anorexia, Pallor, Irritability, Infection
2. Subperiosteal hemorrhage, Large bone tenderness, Scorbutic rosary
3. Hemorrhagic sign

## Investigations
1. "Ground glass appearance" of long bones
2. Thinning of cortex, Triangular defect, White line of Frankel, White rings onf calcifications

## Treatment
1. 100-200mg Orally/Parentally
2. Daily intake 100ml Orange/tomato pulp
