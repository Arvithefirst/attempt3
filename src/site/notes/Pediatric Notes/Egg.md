*169kcal/100mg, 13g*

#### Reference protein
Used as 'reference protein' because it has the best bioavailability calculated by Net protein utilisation and Biological value

$\text{True Digestibility(TD)} = \frac{\text{Nitrogen Absorbed}}{\text{Nitrogen Intake}}* 100$

$\text{Biological value(BV)} = \frac{\text{Nitrogen Retained}}{\text{Nitrogen Absorbed}}* 100$

$$\text{Net Protein Utilisation(NPU)} =\frac{\text{TD * BV}}{100} =\frac{\text{Nitrogen Retained}}{\text{Nitrogen Intake}}*100$$
