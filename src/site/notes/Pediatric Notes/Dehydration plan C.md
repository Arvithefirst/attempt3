#### IV fluids (Ringer lactate with 5% Dextrose)
*100ml/kg bolus dose*
- Over 6 hours for <1year
- Over 3 hours for >1year
Monitored every 15-30 minutes for signs of shock/rehydration

Followed by
*30ml/kg*
- Over 1 hour for <1year
- Over 30 min for >1year
*70ml/kg*
- Over 5 hours for <1year
- Over 2.5 hours for >year
