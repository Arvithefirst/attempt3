*65-70kcal/dl, 0.9-1.1g/dl.* Low vitamins D and K
#### Carbohydrates
- High lactose - 6-7g/dl. Helps in absorption of calcium and Lactobacilli microbiome
- Galactose helps form galactocerebrosides
#### Proteins
- Low (0.9-1.1g/dl) compared to animal milk
- Contains Lactaobumin and Lactglobulin which are easy to digest
- Contains taurine and Cysteine necessary for neurotransmission an dmodulation
#### Fats
- Rich in PUFA necessary for myelnation
- Rich in Omega 2 and 6 required for Prostaglandin/Cholesterol synthesis
#### Vitaimns
- Suffient and Bioavailable enough uptil 6 months
#### Water and Electrolytes
- 88% water
- Low solute load
#### Immunity
- Mainly IgA, Macrophages, Lympohocytes, Lactoferrin, Interferon
- 14 times less likely to diue of diarrhoea, 4 times less in Respiratory illness
#### Other benefits
- Growth factors, hormones -> better intestinal development, less likely to develop allergy
- Lower chance of Ear infections, Hypertension, Diabetes etc
- Better maternal onding, higher IQ
