*Gross motor* - Neck holding
*Fine motor* - Bidextrous reach
*Social* - Social smile, recognises mother
*Language* - Coos 

#### Gross motor
- Spine and pull to sit up - shows decreasing head lag
- Ventral suspension - shows ability to lift head improves over 2, 6, 8 weeks
- Sits with arched back

#### Fine motor
- Fist usually open 
- Bidexterous reach towards object in midline. Overshoots

#### Social 
- Follows mopther at 1 month
- Social smile at 3 months(different from spontaneous smile)
- Recognises mother at 3 months

#### Language
- Vocalises vowel sounds
- Laughs, squeals
