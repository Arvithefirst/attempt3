Occipito frontal circumference >3SD below Mean

## Etiology
1. Isolated
	1. Autosomal recessive, dominant, or X-linked
2. Syndromic
	1. Trisomy 13, 18, 21
	2. Monosomy 1p36 deletion
	3. Wiliam, Cri-du-chat, Secke, Smith WTF ARE ANY OF THESE
3. Structural disease
	1. Neural tube defects
	2. Lissencephaly
4. Metabolilc disorders
	1. Phenylketonuria
	2. DM in mother
5. Infections
	1. TORCH
	2. Meningitis
6. Teratogens
	1. Alcohol, Tobacco, Marijuana, Cocaine
	2. Antineoplastics, Anti-epilepptics
	3. Radiation
7. Perinatal insult
	1. Hypxic Ischaemic insult
	2. Hypoglycemia
8. Endocrine
	1. Hypothyroidism
	2. Hypopituitarism
	3. Adrenal insifficiency
