>[!quote] Defined as any *One* of
>- Weight for Height >-3SD from mean
>- Presence of Bipedal edema
>- Mid-Upper Arm Circumference <11.5cm

## Clinical Syndromes a/w SAM
### Marasmus
Rapid or Acute malnutrition
#### Clinical features
1. Severe wasting
2. Loss of fat pads leads to :"Monkey facies, "Baggy pants" Buttocks
3. Alert
4. No edema

### Kwashiorkar
Chronic, Characteriused by edema.
#### Clinical features
1. Fat "Sugar baby" appearance
2. 5-20% body weight in Edema
3. Severe muscle wasting
4. Incerased pigmentation, desquamation, Dyspigmentation - Flaky/Enamel spots
5. Lesions on mucous membranes - Angular stomatitis, Cheilosis, HSV
6. Dyspigmentation of hair, Loss of curls. Flag Sign
7. Apathy, Irritability, No hunger
8. Anorexia
9. Anemia
10. Cold extremities, Hypotension
11. Low GFR, [[Medicine notes/Metabolic Acidosis\|Metabolic Acidosis]]

## Management
Divided into 1st week of stabilisation followed by several weeks of rehabilitation
1. *Treat hypoglycemia*
	1. IV Dextrose 10% 5ml/kg followed by 50gm Sucrose by NG tube
	2. F-75 Diet
2. *Treat Hypothermia* - Warming by Clothes of heaters
3. *Treat dehydration*
	1. [[Pediatric Notes/WHO ORS\|ORS]]
	2. Zinc and potassium supplement
4. *Correct Electrolyte Imbalance*
	1. Sodium restriction
	2. Potassium and magnesium salts
5. *Treat and Prevent infections*
	1. Investigate with Chest Xray. CBC, TLC, Mantoux, Cultures, PBS, CSF
	2. Antibiotics
6. *Correct Micronutrient deficiencies*
	1. Vitamin A, K
	2. Zinc, Copper
	3. Iron, Folic acid
7. *Initiating re-feeding*
	1. Breast feeding
	2. Nasogastric if not takingn orally
	3. Start with F-75 (75kcal/dl, 1g)
8. *Catch-up growth*
	1. Ready to use therapeutic food (RUTF)
	2. Switch to F-100 (100kcal/dl, 3g)
9. *Sensory stimulation and support* 
10. *Follow up and recovery*

## Prevention
1. Home care
	1. Exclusive and complementary breast feeding
	2. Vaccinations
2. Community-Based Therapeutic Care
	1. Health Education
	2. Growth monitoring
	3. Integrated health plans
3. National level
	1. ICDS
	2. Mid day meal schemes
	3. National anemia prophylaxius program
