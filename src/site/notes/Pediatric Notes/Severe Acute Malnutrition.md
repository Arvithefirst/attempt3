Characterised as Marasmus, Kwashiorkar or Marasmic Kwashiorkar

## Types
### Marasmus
Rapid or Acute malnutrition
#### Clinical features
1. Severe wasting
2. Loss of fat pads leads to :"Monkey facies, "Baggy pants" Buttocks
3. Alert
4. No edema

### Kwashiorkar
Chronic, Characteriused by edema.
#### Clinical features
1. Fat "Sugar baby" appearance
2. 5-20% body weight in Edema
3. Severe muscle wasting
4. Incerased pigmentation, desquamation, Dyspigmentation - Flaky/Enamel spots
5. Lesions on mucous membranes - Angular stomatitis, Cheilosis, HSV
6. Dyspigmentation of hair, Loss of curls. Flag Sign
7. Apathy, Irritability, No hunger
8. Anorexia
9. Anemia
10. Cold extremities, Hypotension
11. Low GFR,m Acidosis

## Management
Divided into 1st week of stabilisation followed by several weeks of rehabilitation
1. Treat hypoglycemia
	1. IV Dextrose 10% 5ml/kg followed by 50gm Sucrose by NG tube
	2. F-75 Diet
2. Treat Hypothermia - Warming by Clothes of heaters
3. Treat dehydration
