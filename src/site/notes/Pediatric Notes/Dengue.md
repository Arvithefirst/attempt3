Dengue Flavivirus infection (serovars DENV-1, DENV-2, DENV-3, DENV-4)

## Aetiology
- Aedes aegypti mosquito (Voracious, Night-time feeder)
- Endogenous, Fresh water breeder
- Man or monkeys act as reservoir
- Incubation period: 4-10 days
- Consecutive infection of *different* serovars is more *severe* due to enhancing effect of antibodies

#### Pathogenesis
- Infection of endothelial cells causes plasma leakage
- Antibodies directed against NS1 cross react with platelet membrane protein

## Clinical features
#### Febrile phase
Last 2-7 days
- Fever, flushing, bodyaches, Myalgia, Nausea, Vomitting
- Minor hemorrhagic events
- Hepatomegaly
#### Critical Phase
Starts on days 3-7, during defeverescense
- Massive Dehydration due to fluid extravasation -> Shock 
- Raised PCV, Falling thrombocytopenia
- Hepatitis, Myositis, Encephalitis
#### Recovery Phase
Starts after 1-2 days of critical phase.
- Fluid gradually reabsorbed over 2-3 days
- Rash of "isles of white in a sea of red"
- Pruritis, Bradycardia, Pulmonary edemafo

>[!info] Diagnostic criteria
>*Labratory diagnosis* - Platelets <100,000cumm
>*Clinical diagnosis* - High grade fever, hemorrhagic manifestations, Fluid extravasation, tender hepatomegaly

## Investigations
1. High Packed cell volume, Thrombocytopenia, Leucopenia
2. Low albumin, total proteins
3. Markedly raised AST, ALT
4. NS1 antigen, PCR
5. IgG or IgM antibody tests
6. Xray may show pleural effusion
7. USG shows gall bladder edema

## Treatment 
Depends on classification as 
- Undifferentiated fever
- Dengue without warning signs
- Dengue with warning signs
- Dengue Shock Syndrome

#### Undifferentiated fever
Antipyretic duh

#### Dengue without warning signs
Paracetamol + Fluids

#### Dengue with warning signs
- Abdominal pain
- Persistant vomitting
- Clinical fluid accumulation
- Mucosal bleed
- Lethargy, Restlessness
- Liver enlargement >2cm

IV Fluids 
- 7ml/kg for 1 hour
- 5ml/kg for hour
- 3ml/kg/hr for next 24-48 hours

#### Dengue shock syndrome
- Severe plasma leakage -> shock, respiratory distress
- Severe organ involvement -> Raised ALT/AST, Impaired consciousness

Ramping up fluid replacement until improvement. followed by 5ml/kg for 1 hour + 3ml/kg for next 24-48 hours
- 10ml/kg for first hour
- 15ml/kg next hour
- 10ml/kg colloid
- Blood transfusion

If BP unrecordable jump to 20ml/kg
