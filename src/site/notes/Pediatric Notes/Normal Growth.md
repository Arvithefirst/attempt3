## Approximate growth
### Weight
Birth - 3kg
	10% weight loss in first few days
	25-30g/day for 3 months
	400g/month upto 1 year
5 months - 6kg (Doubles)
1 year - 9kg (Triples)
2 years - 12kg (Quadruples)
3 years - 15 kg (x 5)
5 years - 18kg (x 6)
7 Years - 21kg (x 7)
	3kg/year until puberty
### Height 
Birth - 50cm
6 Months - 65cm 
1 Years - 75cm (+25)
2 Years - 90cm
3 Years - 95cm
4 Years - 100cm (Doubles)
	+6cm/year until 12 years
### Head circumference
Birth - 34cm
	+2cm/month x 3 
	+1cm/month x 3 
	+0.5cm/month x 6 
1 Years - 46cm (+2)
2 Years - 48cm(+1)
3 Years - 49cm(+1)
