Refractory Rickets with 
1. Normal pH
2. Normal Low Phosphate, Normal Calcium
3. Normal PTH
## Etiology
1. X-linked dominant (PHEX gene)
2. Defect in Phosphate reabsorption

## Clinical features
1. Genu valgum, Coxa Vawa, Genu Varum
2. Skull deformities

## Management
1. Vitmasin D supplement
2. Phosphate 30-50mg/kg
