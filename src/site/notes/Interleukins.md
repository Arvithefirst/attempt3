# Interleukins

#### IL-1
*   **Aliases:** Lymphocyte Activating Factor / Leukocytic Pyrogen
*   **Mechanism:** Core innate pyrogen; induces endothelial adhesion molecules (ICAM-1, VCAM-1), costimulates T-cells, and drives hepatic acute-phase reactant synthesis.
#### IL-2
*   **Aliases:** T-cell Growth Factor
*   **Mechanism:** T-cell and Treg mitogen; binds the high-affinity [[Interleukins#IL-2\|IL-2]]R complex (CD25/CD122/CD132) to drive rapid clonal expansion and survival.
#### IL-4
*   **Aliases:** B-cell Stimulatory Factor 1
*   **Mechanism:** Primary driver of Th2 differentiation via STAT6; induces B-cell class-switching to IgE and IgG4, and downregulates Th1/Th17 pathways.
#### IL-5
*   **Aliases:** Eosinophil Differentiation Factor
*   **Mechanism:** Lineage-specific differentiation, proliferation, and survival factor for eosinophils; induces eosinophil chemotaxis and degranulation.
#### IL-6
*   **Aliases:** Interferon-β2 / B-cell Stimulatory Factor 2
*   **Mechanism:** Pleiotropic mediator of systemic inflammation; activates hepatic CRP synthesis via gp130, drives B-cell differentiation, and cooperates with TGF-β to induce Th17 differentiation.
#### IL-10
*   **Aliases:** Cytokine Synthesis Inhibitory Factor
*   **Mechanism:** Potent anti-inflammatory feedback regulator; downregulates MHC class II, B7 costimulatory molecules (CD80/CD86), and pro-inflammatory cytokine synthesis (TNF-α, [[Interleukins#IL-1\|IL-1]], [[Interleukins#IL-6\|IL-6]]) in antigen-presenting cells.
#### IL-12
*   **Aliases:** NK Cell Stimulatory Factor
*   **Mechanism:** Heterodimer (p35/p40) that activates STAT4 to drive naive CD4+ T-cell differentiation into the Th1 phenotype; potently induces IFN-γ production in NK and CD8+ T-cells.
#### IL-13
*   **Aliases:** NC30
*   **Mechanism:** Th2-derived cytokine that signals via [[Interleukins#IL-4\|IL-4]]Rα/[[Interleukins#IL-13\|IL-13]]Rα1; drives goblet cell hyperplasia, mucus hypersecretion, and subepithelial tissue remodeling/fibrosis.
#### IL-17
*   **Aliases:** CTLA-8 / [[Interleukins#IL-17\|IL-17]]A
*   **Mechanism:** Homodimeric Th17 effector cytokine; binds [[Interleukins#IL-17\|IL-17]]RA/RC to induce epithelial and stromal secretion of CXCL8 ([[Interleukins#IL-8\|IL-8]]) and G-CSF, driving massive neutrophil recruitment.
#### IL-23
*   **Aliases:** Th17 Stimulating Factor
*   **Mechanism:** Heterodimer sharing the p40 subunit with [[Interleukins#IL-12\|IL-12]] linked to a unique p19 subunit; activates STAT3 to stabilize, survival-enhance, and expand committed Th17 cell populations.
#### IL-31
*   **Aliases:** Pruritogenic Cytokine
*   **Mechanism:** Th2-derived cytokine that binds the [[Interleukins#IL-31\|IL-31]]RA/OSMR complex on nociceptive small-diameter dorsal root ganglion (DRG) neurons to directly transmit pruritic signaling.
#### IL-33
*   **Aliases:** NF-HEV / Alarmin
*   **Mechanism:** [[Interleukins#IL-1\|IL-1]] family alarmin sequestered in epithelial/endothelial nuclei; released upon mechanical stress or necrosis to bind the ST2 ([[Interleukins#IL-1\|IL-1]]RL1) receptor, potently activating ILC2s and Th2 immunity.
