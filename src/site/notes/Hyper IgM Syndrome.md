## Pathophysiology
Most commonly due to CD40L defect on [[T-cells\|T-cells]] 
- Failure of class switching (Normal or &uarr; [[Antibodies#IgM\|IgM]], &darr; &darr; [[Antibodies#IgG\|IgG]], [[Antibodies#IgE\|IgE]], [[Antibodies#IgA\|IgA]])
- Failure to make germinal centres in [[Lymph nodes\|Lymph nodes]]

## Clinical Features
- Severe pyogenic infections in early life
- Pneumocystis, Cryptosporidium, [[Cytomegalovirus\|CMV]]
