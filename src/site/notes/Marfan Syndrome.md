# Marfan Syndrome

Connective tissue disorder affecting skeleton, heart and eyes.

## Pathophysiology
1. FBN-1 gene mutation on chromosome 15 results in defective fibrillin-1
2. Fibrillin-1 responsible for function of [[Elastin\|Elastin]]

## Clinical Features
1. Tall with long extremities
2. Pectus excavatum or pectus carinatum
3. Hypermobile joints, Aracnodactyly
4. Cystic medial necrosis of Aorta
5. Aortic root rupture or aneurysm
6. Mitral Valve Prolapse
7. &uarr; risk of spontaneous pneumothorax
8. Upward dislocation of lens
