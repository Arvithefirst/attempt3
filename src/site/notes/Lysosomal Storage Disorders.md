# Lysosomal Storage Disorders
Group of disorders that result in accumulation of abnormally folded proteins. Some have increased incidence in Ashkenazi Jews

![Screenshot 2026-09-14 at 12.01.00 AM.png\|10000](/img/user/Screenshot%202026-09-14%20at%2012.01.00%20AM.png)
## Disease of the Lysosome
1. [[Tay-Sachs Disease\|Tay-Sachs Disease]] (Cherry red spot, NO hepatomegaly)
2. [[Fabry Disease\|Fabry Disease]]
3. [[Metachromatic Leukodystrophy\|Metachromatic Leukodystrophy]]
4. [[Krabbe Disease\|Krabbe Disease]] (CN II atrophy)
5. [[Gaucher Disease\|Gaucher Disease]] (Crumpled tissue macrophages)
6. [[Neimann-Pick Disease\|Neimann-Pick Disease]] (Cherry red spot, Lipid macrophages AND Hepatosplenomegaly)
7. [[Hurler Syndrome\|Hurler Syndrome]] (Skeletal abnormalities, Clouded cornea)
8. [[Hunter Syndrome\|Hunter Syndrome]] (Milder Hurler, NO clouding)
