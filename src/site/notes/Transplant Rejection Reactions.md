## Hyperacute
- Within minutes
- Pre-existing host antibodies to donor antigens([[Type II Hypersensitivity Reaction\|Type II Hypersensitivity Reaction]]). activate [[Complement\|Complement]]
- Widespread thrombosis of graft vessels, ischaemia and fibrinoid necrosis

## Acute
- Weeks to months
- Either
	- Humoural: Formation of antibodies against donor antigen (a/w Cd4 deposition)
	- Cellular: [[T-cells\|T-cells]] response against donor [[Major Histocompatibility Complex\|Major Histocompatibility Complex]] ([[Type IV Hypersensitivity Reaction\|Type IV Hypersensitivity Reaction]])
- Vasculitis of graft vessels & Dense interstitial lymphocytic infiltrate

## Chronic
- Months to Years
- [[Type II Hypersensitivity Reaction\|Type II]] and [[Type IV Hypersensitivity Reaction\|Type IV hypersensitivity reaction]]
- Arteriosclerosis, Proliferation of vascular smooth muscle, parenchymal atrophy, interstitial fibrosis

Also [[Graft vs Host Disease\|Graft vs Host Disease]]
