# Edwards Syndrome
2nd most common autosomal trisomy leading in live birth 

## Clinical Features
1. *P*rominent Occiput
2. *R*ocker-bottom foot
3. *I*ntellectual disability
4. *N*ondisjunction
5. *C*lenched fists with overlapping fingers
6. Low set *E*ars
7. Micrognathia
8. Congenital heart disease
9. Myelomeningocele, Omphalocele
