## Types
### Type I
1. Vitamin D Deficiency
	1. Diminished intake
	2. Diminished absorption - malabsorption syndorme, Biliary diesase
	3. Lack on sunlight
2. Disturbance in Vitamin D metabolism
	1. Hepatic - 25-hydroxylase deficiency, Anticonvulsive therapy
	2. Renal - 1- hydroxylase deficiency
	3. hereditary
### Type II
1. Defect in phosphate absorption
	1. Hypophosphatemic rickets
	2. Fanconi syndrome
	3. Renal Tubular Acidosis
2. Diminiashed intake of phosphates

## Clinical features
1. Craniotabes
2. Frontal Bossing
3. Broadening of long ends
4. Delayed teeth eruption
5. harrisons sulcus
6. Pigeon chest
7. Rachitic rosary
8. Muscular hypotonia - pot belly, lumbar lordosis
9. Knock knees, bow legs
