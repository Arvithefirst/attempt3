Caused by *fall on outstretched hand*. Typically seen in children

#### Pathology
Fracture line lies transversly between epiphysises right above condyles. May be of extension type(80%) or flexion type(20%.) 

## Clinical features
1. *Pain* w h/o fall and *inability to move elbow*. May be associated with gross *swelling*
2. May have unusual posterior poroinence on tip of elbow
3. May have signs of relevant nerve injury

>[!info] Radiological examination
>Typically widely displaced and therefore easy to identify

## Treatment
*Undisplaced fractures* can be treated by immobilisation with plaster with 90 degrees flexion at elbow

*Displaced fractures* can be treated by
- Closed reduction with percutaneous K-wire fixation
- Open reduction with K-wire fixation
- Continuous traction(by K-wire)

## Complications
#### Immediate (at the time of fracture)
1. Brachial artery injury
2. Nerve injury (Median > Radial)
#### Early (<3 days)
1. Volkmanns Ischaemia (Due to occlusion of brachial artery)
#### Late (Upto weeks/months after)
1. Malunion - Causes #gunstock_deformity
2. Myositis ossificans
3. [[Ortho notes/Volkmanns Ischaemic contracture\|Volkmanns Ischaemic contracture]]
