A break in the continuity of bone can be classified in many ways
#### By aetiology
- Traumatic
- Pathological
- Stress
#### By displacement
- Displaced
- Non-displaced
#### By relation with environment
- Open (may be internally open or externally open)
- Closed
#### By complexity of treatment
- Simple fracture 
- Complex fracture
#### By causative force
- High velocity
- Low velocity
#### By patterm
- Transverse
- Oblique
- Spiral
- Comminuted
- Segmental
