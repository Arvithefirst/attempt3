A break in the continuity of bone can be classified in many ways
#### By Aetiology
- Traumatic
- Pathological
- Stress
#### By Displacement
- Displaced
- Non-displaced
#### By Relation with environment
- Open (may be internally open or externally open)
- Closed
#### By Complexity of treatment
- Simple fracture 
- Complex fracture
#### By Causative force
- High velocity
- Low velocity
#### By Pattern
- Transverse
- Oblique
- Spiral
- Comminuted
- Segmental
