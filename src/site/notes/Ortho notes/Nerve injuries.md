## Seddons Classification
1. Neuropraxia - Physiological disruption of conduction in a nerve
2. Axonotmesis - Axon damaged. wallerian regeneration, spontaneous regeneration
3. Neurotmesis - Nerve damaged with no chance of spontaneous regeneration

## Deformities
1. Wrist drop - Radial nerve palsy
2. Foot drop - Common peroneal nerve palsy
3. Winging of scapula - Long thoracic nerve palsy
4. Claw hand - Ulnar nerve palsy
5. Ape thumb - Median nerve palsy
6. Pointing index - Median nerve palsy
7. Policemen tip - Erbs palsy
