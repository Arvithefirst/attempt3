CTEV or "club foot" in the commmonest congenital deformity of the foot. It is characterised by
- *C*avus
- *A*dductus
- *V*arus
- *E*quinus

## Etiology
Can broadly classified into 
1. Idiopathic CTEV
	- Mechanical poressure in utero
	- Ischaemic contracture
	- Genetic
2. Secondary CTEV
	- Paralytic CTEV (polio, spina bifida etc)

## Investigations
1. Xray foot

## Treatment
Can be conservative or non-conservative methods. The earlier the correction is started the less radical method is required

### Conservative methods
Can be eithre by manipulationn alone or by manipulation and POP applications. May be done by either
- Kites philosophy (casting from calcaneocuboid joint)
- Ponsetti philosophy (serial casting from talus)

### Non-conservative methods
1. Posteromedial soft tissue release
2. Limited soft tissue release
3. Tendon transfer
4. Dwyers osteotomy (open wedge)
5. Dilwym evans procedure (calcaneocuboid fusion)
6. Wedge tarsectomy
7. Triple Arthrodesis
8. Ilizarovs technique

>[!faq] Methods of maintaining corrections
	1. CTEV splint
	2. Denis-brown splint
	3. CTEV shoes
