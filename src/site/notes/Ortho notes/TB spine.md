Also calle dPotts spine

## Types
Can be described by area of infection
1. Paradiscal (commonest)
2. Central
3. Anterior
4. Posterior

## Pathophysiology
1. Granulomatous inflammation 
2. Destruction of bone
3. Formation of *cold abscess**
4. Fusion of vertebrae as healing occurs

>[!faq] Cold Abscess
>A collection of pus and tubercular debris. Like a pyogenic ascess but not a/w signs of inflammation. 
>Pus travels through surrounding tissue to cause damage

## Clinical features
1. Dull aching back pain. May progress to pain radiating to parts innervated. by affected nerves
2. Stiffness
3. Cold abscess
4. Paraplegia
5. Deformity
6. Constitutional symptoms

## Investigations
1. Clinical examination to determine
	1. Locality of cold abscess
	2. Neuroloogical involvement
2. Xray spine (AP and Lateral minimum)
	1. Reduction in disc space
	2. Destruction of vertebral body
	3. Evidence of cold abscess
	4. Rarefaction of surrounding vertebrae
	5. Signs of bone healing
3. CT/MRI
4. CT guided needle biopsy
5. ESR, Mantoux, ELISA etc

## Treatment
1. ATT
2. Rest for spine followed by cautious mobilisation
#### Treatment of Cold Abscess
1. Aspiration - Zig-zagh tract
2. Evacuation (closed without drain)
