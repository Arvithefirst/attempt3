*Fracture at distal end of radius*. Typically seen in >40 year old women almost always casued by fall on outstretched hand

## Pathoanatomy
Transverse fracture at cortico-cancellous junction. Typically with atleast 1 ort 2 of the following displacements
- Impaction of fragments
- *Dorsal displacement/tilt*
- Lateral displacement/tilt
- Supination

## Clinical features
- Pain, swelling, restriction of movement
- #Dinnerfork_deformity of hand

## Treatment
Essentiually conservative. Few surgical optionms
- Closed manipulation and immobilisation by Colles cast
- In comminuted fractures k-waires, Locking compression plates may be employed

## Complications
1. Stiffness
2. [[Ortho notes/Malunion\|Malunion]]
3. Subluxation of inferior radioulnar joint
4. Carpal tunnel syndrome
5. Sudecks Osteodystophy
6. Rupture of extensor pollicis longus
