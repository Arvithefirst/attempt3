## Stages of fracture healing
1. Hematoma 
2. Granulatio tissue 
3. Callus
4. Remodelling 
5. Modelling 
### Stage of hematoma
Lasts upto 7 days
Ischaemic necrosis at the ends of the bones sensitizes osteoclasts
### Stage. of granulation tissue
Lasts 2-3 weeks
Sensitized daughter cells form fibroblasts, osteoblasts etc. The blood clot gives way to *fibrous mesh* which is eventually removed by the immune system. Last common point with soft tissue
### Stage of callus formation
Lasts 4-12 weeks
Granulation tissue differentaites into osteoblasts which lay down the *intracellular matrix*. This is impregnated with calcium salts to form *woven bone*. Slower in adults and cortical bones
### Stage of remodelling
Lasts 1-4 years
Woven bone is gradually replaced by mature lamellar bone. Process is multicellular unit based
### Stage of modelling
Lasts many years
Bone is gradually strenghtened by local stimuli such as weight bearing. More pronounced in children

## Factors affecting Fracture healing
1. Age
2. Type of bone ( Flat cancellous > tubular, cortical)
3. Pattern of bone (Spiral > Oblilque > Transverse > Comminuted)
4. Pathoanatomy
5. Type of reduction
6. Immobilisation
7. Open fractures
8. Compression at fracture site
