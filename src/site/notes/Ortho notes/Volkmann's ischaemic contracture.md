Ischaemic injury to the muscles and. nerves of the flexor compartment of the armm due to occlusion of the brachial artery caused by supracondylar fracture of the Humerus

## Clinical Features
1. Severe foremarm pain
2. Stretch pain
3. Swelling and numbness over finders
4. Ternderness over forearm

## Treatment
This is a medical emergency
- Removal of external bandages/splints
- Forearm is elevated
- Fasciotomy of flexor compartment of forearm
