*Delayed union* - When a fracture is taking longer than expected to heal 
*Non-union* - When a fracture shows no signs of union within 6 months

## Causes
Non-union may be of two main types - Atrphic or hypertrphic. Either way the causes are determined by
#### Patient factors
- Age
- Malignancy, illnesses, osteomalacia
#### Fracture factors
<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Distraction at fracture site</mark>
- Muscle pull - # Patella, # Olecranon
- Gravity - # Shaft of huumerus
<mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Soft tissue interposition</mark>
- /# shaft of humerus
- /# Shaft of femur
- /# Medial malleolus
<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Bone loss at time of fracture</mark>
- Open # Tibia
- Open # Ulna
<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Infection of open fracture</mark>
<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Damage to blood supply by fracture fragments</mark>
<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Pathological fracture</mark>
#### Treatment factors
- Inadequate reduction
- Inadequate mobilisation
- Distraction during treatment

## Complications
- Persistant pain
- Deformity
- Reduced mobility

## Treatment
- Open reducction
- Excision of fragments
- Do nothing :)
- Ilizarovs method
