*When a fracture does not unite in thje preper position*. Most fractures result in a slight proportion of malunion

## Causes
- Improper treatment
- Unchecked muscle pull
- Excessive communication

## Complication
- Deformity
- Shortening
- Limitation of ROM

## Treatment
- Osteoclasis
- Surgical correction
- Corrective osteotomy
- Excision

// Treatment may not be required. Children have better remodelling. Some types and locations of fractures do not need surgical interventino
