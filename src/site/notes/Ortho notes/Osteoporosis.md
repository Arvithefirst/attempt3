Commonest metabolic bone disease. Characterised by diffuse reduction in b one density

## Etiology
1. *Senility*
2. Post-immobilisation
3. Post-menopause
4. Protein deficiency - dietary, malabsoption, exxcess loss
5. Endocrine - Cushings, hypertension
6. Drug induced - Steroid, Phenobarbitone

## clinical features
*Asymptomatic* until complications(pathologic fractures)
- Commonly affects *dorso-lumbar spine*
- Slight loss in height and kyphosis

## Investigations
1. Xray spine
	1. Loss of height of vertebrae
	2. *Cod fish appearance* of vertebrae
	3. *Ground glass appeaance*
	4. Grade by Singhs index/Metacarpal index
2. Serum ALP, calcium, ohosphates, Proteins, albumin
3. Densitometry, specifically *DEXA scan*
4. Neutron activation analysis

## Treatment
1. Increased protein and calcium intake. Vitamin D supplementation, Calcitonin
2. Androgens/Estrogens
3. Flouride
4. Alandronate (?)
5. Anabolics - Teriparatide
6. Anti-Bone resorption - Strontium
7. Orthopedic exercises
8. *Prophylactic bracing with ASH/Taylor Brace*
