Vitamin D deficiency in Adults. 

## Etiology
1. Lack of sunlight exposure
2. Dietary defiency
3. Pregnancy
4. Malabsorption syndrome
5. Partial gasrectomy

## Clinical features
1. Bone pain
2. Muscular weakness
3. Spontaneous fractures

## Investiagtions
1. Xray wrists and pelvis
	1. Diffuse rarefaction
	2. Pseudofractures (loosers zone)
	3. Triradiate pelvis in females
	4. Protrusio-acetabulio
2. Bone biopsy
3. Serum Calcium, Phosphate, ALP

## Treatment
1. Vitamon D supplementation 400IU/day
2. Calcium supplement
