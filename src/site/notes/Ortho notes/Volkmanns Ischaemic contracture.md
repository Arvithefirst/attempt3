Sequlae of volkmanns Ischaemia. It is the *result of ischaemic necrosis of flexor compartment of forearm due to the occlusion of brachial artery seen in [[Ortho notes/Supracondylar fracture of Humerus\|Supracondylar fracture of Humerus]]*

## Clinical features
Ischaemic muscles are replaced by fibrous tissue leading to contracture and sensory loss
1. Flexion deformity of wrist
2. Dry scaly skin over forearm
3. Atrophic nails
>[!faq] Volkmanns sign
> It is only possible to extend the fingers when the wrist is flexed due to the shortening of muscle-tendons

## Treatment
1. Mild deformity can be treated. by splinting or soft tiussue release surgeries (*Volkmanns splint*)
2. Severe deformity may require bone shortening surgery
