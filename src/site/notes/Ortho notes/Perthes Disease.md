Osteochondritis of the epiphysis of the femoral head. Causes AVN

#### Pathology
- Likely due to repeated synovitis in susceptible age group(5-10 years)
- Stages Synovitis < Trabecular necrosis < Healing

## Clinical features
1. Pain hip, radiating to knee
2. Limping / Stiffness. Limitation of abduction, shortening of limb
3. Xray shows collapse and sclerosis of head. Hip joint space increased *"sagging rope Sign"*

## Treatment
Goal is to prevent misshaping of bone
1. Head containment with plaster/splint
2. Containment osteotomy
