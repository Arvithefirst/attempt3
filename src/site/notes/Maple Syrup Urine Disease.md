# Maple Syrup Urine Disease

## Pathophysiology
1. &darr; Branched chain $\alpha$-ketoacid dehydrogenases
2. Blocked degradation of branched amino acids (Leucine, Isoleucine, Valine)

## Clinical Features
1. Poor feeding, Vomitting
2. Secretions smell like maple syrup
3. Progressive neurologic decline

## Treatment
1. Dietary restriction or Branched chain aminoacids
2. [[Thiamine\|Thiamine]] Supplementation
