# Golgi Apparatus
Intracellular organelle

## Functions
Post-translational modifications of proteins including
1. Modifying N-oligosaccharides on Aspargine
2. Adding O-oligosaccharides on Serine and threonine
3. Adding mannose-6-phosphate to mark proteins for lysosomal degradation
