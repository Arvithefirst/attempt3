# I-cell Disease

Autosomal recessive lysosomal storage disorder. Defect in *N-acetylglucosaminyl-1-phosphotransferase* in the [[Golgi Apparatus\|Golgi Apparatus]]

## Pathophysiology
1. Deficiency results in Golgi unable to phosphorylate mannose residue on glycoproteins (decreased mannose-6-phosphate)
2. Enzymes secreted extracellularly instead of to lysosome
3. Build up of cellular debris in lysosomes *(Inclusion bodies)*

## Clinical Features
Typically fatal in childhood. similar to [[Hurler syndrome\|Hurler syndrome]]
1. Coarse facies, gingival hyperplasia, *Corneal clouding*
2. Restricted joint movement, Claw hand
3. Kyphoscoliosis
4. Increased plasma lysosomal enzymes
