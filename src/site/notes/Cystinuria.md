# Cystinuria

Defect of Renal PCT that prevents reabsorption of Cystine, Ornithine, Lysine, Arginine

## Pathophysiology
1. Cystine is 2 cysteines connected by disulphide bonds
2. Excess Cystine causes precipitation of *hexagonal crystal stones*
3. Cystinuria detected by Sodium Nitroprusside test and HNMR spectroscopy

## Treatment
1. Urinary Alkalisation
2. Chelating with Penicillamine
3. Hydration
4. Dietary restriction
