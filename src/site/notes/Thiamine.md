# Thiamine
## Vitamin $B_1$ (Thiamine)
### Functions
Thiamine Pyrophosphate is a cofactor for
- Branched-chain ketoacid dehydrogenase (deficient in [[Maple Syrup Urine Disease\|Maple Syrup Urine Disease]])
- $\alpha$-Ketoglutarate dehydrogenase (from [[TCA cycle\|TCA cycle]])
- Pyruvate Dehydrogenase
- Transketolase [[HMP Shunt\|HMP shunt]]

### Deficiency
Impaired glucose breakdown affects highly aerobic tissues first
#### Dry Beri-beri
- Polyneuropathy, symmetrical wasting
#### Wet Beri-beri
- High output cardiac failure due to systemic vasodialtion
#### Wernickes Encephalopathy
- Complication. 
- Confusion, ophthalmoplegia, Nystagmus, Ataxia
#### Korsakoff Syndrome
- Amnestic disorder of chronic alcohol overuse
- Confabulation, personality changes, memory loss
#### Wernicke-Korsaloff Syndrome
- Both of the above
