## Pathophysiology
Several types, including but not limited to 
1. Defective [[Interleukins#IL-2\|Interleukins#IL-2]]R gamma chain (XLR)
2. Adenosine Deaminase deficiency (AR)
3. RAG mutation
Leads to 
- Absent thymic shadow
- Absent germinal centres
- Absent [[T-cells\|T-cells]]
## Clinical Features
1. Failure to thrive
2. Chronic Diarrhoea
3. Oral thrush
4. Recurrrent Bacterial, viral, protozoal, and fungal infections
