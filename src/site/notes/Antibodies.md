F*c* region is
1. Constant

*Affinity* is the strength of a single antibody-antigen interaction, *Avidity* is the total strength of multiple antibody-antigen interactions

## Immunoglobulin Isotypes
Naive B-cells express IgM and IgD. Undergo isotype switching in [[Lymph nodes\|Lymph nodes]] to IgA, IgG, IgE

### IgG
- Main isotype in serum. Secondary reaction to antigens
- Fixes [[Complement\|Complement]], opsonises bacteria and neutralises toxins and viruses
- *Crosses placenta*
- Associated with Warm autoimmune hemolytic anemia
### IgA
- Produced in GI tract, prevents attachment to mucous membranes
- Most produced but least in serum. Released in secretions
### IgM
- Primary response to antigens. Fixes [[Complement\|Complement]]
- Highly avid because of pentamer structure
- Responsible for Cold autoimmune haemolytic anemia

- Mediates Type 1 hypersensitivity reactions
