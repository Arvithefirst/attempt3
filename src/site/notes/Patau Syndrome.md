# Patau Syndrome
Trisomy 13 leads to midline defects

## Clinical Features
1. Intellectual disability
2. Microphthalmia, Microcephaly, cleft lip/palate, holoprosencephaly
3. Cutis aplasia
4. Congenital heart disease
5. Rocker bottom feet
6. Polycystic Kidney disease
