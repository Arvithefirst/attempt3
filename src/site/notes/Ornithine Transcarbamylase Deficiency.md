# Ornithine Transcarbamylase Deficiency
Most common [[Urea cycle\|Urea cycle]] disorder

## Pathophysiology
1. Innability to eliminate ammonia leads to [[Hyperammonemia\|Hyperammonemia]]
2. Excess Carbomyl phosphate converted to orotic acid from [[Pyrimidine Synthesis Pathway\|Pyrimidine Synthesis Pathway]]

## Clinical Features
1. [[Hyperammonemia#Clinical features\|Hyperammonemia#Clinical features]]
2. Incrased blood and uine Orotic acid
3. Decreased BUN
4. No megaloblastic ammonia
