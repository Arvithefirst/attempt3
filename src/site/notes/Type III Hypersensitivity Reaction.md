Immune Complex mediated [[Complement\|Complement]] activation

## Pathophysiology
- Antigen-Antibody complexes (usually [[Antibodies#IgG\|IgG]]) activate complement 
- Neutrophil chemotaxis and release of lysosomal enzymes
- Tissue damage (&darr; serum C3, and C4)
#### Examples
- [[SLE\|SLE]]
- Reactive Arthritis
- Polyarteritis nodosa
- [[Post-streptococcal Glomerulonephritis\|Post-streptococcal Glomerulonephritis]]
- [[IgA Vasculitis\|IgA Vasculitis]]


>[!info] Serum Sickness
>Prototypic immune complex disease characterised by 
>- Fever
>- Urticaria
>- Arthralgia
>- Proteinuria, lymphadenopathy

>[!faq] Arthus Reaction
>Local Subacute immune complex reaction due to intradermal injection of antigen characterised by 
>- Edema
>- Fibrinoid necrosis
>- Complement activation
>Examples: Enhanced local reaction to booster vaccinations
