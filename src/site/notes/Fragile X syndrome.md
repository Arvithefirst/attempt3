# Fragile X Syndrome
Trinucleotide expansion of CGG on FMR gene. Most common inherited cause of intellectual disability

## Pathophysiology
Hypermethylation of cytosine residues -> &darr; expression of FMR1 gene
- 50-200 repeats
	- Tremors, ataxia
	- primary ovarian insufficiency
- >200 repeats
	- Post-pubertal macroorchidism
	- Long face with long jaw and large everted ears
	- Mitral valve prolapse
	- Hypermobile joints
	- Autism
// Self-mutilation is common and may be confused with [[Lesch-Nyhan syndrome\|Lesch-Nyhan syndrome]]
