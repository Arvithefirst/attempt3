# Vitamin C
Found in fruits and vegetables, specifically citrus
#### Functions
1. Required for [[Collagen#Synthesis\|Collagen#Synthesis]]
2. Facilitates iron absorption by reducing $Fe^{3+}$ ro $Fe^{2+}$
3. Necessary for Norepinephrine synthesis

## Deficiency
May be precipitated by tea and toast diet
1. Swollen gums
2. Easy bruising, petechiae, Haemarthrosis, Perifollicular and subperiosteal hemorrhage
3. Poor wound healing
4. "Corkscrew" hair
5. Weakened immune response
//White line of Frankel, Wimbergers sign, elevated periosteum, frayed metacarpals on Xray
## Excess
1. Nausea, vomitting, fatigue
2. Calcium oxalate nephrolithiasis
3. Increases risk of Iron toxicity
