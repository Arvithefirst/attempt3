# Vitamin D
## Metabolism
![Screenshot 2026-09-04 at 3.38.17 PM.png\|900](/img/user/Screenshot%202026-09-04%20at%203.38.17%20PM.png)

## Deficiency
Called *Rickets* in children and *Osteomalacia* in adults
#### Etiology
- Dietary insufficiency, poor sun exposure
- CKD, Advanced liver disease
- Purely breastfed infants

#### Symptoms
Lacking vitamin D leads to loss of $Ca^{2+}$ and $PO_4 ^{3-}$ in urine and decreased enteric uptake
1. Bow-legs (genu varum) in children, bone pain/weakenss in adults
2. Hypocalcemic tetany


## Excess
Seen in Granulomatous disease
1. Hypercalcemia, Hypercalciuria
2. Loss of appetite, stupor
