Hepatically synthesised proteins ![Screenshot 2026-10-02 at 11.15.34 PM.png](/img/user/Screenshot%202026-10-02%20at%2011.15.34%20PM.png)
Activated in 3 ways
- Classically by [[Antibodies#IgG\|IgG]] or [[Antibodies#IgM\|IgM ]] 
- Alternatively by bacterial products
- Lecithin pathway by mannose or other residues on microbes

Out of these mainly
- C3b - Opsonisation
- C3a, C4a, C5a - Anaphylaxis
- C5a - Neutrophil chemotaxis
- C5b-C9 complex (MAC) - Cytolysis
