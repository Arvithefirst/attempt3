# Hartnup Disease
Mimics [[Niacin\|Niacin]] deficiency
## Pathophysiology
1. Deficiency of Neutral Amino acid transporters on proximal renal tubules and enterocytes
2. Neutral aminoaciduria and &darr; absorption from gut
3. &darr; Tryptophan available for niacin synthesis

## Treatment
1. High protein diet
2. [[Niacin\|Nicotinic acid]] supplementation
