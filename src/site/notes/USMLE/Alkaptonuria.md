Autosomal Recessive defect in [[USMLE/Catecholamine Synthesis\|Tyrosine Catabolism]]

## Pathogenesis
- Congenital deficiency of Homogentisate Oxidase
- Build up of Homogentisic acid

## Clinical features
1. Bluidh-black connective tissue, Ear cartilage, Sclerae
 2. Urine turns black on standing
3. Debilitating Arthralgia
