Show *Genetic ANticipation* (Repeats increase every generation causing iincrceased severity and earlier onset)

#### Huntingtons disease
Autosomal dominant *CAG* expansion
- *C*audate had less *A*Ch and more *G*ABA
#### Myotonic dystrophy
Autosomal dominant *CTG* expansion
-  *C*ataracts, *T*oupee(hair loss), *G*onadal atrophy
#### [[USMLE/Fragile X Syndrome\|Fragile X Syndrome]]
X-linked *CGG* expansion
- Large *C*hin, *G*iant *G*onads

#### Friedreich Ataxia
Autosomal recessive *GAA* expansion
- Ataxic *GAA*it
