## Students Achievements
The new Students's council has hit the ground running with numerous intra- and inter-collegiate events.

With the upcoming events, intra- and inter-college competitions of this year, preparations are made by the new Students council of this year by holding various auditions!

 Along with some friendly events, auditions are also being taken for the upcoming event AVYANNA’25 SYMBIOSIS tournament at Symbiosis Medical College for Women (SMCW) which will be from March 5th-7th.

  

Here are few of the events-

  

1. A friendly dodgeball match was organised by the new sports secretaries of this year’s Students council 24-25 on 15/02/25 at 4:30pm in our very own Lawn tennis court. Fixtures were between 1st vs 2nd year, and 3rd vs 4th year. In this friendly game, girls of the 4th year and boys of the 2nd year were the respective winners.

A group of team was selected for cricket to represent the college on AVYANNA'25.

2. Debate auditions were organised by the Debate department on 20/02/25  at 5pm for English Formal Debate at SMCW. 
3. Fashion Fusion, organised by the Arts department held a fashion audition on 17/02/25 at 5:30pm. Out of the many participants, 15 were selected to represent the college on the upcoming AVYANNA’25.
