Act on Nicotinic($N_n$ , $N_m$) or Muscarinic receptors ($M_{1-5}$). Antichollinesterases inhibit breakdown of AcH in synaptic cleft

## Direct agonists
1. Bethanechol - Acts on bladder smooth muscle -> Improves retention
2. Methocholine - Used for [[Medicine notes/Asthma\|Asthma]] challenge test
3. Carbachol* - Used to constrict pupil intraoperatively
4. Pilocarpine - Used in Glaucoma and Sjorgens syndrome
*// Carbon copy of ACh thats resistant to AChE

## Anticholinesterases
1. Donepezil, Rivastigmine, Galantamine -> Centrally acting, first line drugs for [[Medicine notes/Alzheimers Disease\|Alzheimers Disease]]
2. Neostigmine -> Positively charged, no CNS penetration -> used for [[Medicine notes/Myasthenia Gravis\|Myasthenia Gravis]]
3. Pyridostigmine -> Long acting for [[Medicine notes/Myasthenia Gravis\|Myasthenia Gravis]]
4. Physostigmine -> Antidote for Anticholinergic toxicity
