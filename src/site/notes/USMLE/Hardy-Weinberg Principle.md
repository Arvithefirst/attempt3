#USMLE
In a population where $P$ and $Q$ represent alleles in the genetic pool

$$P + Q = 1$$
Also because punnet square and math
$$ P^2 + 2PQ + Q^2 = 1$$

Use this to find population of carrier/affected populations when applicanle
