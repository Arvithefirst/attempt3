X-linked dominant CGG [[USMLE/Trinucleotide Expansion Diseases\|trinucleotide expansion]] in FMR1 gene causing hypermethylation of cystine and underexpression of the gene

## Clinical Features
Most common cause of inherited intellectual disability
#### Premutation (50-200 repeats)
- Tremors, Ataxia
- Primary ovarian Insufficiency
#### Full mutation (more than 200 repeats)
- Post-pubertal Macrochidism
- Long face with large jaw and everted ears
- Autism
- Mitral valve prolapse
- Hypermobile joints
- Self-mutilation (not to be confused with [[USMLE/Lesch-Nyhan Syndrome\|Lesch-Nyhan Syndrome]])
