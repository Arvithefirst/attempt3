#### Cyclosporine
- Calcineurin inhibitor
- Indicated in [[Medicine notes/Psoriasis\|Psoriasis]], [[Medicine notes/Rheumatoid Arthritis\|Rheumatoid Arthritis]]
- Nephrotoxic -> Hypertension, Hyperlipidemia, *Gingival hyperplasia, Hirsuitism*

#### Tacrolimus
- Calcineurin Inhibitor
- Indicated after Organ transplants
- Nephrotoxic -> Same as above except last 2

#### Sirolimus
- mTOR inhibitor
- Indicated specifically after *Sir Basils Kidney transplant*
- Pancytopenia, Hyperlipidemia

#### Basiliximab
- Monoclonal antibody blocking IL-2R
- Indicated specifically after *Sir Basils Kidney Transplant*
- Edema, Hypertension, Tremors

#### Azathioprine
- Precursor to 6-mercaptopurine. Inhibits lyphocyte proliferation by blocking nucleotide synthesis
- [[Medicine notes/Rheumatoid Arthritis\|Rheumatoid Arthritis]], [[Medicine notes/Crohns Disease\|Crohns Disease]], [[Medicine notes/Glomerulonephritis\|Glomerulonephritis]], other autoimmunes
- Pancytopenia
*// Is degraded by Xanthine Oxidase. Increased Toxicity concurrent with Allopurinol*

#### Mycophenolate mofetil
- Reversible inhibitor of IMP dehydrogenase, prevents synthesis of purines in B and T cells
- [[Medicine notes/Rheumatoid Arthritis\|Rheumatoid Arthritis]], [[Medicine notes/Acute Rheumatic heart disease\|Acute Rheumatic heart disease]]
- GI upset, Pancytopenia, Hypertension. Associated wtih invasive CMV

#### Glucocorticoids
- Inhibits NF-kd. Suppresses both B and T cells by decreases transcription of cytokines and indicing T cell apoptosis
- EVERYWHERE WITH INFLAMMATION. [[Medicine notes/Asthma\|Asthma]], CLL, Non-hodgkins disease
- Cushings syndrome
*// May develop adrenal insufficiency if suddenly stopped*
