De novo mutaition of MECP2 gene on X-chromosome.

## Clinical features
- Embryologically lethal in males
- Normal development until 6-18 months followed by *Rett*urn in motor/verbal/cognition
- Seizures, Ataxia
- Scoliosis
- Stereotypic hand-wringing
