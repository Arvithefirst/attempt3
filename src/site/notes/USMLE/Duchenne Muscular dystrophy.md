X-linked recessive frameshift or nonsense mutation of Dystrophin Gene(DMD)
- Truncated or deleted dystrophin
- Progressive myofiber damage

## Clinical Features
- Pelvic girdle weakness progressing superiorly
- Pseudohypertrophy of calf muscle due to replacement of muscle by fibrofatty material
- Waddling gait
- Dilated Cardiopathy -> Death
- Increased CK and aldolase
*//Gowers sign - Use of upper limbs to help stand. Characteristic of muscular dystrophies*


>[!info] Beckers Muscular dystrophy
>Caused by *non frameshift mutations* in DMD. Similar to Duchennes but less severe
>Onset in adolescence
