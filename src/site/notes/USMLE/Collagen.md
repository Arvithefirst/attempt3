#### Type 1 - Skeletal
Most common type, Bones, tendons, fascia, cornea, Late wound repair
Defective in [[USMLE/Osteogenesis Imperfecta\|Osteogenesis Imperfecta]]

#### Type 2 - Cartilage
All cartialge including hyaline, Vitreous bod

#### Type 3 - Arteries
Arteries, Uterus, Fetus, Early wound repair
Defective in [[USMLE/Ehler-Danlos Syndrome\|Ehler-Danlos Syndrome]]

#### Type 4 - Basement membranes
Basmeent membrane of glomerulus, basal lamina, Lens
Defective in goodpastures syndrome, Alports etc
