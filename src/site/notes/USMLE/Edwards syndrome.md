Trisomy 18 (2nd most common trisomy)

## Clinical features
*PRINCE* Edwards
- *P*rominent occiput, micrognathia(small jaw)
- *R*ocker-bottom feet
- *I*ntellectual disability
- *N*ondsijunction
- *C*lenched fists with verlappping fingers
- Low set *E*ars
- Congential heart disease, Omphalocele, meningomyelocele

// All quadruple markers are decreased in edwards
