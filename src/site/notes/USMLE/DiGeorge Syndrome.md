Chromosome 22q11.2 microdeletion causes defective neural crest migration in to the third and fourth pharyngeal pouches

## Clinical features

- Cardiac defects ([[Medicine notes/Tetralogy of Fallot\|Tetralogy of Fallot]])
- Abnormal facies
- Thymic hypoplasia
- Cleft Palate
- Hypocalcemia/Hypoparathyroidism
