Genetic Defect in [[USMLE/Collagen Synthesis\|Collagen Synthesis]]. Usually Autosomal dominant defect of COL1A1 and COL1A2

#### Clinical Features
- Brittle bones (Multiple fractures with minimal trauma)
- Blue Sclerae
- Opalescent Teeth
- Conductive Hearing Loss

#### Treatment
Biphosphonates
