Autosomal recessive defect in Cystine Metabolism
![Screenshot 2025-06-26 at 9.37.04 AM.png](/img/user/Pictures/Screenshot%202025-06-26%20at%209.37.04%20AM.png)
#### Pathogenesis
Multiple possible Etiologies
- Deficiency of Cystathioine Synthase
- Decreased affinity of cystathione synthase
- Methionine Synthase deficiency
- Methyl-THFR deficiency -> Decreased Methylcobalamine

## Clinical Features
- Homocystiene in Urine
- Osteoporosis
- Marfanoid Habitus
- Ocular changes (Subluxation of lens)
- Cardiovascular defects (Increased chance of thrombus and stroke)
- Kyphosis
- Intellectual disabbility
- Hypopigmented skin

## Management
Dietary management to avoid build up of Homocystine
