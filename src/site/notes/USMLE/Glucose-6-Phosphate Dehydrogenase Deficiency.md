X-linked recessive disease endemic to Subsaharan Africa and Southeast Asia

#### Functions
- Produces NADPH used in
	- Reduction of Reactive Oxygen by Gluthathione reductase
	- Cholesterol synthesis
- Produces Ribose-5-phosphate for [[USMLE/HMP Shunt\|Pentose Phosphate Pathway]] or [[USMLE/De novo Pyrimidine Synthesis\|De novo Pyrimidine Synthesis]]/[[USMLE/Purine Synthesis\|Purine Synthesis]]

## Clinical features
- Hemolytic anemia especially when exposed to oxidising agents (Fava beans, Nitrofurantoin, Primaquine)
- Frequent Infections
- Heinz Bodies in RBC due to oxidative stress
- Bite cells due to phagocytic removal of Heinz bodies
