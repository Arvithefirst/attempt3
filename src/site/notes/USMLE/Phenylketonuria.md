Autosomal recessive error in [[USMLE/Catecholamine Synthesis\|Catecholamine Synthesis]]

#### Causes
Decreased Phenylalanine Hydroxylase causes build up of Phenylalanine -> Increased Phenyl ketones in urine

## Clinical Features
- Intellectual disability, [[Pediatric Notes/Microcephaly\|Microcephaly]]
- Hypopigmented skin, Eczema
- *Musty Odor*

## Treatment
1. Avoid Dietary Phenylalanine
2. Supplement Biotin and Tyrosine (Soy, Fish, chicken , milk)
