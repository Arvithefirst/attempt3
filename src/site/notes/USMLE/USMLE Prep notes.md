#USMLE
#### Layers of kidney
1. Zona Glomerulosa - Arched clusters of mineralocorticoid (*Aldosterone*) producing cells
2. Zona Fasciculata - Foamy bundles of glucocorticoid (*Cortisol*) producing cells
3. Zona Reticularis - Anastomosing cords of *Androgen* producing cells
4. Adrenal Medulla - Deeply basophillic *Catecholamine* producing Chromaffin cells

Xeroderma Pigmentosum - Defective nucleotide excision
