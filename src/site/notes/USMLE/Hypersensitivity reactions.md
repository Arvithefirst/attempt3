### Type I (immediate)
- IgE mediated
- Basophils + Mast cells
- Anaphylaxis/allergy

### Type II (Cytotoxic)
- IgG, IgM
- NK cells + eosinophils + Neutrophils + Macrophages
- AUtoimmune hemolytic anemia, Goodpasture syndrome

### Type III (Immune Complex)
- Antigen complexes
- Neutrophils
- Serum sickness, PSGN, Lupus nephritis

### Type IV (Delayed)
- No humoural part
- T cells and Macrophages
- Contact dermatitis, Tuberculin
