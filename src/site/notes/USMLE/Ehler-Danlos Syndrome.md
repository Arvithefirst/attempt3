Defect in [[USMLE/Collagen Synthesis\|Collagen Synthesis]]

#### Aetiology
1. Classical type - Defect in [[USMLE/Collagen\|Collagen V]] (COL5A1, COL5A2)
2. Vascular Type - Defect in [[USMLE/Collagen\|Collagen III]] (COL3A1)
3. Can also be caused by procollagen peptidase deficiency

#### Clinical Features
1. Hyper-extensible skin
2. Hypermobile joints
3. Tendency to bleed
4. Joint dislocation, berry and aortic aneurysm
5. Fragile vessels and organs
