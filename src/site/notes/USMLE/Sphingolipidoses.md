## Fabry Disease
*X linked recessive* deficiency of *a-Galactosidase A* leading to accumulation of *Globotrialsylceramide* causing
- Angiokeratomas
- Peripheral neuropathy
- Glomerulopathy

## Tay Sachs Disease
*Autosomal Recessive* deficiency of *β-Hexosaminidase A* leading to accumulation of *ganglioside* leading to 
- Macular cherry-red spot
- Progressive neurodegeneration

## Gaucher disease
*Autosomal recesive* deficiency of *β Glucocerebrosidase* leading to accumulation of *Glucocerebroside* leading to 
- Hepatosplenomegaly
- Pancytopenia
- Bone pain, Osteopenia

## Neimann-Pick Disease
*Autosomal recessive* deficiency of *Sphingomyelinase* leading to accumulation of *Shpingomyelin* leading to 
- Macular cherry red spot
- Progressive neurodegeneration
- Hepatosplenomegaly

## Krabbe Disease
*Autosomal recessive* deficiency of *Galactocerebrosidase* leading to accumulation of *Galactocerebroside* leading to 
- Progressive neurodegeneration
- Peripheral neuropathy
- Optic Atrophy

## Metachromatic Leukodystrophy
*Autosomal recessive* deficiency of *Arylsulfatase A* Leading to accumulation of *Cerebroside sulfate* Leading to 
- Progressive neurodegeneraion
- Peripheral neuropathy
