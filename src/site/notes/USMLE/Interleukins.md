>[!info] Mnemonic
>*Hot T-bone* st*EAK*
IL-1 - Fever (*Hot*)
IL-2 - *T* cells
IL-3 - *Bone* marrow
IL-4 - Ig*E*, induces Th2 differentiation, Ups B-cell growth
IL-5 - Ig*A*, Promotes B-cell and Eosinophil growth
IL-6 - A*k*ute phase reactants

## Macrophages
IL-1, IL-6, IL-8, IL-12, TNFa

TNFa is responsible for granulomas in TB and cachexia in malignancy
IL-8 recruits neutrophils
IL-12 induces differentiation of Th1 and Activates NK cells

## T cells
All - IL-2, IL-3
Th1 - Interferon-y
Th2 - IL-4, IL-5, IL-10, IL-13

Interferon-y - inhibits differentiation in to Th2, IgG isotype switching
IL-10 - Attenuates immune response - Inhibits macrophages, dendritic cells
IL-13 - Promotes IgE
