Xlinked Recessive defect in [[USMLE/Purine Synthesis\|Purine Synthesis]]. 
Absent HGPRT -> Inability to recycle Guanine and Hypoxanthine -> Excess Uric acid

#### Clinical features (HGPRT)
- Hyperuricemia
- [[Medicine notes/Acute Gout\|Gout]]
- Pissed off (irritiable)
- Red/Orange crystals in urine
- Tense Muscles (Dystonia)

#### Treatent
- Allopurinol
- Febuxostat
