Autosomal Recessive disease of Amino Acid catabolism

#### Pathogenesis
- Decreased Branched chain a-ketoacid Dehydrogenase
- Blocked degradation of branched Amino acids (Leucine, Isoleucine, Valine)

## Clinical Features
1. Vomitting, Poor feeding
2. Urine smells like maple syrup/Burnt Sugar
3. Progressive neurologicacl decline

## Management
1. Amino Acid restriction
2. Thiamine supplementation
