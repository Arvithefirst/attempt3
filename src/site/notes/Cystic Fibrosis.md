# Cystic Fibrosis

*Deletion of $\Delta$F508 on CFTR gene on chromosome 7.* Most common lethal genetic disease in people with European descent

## Pathophysiology
CFTR encodes ATP-gated $Cl^-$ channels 
1. Deletion of $\Delta$F508 region results in misfolded protein that doesnt make it to the membrane
2. &darr; $Cl^-$ secretion -> Compensatory &uarr; $Na^+$ reabsorption via epithelial channels
3. &uarr; $H_2O$ reabsorption -> Abnormally thick mucus in RS and GI tracts

## Clinical Featuers
1. *Recurrent LRTI* ([S.aureus] in childhood. [P.Aeruginosa] in adults)
2. Allergic BronchoPulmonary Aspergillosis, Chronic Bronchitis, Bronchiectasis leading to Reticulonodular CXR, Opacification of sinuses and clubbing
3. *Pancreatic Insufficiency*, steatorrhoea, fat soluble *vitamin deficiency*
4. CF-related diabetes
5. Biliary cirrhosis, liver disease
6. *Infertility* in males(absence of vas deferens) and subfertility in females(thick mucus)

## Diagnosis
1. &uarr; $Cl^-$ on Pilocarpine induced sweat test
2. Contraction alkalosis and hypokalemia (ECF $Na^+$ loss leads to $K^+$ wasting)

## Treatment
1. Chest physiotherapy
2. Aerosolised Dornase Alfa *(DNase)*
3. Inhaled hypertonic saline
4. Azithromycin, Ibuprofen
5. Pancreatic enzyme replacement therapy 
>[!info] CFTR Modulators
>1. Potentiates $Cl^-$ channels -> Ivacaftor
>2. Corrects protein folding -> Lumacaftor, Tezacaftor
