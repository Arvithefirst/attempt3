## Pathophysiology
- 22q11 microdeletion
- Failure to develop 3rd and 4th pharygeal pouches
- Absent thymus and parathyroids

## Clinical Features
CATCH-22
- *C*ardiac defects (conotruncal abnormalities like [[Tetralogy of Fallot\|Tetralogy of Fallot]], truncus arteriosus)
- *A*bnormal facies
- *T*hymic hypoplasia -> Deficient [[T-cells\|T-cells]] -> Recurrent Viral/fungal infections
- *C*left palate
- *H*ypocalcemia ->Tetany
