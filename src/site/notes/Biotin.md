# Biotin

## Vitamin $B_7$ (Biotin)
Cofactor for carboxylation enzymes
- Pyruvate carboxylase ([[Gluconeogenesis\|Gluconeogenesis]])
- Acetyl-CoA carboxylase  ([[Fatty acid synthesis\|Fatty acid synthesis]])
- Propionyl-CoA carboxylase (Fatty acid Oxidation)

### Deficiency
Very rare, usually caused by long term antibiotic use or consumption of raw egg whites
1. Dermatitis
2. Alopecia
3. Enteritis
