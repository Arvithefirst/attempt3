# Refsum Disease
Disease of the [[Peroxisome\|Peroxisome]]

## Pathophysiology
1. Autosomal recessive disorder of $\alpha$-oxidation
2. Build up of phytanic acid

## Clinical Features
1. Retinitis pigmentosa
2. Anosmia, hearing loss
3. Ataxia, peripheral neuropathy
4. Ichthyosis
5. Cardiac conduction disordes

## Treatment
1. Diet
2. Plasmaphoresis
