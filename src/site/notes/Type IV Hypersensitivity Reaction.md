Delayed reaction mediated by [[T-cells\|T-cells]]

## Pathophysiology
2 mechanisms of tissue damage
1. Direct CD8+ cytotoxic effect
2. CD4+ recognise antigen and release proinflammatory [[Interleukins\|Cytokines]]
#### Examples
- Contact Dermatitis
- Drug reactions in patients with SJS, TEN, or DRESS
- [[Graft vs Host Disease\|Graft vs Host Disease]]
- Candida skin test and Tuberculin test
