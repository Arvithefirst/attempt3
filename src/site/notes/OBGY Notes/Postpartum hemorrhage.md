Defined by WHO as *"Blood loss in excess of 500ml following the birth of the baby"* however clinically defined as any blood loss following birth that adversly affect the general condition of the patient as evidenced by raised pulse and lowered BP.

## Types
1. Primary(within 24 hours)
	- Third stage hemorrhage
	- [[OBGY Notes/True postpartum hemorrhage\|True postpartum hemorrhage]]
2. Secondary(after 24 hours within pueperium)
