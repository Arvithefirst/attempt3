From full dilation of cervix uptil delivery of fetus. Divided into 2 stages
- Propulsive phase (From dilation uptil engagement)
- Expulsive phase (from effacement uptil delivery)

## Clinical features
1. Pain
2. Bearing down efforts initiated by ferguson reflex
3. Rupture of membranes
4. Descent of fetus(measured using *Fifth formula*)
5. Vulval opening turns oval with contrations. Crowning is seen
6. Exhaustion, tachypnea
7. Slowing of FHR during contractions

>[!faq] Fifth formula for abdominal measurement of descent of head
>*Measures the number of 1/5ths of the head that can be felt from the abdomen.* Preferred of ischial spine "Station of head" method as it avoids repeated vaginal examination

## Management
Aims to (a) assist natural expulsion and (b) prevent perineal injury
