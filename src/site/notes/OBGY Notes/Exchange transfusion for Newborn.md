Aims to 
1. Correct anemia
2. Remove circulating antibodies and Billirubin

## Indications
1. Cord bilirubin >4mg/dl, Hb% <11g/dl
2. Rising bilirubin >1mg/dl/hr despite phototherapy
3. Total bilirubin >20mg/dl

## Procedure
1. Replaces 80-90% newborns blood with Fresh compatible warmed blood
2. Umbilical vein ins catheterised
3. Bicarb is given to prevent [[Medicine notes/Metabolic Acidosis\|Metabolic Acidosis]]

#### Post transfusion care
1. Radiant warmer
2. Serum bilirubin monitoring
3. Umbilical vein inspection
4. BSL monitoring

## Complications
#### Immediate
1. Cardiac failure
2. Air embolus
3. Clotting disorders
4. Hyperkalemia, Acidosis, Hypocalcemia, Hypoglycemia

#### Delayes
1. Necrotising enterocolitis
2. Portal vein thrombosis -> [[Medicine notes/Portal Hypertension\|Portal Hypertension]]
3. Complications of prematurity, kernicterus
