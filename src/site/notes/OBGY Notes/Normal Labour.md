Labour is defined as the series of events that occur in the genital to expel a viable product of conception out throughthe vagina

*Normal Labour(eutocia)* must meet the following criteria(5)
- Spontaneeous and at term
- Vertex presentation
- No undue prolongation
- Natually terminates without side effects
- Without complications to mother/fetus

## Causes of onset of labour
1. *Stetch receptors* on uterine lining
2. Fetal activation of HPA axis induces increase in estrogen and decrease of progesterone
	- Oncreased Estrogen promotes *oxytocin synthesis and receptors* and promes myometrim for contractions
	- Alteration of Estrogen:progesterone ratio causes *prostaglandin synthesis* which initiate an dmaintain labour

## Prelabour
May begin 2-3 weeks befor true labour in primigravids and a few days before in multigravids. Inconsistent features may consist of
1. Lightening (diminished fundal height due to sinking of presenting part)
2. Ripening of cervix
3. [[OBGY Notes/False Labour pains\|False Labour pains]]

## Stages of Labour
1. [[OBGY Notes/First Stage of Labour\|First Stage of Labour]] - From onset of true labour pain upto full dilation of cervix. From 6-12 hours
2. [[OBGY Notes/Second Stage of Labour\|Second Stage of Labour]] - From full dilation of cervix upto expulsion of fetus. From 30 minutes upto 2 hours
3. [[Third Stage of Labour \|Third Stage of Labour ]]- From expulsin of fetus upto expulsion of placenta and membranes. Usually 15 minutes but can be brought down to 5
4. [[Fourth stage of Labour \|Fourth stage of Labour ]]- Observation for 1hour after birth
