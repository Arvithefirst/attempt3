Hormonal involvement is in 4 stages. Lactogenesis. Mammogenesis, Galactopoiesis. Galatcokinessis

#### Lactogenesis
1. Occurs in fully developed mammary gland
2. Induced by Thyroxine, Prolactin, Growth hormone, Insulin, Glucocorticoids
3. Also withdrawal of Estrogen and Progesterone
#### Mammogenesis
1. Development of Lobuloalveolar system by Estrogen, Prgesterone, Prolactin, Growth hormone
2. Development of ducts by Estrogen, Glucocorticoids
#### Galactopoiesis
1. Induced by Suckling and Prolactin
#### Galactokinesis
1. Induced by suprooptic nucleus "seeing" suckling
2. Ejection of milk induced by oxytocin
