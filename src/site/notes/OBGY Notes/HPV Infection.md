Human pallioma virus Infection, 99.7% of CIN and invasive cancer are found to be HPV positive

## Histological features
1. Enlarged cells with perinuclear halos
2. Nucleus is large, irregular and hyperchromic

High oncogenic risk in subtypes 16, 18, 31, 33, 35, 45, 56

## Triage screening for HPV
1. Pap Smear
2. HPV DNA testing
3. Colposcopy
