Premalignant condition of cervical squamous epithelium

## Classification
### WHO classification
Divided on limit of histological changes
CIN I - Basal 1/3rd 
CIN II - Basal 1/2 to 2/3rd
CIN III - Whole thickness except superficial 2-3 layers
CIN IV(CIS) - Whole thickness (Carcinoma in-situ) [[OBGY Notes/Carcinoma Cervix\|Carcinoma Cervix]]

### Bethesda Classification
1. Atypical squamous cells
2. Low grade squamous intraepithelial lesions (LSIL, CIN I)
3. High grade squamous Intraepithelial lesions (HSIL, CIN II, III, IV)

## Pathology
>[!summary] Relevant anatomy
> - Endocervical canal in lined with columnar epithelium, while ectocervix is lined with squamous type. These types meet at the "*Squamocolumnar junction"*
> - Under estrogen some squamous cells undergo metaplasia annd become columnar (and physiologically active )
> - Thus the squamocolumnar junction moves and the region of cells that changed is the *"Transformation zone"*

Carcinogenesis occurs at the Transformation zone
1. By squamous metaplasia 
2. By squamous epidermisation due to ingrowth of the squamous epithelium of the ectocervixunder the columnar epithelium

## Etiology
1. [[OBGY Notes/Human Papilloma Virus\|Human Papilloma Virus]], other STDs
2. Early sexual intercourse, Early pregnancy
3. Too many and too frequent childbirth, Multiple sexual partners
4. Low socioeconomic, Smokers
5. Immunosuppressed
6. [[OBGY Notes/Oral Contraceptive Pills\|Oral Contraceptive Pills]]

## Investigations
Routine things ig and ALSO
#### [[OBGY Notes/Papanicolaou and Traut Smear\|Pap smear]] (Papanicolau and Traut)
Exfoliative cytology is the gold standard screening test. Cells are fixed and viewed to see
- Dyskaryotic cells(Atypical with hyperchromatic nuclei abundant cytoplasm)
- Carcinoma in-situ cells
#### [[OBGY Notes/Human Papilloma Virus\|HPV]]-DNA Test
- 2-5% with +ve test will develop CIN
- Quantitative > Qualitative
#### Visual Inspection with Acetic acid
#### Colposcopy
1. White epithelium or Aceto-white epithelium
2. Mosiac epithelium
3. Atypical blood vessels
4. Irregular surface contour
#### Biopsy
- Of abnormal area (if colposcopy available, otherwise Areas unstained by schillers iodine solution)
- Endocervical Curettage

## Treatment
Can be preventitive or definitive

### Preventitive
- [[OBGY Notes/Human Papilloma Virus\|HPV]] vaccination
### Definitive treatment
CIN I and CIN II go for observation and follow up every 4-6 months
CIN III and CIS can have
1. Local ablation
	1. Cryosurgery
	2. Cold Coagulation
	3. Electrodiathermy
	4. Laser vaporisation (preferred)
2. Excision
	1. Cone excision
	2. Large loop excision of Transformation Zone (LLETZ) *//also used for CIN II*
3. [[Abdominal Hysterectomy\|Hysterectomy]]
