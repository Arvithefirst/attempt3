Cyclical bleeding at normal intervals either excessive in amount(>80ml) or duration(7 days)

## Etiology
1. [[OBGY Notes/Fibroid\|Fibroid]]. [[OBGY Notes/Intrauterine Contraception Devices\|IUCD]], Adenomyosis, Pelvic TB
2. Congestive Cardiac Failure, Severe HTN
3. Hypo/hyperthyroidism
4. Idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpua, Leukemia
