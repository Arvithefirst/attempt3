Can refer to any methods that stop sperm from reaching an ovum. They can be 
#### Mechanical
- Condom 
- Femidom/Diaphram/Cervical cap
#### Chemical (Vaginal spermicides)
- Creams - Delfen
- Jellys - Koromex
- Foam tablets - Aerosol foam, Sponge
