Bleeding with interval of >35 days

## Etiology
1. Adolescence or pre-menopaussal
2. Weight related, Stress, exercise
3. Endocrine disorders like [[OBGY Notes/Polycystic Ovarian Disease\|PCOS]], hypothyroidism
4. Androgen secreeting tumour
5. Drugs like methyldopa
