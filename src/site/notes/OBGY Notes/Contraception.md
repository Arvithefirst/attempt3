The activity of inhibiting fertility (not to be confused with fertility control.)
## The Ideal contraceptive
- Widely acceptable
- Cheap
- Easy to use
- Highly effective
- Doesnt require motivation, maintenance

## Methods
These can be permanent or temporary. 
*Temporary*
1. [[OBGY Notes/Natural Contraception\|Natural Contraception]]
2. [[OBGY Notes/Barrier Contraceptives\|Barrier Contraceptives]]
3. [[OBGY Notes/Intrauterine Contraception Devices\|Intrauterine Contraception Devices]]
4. [[OBGY Notes/Steroidal Contraception\|Steroidal Contraception]]
*Permanent*
1. Tubal occlusion
2. Vasectomy
