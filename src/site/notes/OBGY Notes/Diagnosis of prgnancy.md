### Presumptive signs
1. [[OBGY Notes/Amenorrhoea\|Amenorrhoea]]
2. Polyuria
3. Morning sickness
4. Fatigue
5. Brest changes
### Probable signs
1. Abdominal distension
2. Braxton kicks
3. Changes in uterine shape
4. Jacquemiers/Chadwick sign (dusky hue of the ant. vaginal wall)
5. Osieanders sign - Pulsations felt on lateral fornices
6. Cervical softening
7. Immunological tests for hCG
### Positive signs
1. Palpatiojn of fetal parts (>20weeks)
2. Fetal heart sounds
3. USG embryo 
4. Radiological determination of fetal skeleton
