One where the fertilised ovum gets implanted and develops outside the normal endometrial cavity

## Types
1. Tubal(97%)
	1. Ampulla (55%)
	2. Isthmus (25%)
	3. Infundibulum (18%)
	4. Interstitial (2%)
2. Ovarian
3. Abdominal
4. Uterine

## Clinical features
Can be Acute, Unruptured, or subacute(old)
### Acute Ectopic(30%)
1. Patient is between 20-30
2. Abdominal pain preceeded by amenorrhoea. Vaginal bleeding
3. Patient is in hypovolemic shock
4. Vaginal examination may worsen bleed

### Unruptured ectopic
1. Features of pregnancy, with uneasyness on one side
2. Diagnosable by TVS, ß-hCG and Laprascopy
### Old ectopic
1. She had pain and now doesnt
2. Features of shock, sepsis

## Investigations
For acute *do blood typing and stuff* because medical emergency. Treat like shock

### Acute ruptured ectopic
1. Ringers lactate
2. Blood transfusion, artificial blood
3. Laparatomy
4. Salpingectomy
5. Subtotal hysterectomy

### Unruptured tubal pregnancy
