It is a form of anovular [[OBGY Notes/Dysfunctional Uterine Bleeding\|DUB]]

## Clinical features
1. History of 6-8 weeks of [[OBGY Notes/Amenorrhoea\|amenorrhoea]] followed by painless heavy vaginal bleeding. Typically seen in premenopausal women
2. *"Swiss cheese" hyperplasia* of endometrium
3. Cystic changes in one or both Ovaries
