Defined as when labour starts <37 completed weeks of amenorrhoea

## Etiology
*Idiopathic 50% of the time.* Risk factors are
#### History of
1. Previous Induced labour, Spontaneous abortion or preterm labour
2. [[OBGY Notes/Assisted Reproductive Techniques\|Assisted Reproductive Techniques]]
3. Aysmptomatic bacteruria or recurrent UTI
4. Smoking, Poor nutrition, Stress

#### Complications of present pregnancy
1. [[OBGY Notes/Pre-eclampsia\|Pre-eclampsia]], [[OBGY Notes/Antepartum Hemorrhage\|Antepartum Hemorrhage]], [[OBGY Notes/Premature Rupture of Membranes\|PROM]], [[OBGY Notes/Polyhydramnios\|Polyhydramnios]]
2. Cervical incompetence, Uterine malformations
3. Infections - Genical tract infections, Pyelonephritis, TORCH, Appendicitis etc
4. [[OBGY Notes/Hypertension in pregnancy\|Hypertension in pregnancy]], [[OBGY Notes/Gestational diabetes\|GDM]], [[OBGY Notes/Anemia in pregnancy\|Anemia in pregnancy]], Heart disease
5. [[OBGY Notes/Twin Pregnancy\|Multiple Pregnancy]], IUD
6. Placental Infarct, thrombosis

>[!faq] Diagnostic Criteria
>1. Regular uterine contractions with or without pain (>1 in 10 min)
>2. Dilatation >2cm and Effacement >80%
>3. Lenght of cervix <2.5cm
>4. Pelvic pressure, backache, Vaginal discharge/bleeding

## Investigations
1. Complete blood count, urinalysis + cultures
2. Cervicovaginal swab for fibrronectic
3. Sonography (fetal health, cervical lenght, placenta localisation)
4. Serum electrolytes, BSL

## Treatment
#### Measure to arrest labour
1. Bed rest
2. Adequate hydration, prophylactic antibiotics
3. Prophylactic cervical circlage
4. Tocolytics
5. Glucocorticoids 
>[!info] Glucocorticoid therapy
>used to hasten fetal lung development adn prevent RDS. *Either 2 X Betamethasone 12mg 24 hours apart or 4 X Dexamethasone 6mg 12 hours apart*
>Considerations
>- May cause flare up of infection
>- Insulin needs readjustment

#### Definitive treatment (delivery)
In first stage of labor
1. J shaped or cvertical Cesarean section
2. Epidular anesthesia
3. NICU admission

In [[OBGY Notes/Second Stage of Labour\|second stage of labour]]
1. Gentle delivery
2. [[OBGY Notes/Episiotomy\|Episiotomy]]
3. Low forceps
4. Immediate cord clamping
5. NICU
