[[OBGY Notes/Human Papilloma Virus\|HPV]] Cytology test used to screen for [[OBGY Notes/Cervical Intraepithelial Neoplasia\|CIN]] and [[OBGY Notes/Carcinoma Cervix\|Ca Cervix]]

#### Procedure
1. In lithotomy position, Introduce Cuscos speculum without lubricant
2. Cervical scraping using Ayers spatula or Cytobrush
3. Fixed with 95% Ethyl Alcohol. Stained by Papnicolaous method

## Dyskaryotic moprhologies
1. Nuclear abnormalities (Multinucleation, Hyperchromasia)
2. Scanty Cytoplasm
3. Decreased N:C ratio
4. Koilocytosis
5. Perinycleaer halo

#### Accuracy
60% sensitivity. 25% false positives

## Papanicolaou grading
Not always done. Usually reported as Normal/Abnormal
*Group 1* - Normal
*Group 2* - Presence of borderline atypical cells
*Group 3* - Cells suspicious of malignancy
*Group 4* - Few malignant cells
*Group 5* - Large number of malignant cells
