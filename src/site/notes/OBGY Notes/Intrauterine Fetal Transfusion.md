Aims to
- Correct anemia and improve oxygenation 
- improve hepatic function

#### Intraperitoneal
1. Blood is transfused into thefetal peritoneal cavity under USG guidance
2. Can be started at 18 weeks, and repeated every 1-3 weeks

#### Intravascular
1. USG guided. Into umbilical vein
