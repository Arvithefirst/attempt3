Anti-estrogenic drug with weak estrogenic effects
#### Dose
- 50mg daily upto 250mg
- Started for 6 days between day 2-5 of follicular phase
- Ovulation is expected 5-7 days after last dose
#### MoA
- Blocks Estrogen receptors in Hypothalamus causing increased GnRH release and increased estrogen
- Anti-estrogenic effect seen on endometrium and cervical mucous
#### Side effectsd
- Hot flashes, Nausea, vomitting, 
- Visual symptoms
- Ovarian hyperstimulation(rare)
