CO2 or air is pushed transcervically under pressure. Tubal patency is tested

*Pre-requisites* - 2 days post-mentrual bleeding
*Limitation*s - PID
*Observations*
- Fall in pressure when >120mmHg
- Hissing sound in iliac fossa
- Shoulder pain
