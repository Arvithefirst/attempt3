## Mechanism of Action
1. Inhibition of ovulation
2. Producing a state of static endometrial hypoplasia
3. Alteration of cervical mucus
4. Alters tubal motility

## Classification
#### Oral
1. [[OBGY Notes/Oral Contraceptive Pills\|Oral Contraceptive Pills]]
	- Monophasic
	- Biphasic
	- Triphasic
	- Emergency Contraception
2. Single preparations
	- Progesterone only
	- Estrogen only
#### Parenteral
1. Injectables
	- Depot Medroxy-Progesterone Acetate
	- NET-EN
2. Implant
	- Norplant
	- LNG rod
#### Device
1. [[OBGY Notes/Intrauterine Contraception Devices\|Intrauterine Contraception Devices]]
2. Vaginal ring
3. Nestorone (testosterone patch)
