Also called Manchester operation. Designed to *correct uterine descent along with cystocele or enterocele where preservation of uterus is desirable.* Involves the following steps
1. Preliminary Dilatation and Curettage
2. Amputation of cervix
3. Plication of Macenrodts ligaments
4. [[OBGY Notes/Anterior wall colporrhaphy\|Anterior wall colporrhaphy]]
5. [[OBGY Notes/Colpoperineorrhaphy\|Colpoperineorrhaphy]]
