Lasts 15 minutes from after delivery of fetus upto delivery of placenta and membranes. Consists of a phase of *placental seperation*, *descent into lower segment* and *expulsion of memebranes*

## Clinical features
1. Pain
2. Post-searation uterus becomes globular, firm and ballotable
3. Slight pre-separation bleeding followed by a gush of blood immediately after

## Management
May be either epectant(traditional) or Active management

### Expectant management
1. Supervision of mother 
2. Dorsal position
3. With hand paced over fundus to monitor signs of separation
4. Expulsion is achieved with bearing down efforts and gravity
In the event that spontaneous expulsion does not occur then *Assisted expulsion* can be attempted

>[!faq] Methods of assisted expulsion
	1. Controlled cord traction
	2. Fundal pressure
	3. Oxytocin(5-10 units) or 0.2ng methergine

### Active management
Administration of parenteral oxytocics to incite powerful contractions within a minute of delivery. Drugs used are 
- IV Oxytocin 5-10units
- IM methergine 0.2mg
#### Advantages
1. Cuts blood loss to 1/5th. 
2. Shortens duration of third stage (15min -> 5min)
#### Disadvantages
1. Slightly increased chance of placental retention
#### Indications(high risk of PPH)
1. Anemia
2. Hydramanios
3. Teins
4. Grand multipara
5. History of PPH
#### Contraindications
1. Heart disease predisposing to heart failure
2. Severe pre-eclampsia
