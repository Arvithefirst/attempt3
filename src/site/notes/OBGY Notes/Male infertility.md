## Causes
1. Pre-testicular
	1. Endocrine - GnRH deficiency, Obesity, Thyroid dysfunction, Hyperprolactinemia
	2. Psychosexual - ED, Impotence
	3. Drugs - Anti-Hypertensve, Antipsychotics
	4. Genetic - XXY(kline felters)
2. Testicular
	1. Kartageners
	2. Cryptochidism
	3. Drugs, smoking, radiation
	4. Sertiolu cell syndrome
	5. Promary faiilure
3. Post-testicular
	1. Obstruction
		1. Congenital (Cystic Fibrosis, Youngs)
		2. Infection (TB, Gonorrhoea)
		3. Surgical (Vasectomy)
	2. Other
		1. Ejaculatory failure
		2. Retrograde ejaculation
		3. Hypospadias

## Investigations
or Semen analysis SAQ. This is a fowchart but who has the time

1. Microscopic study shows pus cells/leucocytspermia on prostatic massage - Infection
2. Fructose content shows absence 
	1. Congenital absence of seminal vesicle
	2. Partial duct obstruction
3. Hormonal tests
	1. TSH high  - Hypothyroidism
	2. Testosterone low - Klinefelters, Hypogonadotrophic-hypogonadism
	3. Prolactin high
	4. FSH high - Testicular failure
	5. FSH low - Hypogonadism
	6. FSH normal
		1. Spermatogenesis absent - Destruction of epithelium
		2. Spermatogenesis normal - Vasovasectomy

Also Transrectal Ultrasonography to visualise obstruction
