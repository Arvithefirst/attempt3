Tocolytic and Anti-convulsant used in [[OBGY Notes/Pre-eclampsia\|Pre-eclampsia]] and [[OBGY Notes/Eclampsia\|Eclampsia]]

#### Mechanism of action 
- Competitive inhibition of calcium ion uptake
- Decreases acetylcholine release
- Vasodilation

### *Pritchard intramuscular regimen*
- 4gm slow IV + 5mg IM in each buttock = 14gm (loading dose)
- 5mg in alternate buttock every 4. hours (maintenance dose)

#### Side effects
- Flushing
- Muscle paresis
- rarely pulmonary edema

*Antidote* is 10% Calcium Gluconate 10ml IV
