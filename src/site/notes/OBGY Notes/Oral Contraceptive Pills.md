Progestins - Levanogestrel, Norethisterone, Desogestrel, Drospirenone
Estrogens - Ethinyl-estradiol, Menstranol

## MoA
1. Inhibition of ovulation 
2. Produces static endometrial hypoplasia
3. Alteration fo cervical mucous
4. Interferes with tubal motility

## WHO criteria for prescription
#### Cat 1 - No restriction
1. Upto 40 years
2. Post abortion
3. Anemia, [[Medicine notes/Malaria\|Malaria]], TB, PID
4. [[Medicine notes/HIV\|HIV]]/AIDS
5. [[OBGY Notes/Dysmenorrhoea\|Dysmenorrhoea]], Benign breat disease, [[OBGY Notes/Endometriosis\|Endometriosis]], , CA ovary
6. Epilepsy, Thyroid disease, [[Surgery Notes/Varicose veins\|Varicose veins]]
#### CAT 2 - Advantages outweight risks
1. Older than 40, Smoker, h/o jaundice,mild hypertension
2. Gall bladder disease, [[Medicine notes/Diabetes Mellitus\|Diabetes Mellitus]], Sickle cell disease
3. [[OBGY Notes/Carcinoma Cervix\|Ca Cervix]]
#### Category 3 - Risks outweigh advantages
1. Unexplained vaginal bleed
2. Hyperlipidemia
3. Heavy smoker
4. Past [[Surgery Notes/Carcinoma Breast\|breast cancer]]
#### Category 4 - Absolute Contraindications
1. Circulatory diseases (DVT, [[Medicine notes/Stroke\|Stroke]], IHD, Migraine with aura)
2. Liver diseases (ALD, Carcinoma)
3. Pregnancy
4. Major surgery
5. [[Surgery Notes/Carcinoma Breast\|Breast cancer]]

## Uses
1. [[OBGY Notes/Contraception\|Contraception]] - convenient effective (0.1 /HWY), reversible
2. Regulation of menstruation
3. [[OBGY Notes/Dysmenorrhoea\|Dysmenorrhoea]]
4. Reduction of PMS
5. Reduction of Mittelshmertz syndrome(?)
6. Protection against Iron deficiency anemia
7. Protection against PID
8. Protection against [[OBGY Notes/Ectopic pregnancy\|Ectopic pregnancy]]
9. Protection against [[OBGY Notes/Endometriosis\|Endometriosis]]
10. Protection against [[OBGY Notes/Fibroid\|Fibroid]]
11. Mangement of [[Polycystic Ovarian Disease\|PCOS]]
12. Protection against benign Breast disease
13. Protection against Osteopenia, postmenopausal [[Ortho notes/Osteoporosis\|Osteoporosis]]
14. Protection against autoimmune thyroid diseases
15. Protection against [[Medicine notes/Rheumatoid Arthritis\|Rheumatoid Arthritis]]
16. Prevention of Ca Endometrium
17. Prevention of Ca Ovary
18. Prvention of Colo-rectal Cancer

## Side effects
Minor
1. Nausea, vomitting, Headache
2. Mastalgia
3. Weight gain
4. Chloasma, Acne
5. Breakthrough Bleeding, Hypomenorrhea, Amenorrhoea
6. Diminished Libido
7. Leucorrhoa
Major
1. Depression
2. Hypertension
3. Vascular disorders (DVT, [[Medicine notes/Acute Coronary Syndrome\|MI]])
4. Cholestatic Jaundice
5. Neoplasia
