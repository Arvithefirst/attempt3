Defined as *Hypertension of >140/90mmHg and proteinurea(>3g/day) developed after the 20th week of pregnancy* 

## Aetiopathogenesis
Risk factors include Primigravid state, placental abnormalities, family history and Obseity. It is caused by
- Increased pressor substances
- Increased sensitivity of vascular system to pressor substances
- May complicate into [[OBGY Notes/HELLP Syndrome\|HELLP Syndrome]]

## Classification
#### Mild 
- 140/90mmHg > Blood pressure  > 160/110mmHg
#### Severe
- Blood pressure > 160/110mmhg
- Proteinurea >5mg/day
- Oliguria <400ml/day
- [[OBGY Notes/HELLP Syndrome\|HELLP Syndrome]]
- Cerebral visual symptoms
- IUGR

## Clinical features
*Symptoms*
- Pedal edema (only symptpm in mild disease)
- Headache
- Disturbed sleep
- Oliguria
- Visual symtptoms
*Signs*
- Abnormal weight gain
- Rise in BP
- Edema / Anasarca
- Placental insufficiency

## Investigations
1. Urine examination
2. Ophthalmoscopic examination
3. Serum urea, creatinine, LFTs

## Management
No clinical tests are useful in detecting pre-eclampsia before on set. However prophylaxis is possible by
- Regular clinical assessment
- Antithrombotic therapy
- Calcium supplement
- Antioxidants

Once the disease is diagnosed the goals of treatment ar to 
1. Stabilise hypertension
2. Prevent complications
3. Prevent eclampsia
4. Delivery of healthy baby
5. Restoration of health

This is done using
1. Rest
2. Diet
3. Diuretics (furosemide)
4. B-blockers - Labetalol
5. CCbs - Nifedipine
6. Vasodilators - Hydraslazine
7. Anti-adrenergic drugs - Methyldopa
