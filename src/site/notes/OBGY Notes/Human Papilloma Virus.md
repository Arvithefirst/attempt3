Human pallioma virus Infection, 99.7% of [[OBGY Notes/Cervical Intraepithelial Neoplasia\|CIN]] and Invasive [[OBGY Notes/Carcinoma Cervix\|Ca Cervix]] are found to be HPV positive

#### Pathology
- Epitheliotropic, Oncogenic virus
- HPV E6 and E7 proteins degrade p53 tumour suppressor gene
- Immortalisation and viral multiplication

## Clinical Features
Usually subclinical but may present with anogenital warts
1. Single or multiple, Exophytic, Macular/Papular
2. Giant condyloma formation is rare

## Investigations
[[OBGY Notes/Papanicolaou and Traut Smear\|Pap smear]]
Triage screening 
1. Liquid cytology
2. HPV DNA testing
3. Colposcopy

## Prevention
#### Lifestyle Modifications
- Local hygeine
- Delay sexual intercourse until maturity
- Barrier protection
- Cessation of smoking

#### Vaccinations
0.5ml IM at 0, 2, 6 months. 

Given to all girls 12-18. Protects for 7.5 years. Prevents 90% of [[OBGY Notes/Carcinoma Cervix\|Ca Cervix]]

Contains Virus-like Particles(VLP) against HPV types
1. Cervarix(bivalent for types 16,18)
2. Gardasil(quadravalent for types16, 18 ,6, 22)
