Invasive test of fetal respiration during artificially induced contractions. Predicts birth outcome 

## Procedure
Oxytocin in infused 1mU/min slowly ramping up every 20 min to induce contractions. FHS is measured. 

Positive - Persistant late deceleration
Negative - No late deceleration (good prognosis)
Suspicious - Inconsistant non-persistant decelerations
Not satisfactory - Poor recording
Hyperstimulation - Long decelerations

## Indications
1. IUGR
2. Postmaturity
3. [[OBGY Notes/Hypertension in pregnancy\|Hypertension in pregnancy]]
4. [[OBGY Notes/Gestational diabetes\|Gestational diabetes]]

## Contraindications
1. Compromised fetus
2. Previous LSCS
3. [[OBGY Notes/Twin Pregnancy\|Multiple pregnancy]]
