Severe vomitting that limits the day-to-day activities of the mother

## Etiology
1. Usuallly limited to first trimester
2. Genetic component, Ofter recurs in next pregnancy
3. More common. in [[OBGY Notes/Twin Pregnancy\|multiple pregnancy]], Hydatiform Mole

## Clinical features
1. Vomitting :0
2. Signs of dehydration and ketoacidosis
3. Epigastric pain, signs of bleed
### Complications
1. Ketoacidosis
2. Wernicke encephalopathy
3. Korsokoff psychosis
4. Convulsions
5. Jaundice
6. Stress ulcer
7. Mallory weiss tear

## Investigations
1. Urinalysis
2. Electrolytes, ABG, 
3. ECG
4. Ophthalmic examinations

## Management
1. Fluids - 1.5L 5% derxtrose + 1.5L RL + Urine/vomitus volume of 5% dextrose
2. Antiemetics - Promethazine(25mg), Metaclopromide
3. Hydrocortisone
4. Nutrityinoal support
