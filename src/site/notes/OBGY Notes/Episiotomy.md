A surgically planned incision on the perineum and posterior vaginal wall during the [[OBGY Notes/Second Stage of Labour\|second stage of labour]]. *Ideally done just prior to crowning*

#### Objectives
1. To enlarge the vaginal introitus for safe delivery of fetus
2. To minimise overstrectching and rupture of perineal muscles

## Indications
1. *Rigid perineum* - elderly primigravid
2. *Risk of perineal tear* - Big baby, Face to pubis, [[OBGY Notes/Breech Presentation\|Breech Presentation]], Shoulder dystocia
3. *Operative delivery* - Forceps, Ventouse
4. *Previous perineal surgery* - pelvic floor repair

## Types
1. Mediolateral
2. Median
3. Lateral(obsolete)
4. J-shaped(Rarely done, scar tends to pucker)

>[!bug] Mediolateral vs Medial incision
>Medial is usually better - Easier, less blodd loss, no muscles inscised, better healing and less chance of dyspareunia. However has more chance of rectal involvement

## Steps
1. Lithotomy and painting
2. Local anesthesia
3. Incision of structures
	1. posterior vaginal wall
	2. Superficail and deep transverse perineal muscles, Bulbospongiosus, Levator ani
	3. Fascia
	4. Transverse perineal branches of pudendal vessels and nerves
	5. Subcutaneous tissue and skin
4. *Delivery of fetus and placenta*
5. Repair - with perfect hemostasis, No dead space and no tension in sutures
	1. Vaginal mucosa
	2. Perineal muscles
	3. Skin and subcutaneous
6. Packing
