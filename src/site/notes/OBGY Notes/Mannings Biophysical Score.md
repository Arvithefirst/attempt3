Grading system for measuring  Intrauterine fetus health over 30 minutes using
1. Fetal breath movements (> 1 lasting 30s = 2 points)
2. Fetal Tone (>1 episode flexion+extension = 2 points)
3. Gross fetal movements (>3 = 2 points)
4. [[OBGY Notes/Non-stress test\|Non-stress test]] (reactive = 2 points)
5. Amniotic fluid index (>1 2x2cm pocket = 2 points)

Score
<2 = Strong evidence of Asphyxia
<4 = Chronic Asphyxia -> deliver if persistant
<6 = Chronic asphyxia -> Repeat test 4-6 hourly
8-10 = No evidence of asphyxia
