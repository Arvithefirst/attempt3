- *Anatomically* defined as liquor amnii > 2000ml
- *Clinically* defined as when liquor accumulation causes discomfort or makes it so imaging is required to identify lie and presentation of fetus
- *Sonographically* defined as AFI 24cm with largest pocket >8cm

## Etiology
Pathogenesis is speculative; either excess production or deficient absorption.
1. Idiopathic (60%)
2. Fetal anomoalies
	1. Anencephaly, Spina Bifida
	2. Esophageal/duodenal atresia, Faical clefts
	3. [[OBGY Notes/Hemolytic disease of Newborn\|Hydrops fetalis]]
3. Placenta (Choriangioma)
4. [[OBGY Notes/Twin Pregnancy\|Multiple pregnancy]]
5. Maternal
	1. [[OBGY Notes/Gestational diabetes\|Gestational diabetes]]
	2. Heart disease in pregnancy, Renal problems

## Clinical features
Can be of Chronic or acute(very rare) presentations. Find Acute Hydramnios on a different page
1. Dyspnea at rest
2. Palpitations
3. Evidence of [[OBGY Notes/Pre-eclampsia\|Pre-eclampsia]] (Pedal edema, proteinuria, Varicosities)
4. Inspection shows distended shiny abdomen with striae
5. Palpation shows disproportionate distension, Fluid thrill
#### Complications
1. [[OBGY Notes/Pre-eclampsia\|Pre-eclampsia]]
2. Malpresentation
3. [[OBGY Notes/Premature Rupture of Membranes\|PROM]], [[OBGY Notes/Preterm Labour\|Premature labour]]
4. Subinvolution of Uterus
5. Cord Prolapse, Retained placenta, [[OBGY Notes/Postpartum hemorrhage\|Postpartum hemorrhage]]
6. Perinatal mortality

## Investigations
1. Sonography (see definition)
2. Blood typing, Rh grouping
3. ⍺ fetoprotein

## Treatment
In cases of fetal abnormailty the fetus is delivered regardless of gestational age

#### Supportive therapy
- Bed rest
- Control of [[OBGY Notes/Pre-eclampsia\|Pre-eclampsia]], [[OBGY Notes/Gestational diabetes\|Gestational diabetes]]
- Indomethacin

#### Definitive treatment
Done only if condition is unresponsive to supportive treatment and maternal distress is present.
- Amnioreduction repeated until >37 weeks *(slow reduction of upto 500ml/hour)*
- At 37 weeks begin [[OBGY Notes/Oxytocin\|Oxytocin]] drip 
- Controlled Artificial Rupture of Membranes
