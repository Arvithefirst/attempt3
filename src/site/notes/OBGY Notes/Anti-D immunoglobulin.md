IgG Immunoglobulin given to prevent isoimmunisation in [[OBGY Notes/Hemolytic disease of Newborn\|Rh Incompatibility]] pregnancy

#### (possible) Mechanism of action
Antibody mediated Immune Suppression
1. Blocks Rh-antigen
2. Coated RBC removed from circulation beofre sensitisaiton
3. Central inhibition of IgG antibody production

## Dosage
>[!faq] Kleihauer-Betke test
>Used to estimate volum e of fetal blood in maternal circulation.
>- Acid elution test - fetal RBC more resistant to acid
>- 80 cells in 50 low power fields represent 4ml hemorrhage
>- Estimatino can be done mor accurately by IF/Flow cytometry

1. 300µg IM given after delivery
2. 50µg given to unsensitized women witinn 72 hours after Abortion/[[OBGY Notes/Ectopic pregnancy\|Ectopic pregnancy]] etc
