## Aetiology
The common causes of AUB are classified by FIGO under the PALM-COEIN classification system
- *P*olyp
- [[OBGY Notes/Adenomyosis\|Adenomyosis]]
- [[OBGY Notes/Fibroid\|Leiomyoma]]
- *M*alignancy
- *C*oagulopathy
- *O*vulatory dysfunction
- *E*ndometrial
- *I*atrogenic
- *N*ot yet identifies

## Investigations
1. History of amount/time of bleeding and to rule out other etiologies
2. Bimanual examination
3. CBC, Iron study, Platelets, Ptt, BT/CT. Thyroid profile
4. USG and color doppler(finds endometrial hyperplasia)
5. Saline Infusion Sonography to diagnose polyps, fibroids etc
6. MRI and Histopathology
