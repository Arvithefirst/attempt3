## Aetiology
The common causes of AUB are classified by FIDO under the PALM-COEIN classification system
- *P*olyp
- *A*denomyosis
- *L*eiomyoma
- *M*alignancy
- *C*oagulopathy
- *O*vulatory dysfunction
- *E*ndometrial
- *I*atrogenic
- *N*ot yet identifies
