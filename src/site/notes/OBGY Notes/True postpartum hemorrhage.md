Loss of >500ml of blood after birth after expulsion of the placenta

## Causes
4T's - Tone, Trauma, Tissue, Thrombus
#### Atonic uterus(80%)
It is the imperfect contraction and retraction of the uterus. Most common cause of PPH. Often seen in the follwoing conditions
- Grand multipara
- Over-distension of the uterus (Multiple pregnancy, Hydramnios, Large baby)
- Malnutrition and [[OBGY Notes/Anemia in pregnancy\|Anemia in pregnancy]]
- Antepartum Hemorrhage
- Prolonged labour
- Anesthesia
- Premature cessation of oxytocin in induced labour
- Malformation of uterus
- [[Uterine Fibroid\|Uterine Fibroid]]
- Mismanaged [[OBGY Notes/Third Stage of Labour\|Third Stage of Labour]]
- Adherent placenta
- Precipitate labour related trauma
#### Trauma (20%)
Usually following instrumental delivery. May follow episiotomy
#### Retained Tissue
Bits of plaecnta, Blood clots may impede uterine retraction
#### Coagulation disorders
Acquired on congenital coagulopathies. May also be seen in Abruptio Placentae, Jaundice, Thrombocytopenic purpura, HELLP syndrome, or IUD

## Prevention
Post PPH cases have no identifiable risk factors, however with proper investiagtions some risk can be mitigated

*Pre-natal steps*
1. Correction of nutritional status
2. Identification of high risk patients
3. Blood grouping
4. Placental localisation
*Natal steps*
1. [[OBGY Notes/Third Stage of Labour#Active management\|Active management of 3rd stage of labour]]
2. Induction of labour
3. Cesarean section
4. Uterovaginal exploration
5. Post-delivery observation

## Management
Call for help, secure large bore IV, send blood for grouping/acquire blood products for transfusion. 
1. Perform abdominal palpation to determine whether Atonic or traumatic/tissue cause
	- If uterus is hard and contracted then the cause is traumatic. Explore uterus and suture for hemostasis
	- Else continue
2. Uterine massage. IV oxytocin 5-10 units + 0.2mg methergine IV. Catheterise. Examine placenta
3. Blood transfusion. Explore for clots/tissue
4. 15methyl PGF or misoprostol
5. Uterine Tamponade (bimanual compression/balloon tamponade/Packing)
6. Surgical ligation of involved arteries
7. Hysterectomy
