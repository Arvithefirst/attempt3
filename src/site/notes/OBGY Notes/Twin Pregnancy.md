1. Dizygotic(80%)
2. Monozygotic(20%)
	1. Diamniotic-Dichorionic(DD) - Seen when division occurs <72 hours
	2. Diamniotic - Monochorionic (DM) - Division between 4-8th day
	3. Monoamniotic - Monochorionic(MM) - Division >8th day
		1. Conjoined - Divsion after 2 weeks

1. Race

4. Multiparity

1. Exaggerated ailmetns of normal pregnancy
2. Excess weight gain, preeclampsia
3. Barrel uterus, palpation of too many fetal parts, Distinct FHRs
4. USG - labda sign of chorionicity
5. MShCG

## Complications
### Maternal
1. During pregnancy
	1. Nausea vomitting, anemia
	2. Pre-eclampsia, Hydramnios
	3. Antepartum hemorrhage
	4. Malpresentation
	5. Preterm labour
2. During labour
	1. Premature Rupture of Membranes
	2. Prolonged labour
	3. [[OBGY Notes/Postpartum hemorrhage\|Postpartum hemorrhage]]
3. Pueperium
	1. Subinvolution
	2. Infection
### Fetal
1. Miscarraige
2. Prematurity
3. Discordant twins
4. Twin-to-twin Trasfusion syndrome
5. Twin Reverse Arterial Perfusion
>[!faq] Twin-twin Transfusion Syndrome
>- Exclusively in monozygotic twins
>- One twin bleeds into. another due to placental anastomosis
>- One twin becomes biggger with hydroamnios, polycythemia, hypertension
>- Other twin becomes smaller with oligohydramnios, anemia, hypotension
>- Manage with repeated amniocentesis, Septostomy, Laser photocoagulation, MURDER(feticide)

## Management
1. Diet, Rest, Supplementation
2. Increased ANC visitss
3. Vaginal delivery is allowed if both are vertex
4. Methergin given only after 2nd fetus is delivered
