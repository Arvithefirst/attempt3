## Types
1. Dizygotic(80%)
2. Monozygotic(20%)
	1. *Diamniotic-Dichorionic(DD)* - Seen when division occurs <72 hours
	2. *Diamniotic - Monochorionic (DM)* - Division between 4-8th day
	3. *Monoamniotic - Monochorionic(MM)* - Division >8th day
	4. Conjoined - Division after 2 weeks
		1. Thoraco phagus
		2. Pyophagus
		3. Craniophagus
		4. Ischiophagus
		5. Paraphagus
		6. Brachiophagus

#### Also some fun terms
*Superfecundation* - fertilisation of 2 ova on different occasions
*Superfetation* - Fertilisation of ova from consecutive menstrual cycls
*Fetus papyraceous* - Early death causes compression and flattening and mummifications
*Fetus acardiacus* - One fetus becomes parasitic
Hydatiform mole
Vanishing twin

## Etiology
1. Race
2. Genetic
3. Age
4. Multiparity
5. [[OBGY Notes/Ovulation Induction\|Ovulation Induction]]

## Clinical features
1. Exaggerated ailments of normal pregnancy
2. Excess weight gain, [[OBGY Notes/Pre-eclampsia\|Pre-eclampsia]]
3. Barrel uterus, palpation of too many fetal parts, Distinct FHRs
4. USG - labda sign of chorionicity
5. MShCG

## Complications
### Maternal
1. During pregnancy
	1. Nausea, vomitting, Anemia
	2. [[OBGY Notes/Pre-eclampsia\|Pre-eclampsia]], [[OBGY Notes/Polyhydramnios\|Hydramnios]]
	3. [[OBGY Notes/Antepartum Hemorrhage\|Antepartum hemorrhage]]
	4. Malpresentation
	5. [[OBGY Notes/Preterm Labour\|Preterm Labour]]
2. During labour
	1. [[OBGY Notes/Premature Rupture of Membranes\|Premature Rupture of Membranes]]
	2. Prolonged labour
	3. [[OBGY Notes/Postpartum hemorrhage\|Postpartum hemorrhage]]
3. Pueperium
	1. Subinvolution
	2. [[OBGY Notes/Puerperal sepsis\|Puerperal sepsis]]
### Fetal
1. Miscarraige
2. Prematurity
3. Discordant twins
4. Twin-to-twin Trasfusion syndrome
5. Twin Reverse Arterial Perfusion
>[!faq] Twin-twin Transfusion Syndrome
>- Exclusively in monozygotic twins
>- One twin bleeds into. another due to placental anastomosis
>- One twin becomes biggger with hydroamnios, polycythemia, hypertension
>- Other twin becomes smaller with oligohydramnios, anemia, hypotension
>- Manage with repeated amniocentesis, Septostomy, Laser photocoagulation, MURDER(feticide)

## Management
1. Diet, Rest, Supplementation
2. Increased ANC visitss
3. Vaginal delivery is allowed if both are vertex
4. Methergin given only after 2nd fetus is delivered
