## Investigations 
1. CBC - hb% lowered in physiological hemodilution. MCV clues type of pathology
2. Iron binding studies, B12/folate levels - Nutritional anemia
3. FNAC of bone marrow erythropoetic disorders
4. History of chronic disease
5. Testing for Thalassemias, hemoglobinopathies (genetic? PBS??)

One most revealing Investigation
Perhaps Peripheral blood smear? 
can show nutritional Nutritional deficiencies. Can show signs of deranged erythropoeisis. Can show hereditary conditions affecting RBC shape. Can show Parasite in Malaria

Principles of Investigation
To classify as physiological/pathological
To identify etiology of pathological. Degree of illness to decide modality of treatment

Classification of Anemia in Pregnancy
Mild(>10%) > Moderate(?) > Severe(>7%)

Indications of Blood transfusion
1. Severe anemia that needs to be urgently corrected
2. Non-responsive to other treatments
3. No cardiac abnormalities
