## Definitions
#### Hypertension
$BP>140/90mmHg$ measured 2 times within 6 hours
#### Proteinurea
Urinary excretion of >3gm protein/24 hours
#### Gestational hypertension
Hypertension recorded for the first time after 20weeks of pregnancy
#### [[OBGY Notes/Pre-eclampsia\|Pre-eclampsia]]
Gestational hypertension with proteinuria
#### [[OBGY Notes/Eclampsia\|Eclampsia]]
Preclampsia with convulsions
#### Superimposed pre-eclampsia/Eclampsia
New proteinuria in patients with hypertension since before 20th week of pregnancy

>[!bug] Blood volume changes
>Severe hypertension may cause hemoconcentration leading to false increase in hb% readings
