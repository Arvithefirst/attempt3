Presence of functioning endometrium in sites other than the uterine mucosa. If the site is the myometrium it is calle Adenomyosis

## Etiology
More common now because
 - Late marraige, Late first child
 - Improved detection
#### Sites
- Abdominal - Ovary, Pelvic peritoneum, Uterosacral ligaments. Appendix, Sigmoid colon
- Extra-abdominal - typically over scarred sites
- Remote - Lungs, Arms, legs 

## Pathology
3 Main theories of how endometrial tissue travels to ectopic sites
1. Sampsons Theory of *retrograde menstruation*
2. Meyer and Ivanoffs theory of *Coelomic implantation*
3. *Direct implantation*
4. Halbans theory of lymphatic spread
5. Vascular theory
Genetic, immunological and environmental factors are also at play 

Once tissue is implanted
- Tissue undergoes changes cyclical under action of ovarian hormones
- Shed blood becomes Encysted or Bursts
- Encysted blood give *"Chocolate cyst"*
- Leaked blood is an irritant causes fibrosis and adhesions
>[!faq] Pelvic endometriosis
>Typically presents as small black dots descibed as "Powder burns" may also be
>- Red Flame shaped areas
>- Red polypoid areas
>- yellow brown pathces
>- White peritoneal areas
>- Circular peritnoeal patches
>- Subovarian adhesions

## Clinical features
25% are asymptomatic
1. [[OBGY Notes/Dysmenorrhoea\|Dysmenorrhoea]]
2. [[OBGY Notes/Infertility\|Infertility]]
3. Dyspareunia
4. Pelvic / Abdominal pain that aggravates during menses
5. Nodules in pouch of Douglas, Nodular ligaments, Adnexal masses

#### Complications
1. Endocrinopathy
2. Rupture/infection of cyst
3. Obstructive features
4. Malignant change

## Investigations
1. USG
2. MRI/CT
3. CA 125
4. Laprascopy (gold standard)
American Fertility Society(AFS) scoring system is used to advise treatmewnt

## Treatment
#### Preventative
- Avoid forceful examination after menstruation
- Avoid Tubal patenct tests after curettage/ around menses
- Women with family history should finish families early

### Medical Management
Hormonal treatment to induce Atrophy of ectopic tissue
1. [[OBGY Notes/Oral Contraceptive Pills\|Oral Contraceptive Pills]] (pseudopregnancy)
2. Danazol (pseudomenopause)
3. GnRH agonist (medical oopherectomy)

### Surgical Management
1. Laprascopic excision, laser ablation or electrodiatherapy
2. Laprascopic Uterosacral Nerve Ablation(LUNA)
3. Bilateral salpingo-oopherectomy
