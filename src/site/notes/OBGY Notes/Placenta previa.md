A form of [[OBGY Notes/Antepartum Hemorrhage\|Antepartum Hemorrhage]]. It is the *implantation of placenta partially or completely over lower uterine segment*

## Etiology
Pathophysiolgy isnt exactly knowmn. Proably dropping down or defective decidua
1. Multiparity
2. Increased maternal age
3. h/o past Cesarean
4. Smoking
#### Types
1. Low lying - Encroaches lower segment >2cm from os
2. Marginal - <2cm from os
3. Incomplete - Partially covers os
4. Complete - Covers os

>[!faq] Dangerous Placenta Previa
>- Name given to type 2 placenta previa on posterior wall
>- Most likely to cause cord compression and anoxia/death of fetus in vaginal delivery

## Clinical features
1. Sudden, painless, recurrent bleeding. May be preceeded by warning hemorrhage
2. Proportional anemia
3. Uterus soft, relaxed. Persistant malpresentation, Floating head
4. FHS slows as head is pushed into pelvis *(Stallworthys sign)*

## Investigations
1. USG (Transvaginal, Transabdominal)
2. Colour doppler
3. MRI

## Complications
Maternal
1. Malpresentation
2. [[OBGY Notes/Preterm Labour\|Premature labour]]
3. [[OBGY Notes/Premature Rupture of Membranes\|Premature Rupture of Membranes]]
4. Cord Prolapse
5. Prolonged labour
6. [[OBGY Notes/Postpartum hemorrhage\|Postpartum hemorrhage]]
7. Retained placenta
8. Sepsis
Fetal
1. LBW
2. Asphyxia
3. Congenital malformation

## Treatment
#### Prevention
1. ANC care
2. Counselling and indentification of Warning Hemorrhage
3. All cases of [[OBGY Notes/Antepartum Hemorrhage\|Antepartum Hemorrhage]] should be admitted

### Immediate attention
1. Shock status assessment, IV bore access 
2. Blood typing
3. Vulval inspection

### Expectant management
Done by *McAfee and Johnsons regimen*
1. Bed rest
2. Investigations - CBC, Blood typing, Urine
3. Periodic inspection
4. Supplementary hematinics
5. Gentle PS examination
6. [[OBGY Notes/Tocolytics\|Tocolytics]]
7. Cervical circlage
8. Rh immunoglobulin
9. Steroid therapy

### Definitive treatment
Delivery is done if bleeding occurs >37 weeks or mother goes into spontaneous labour or fetus is dead
1. Low rupture of membranes can be attempted if Grade 1
2. Cesarean section
