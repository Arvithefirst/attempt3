### Fetal Skull

| Attitude             | Engaging diameter     | Measurement(cm) |
| -------------------- | --------------------- | --------------- |
| Complete flexion     | Suboccipito-bregmatic | 9.5             |
| Incomplete flexion   | Suboccipito-frontal   | 10              |
| Marked deflexion     | Occipito-frontal      | 11.5            |
| Partial extension    | Mento-vertical        | 14              |
| Incomplete extension | Submento-vertical     | 11.5            |
| Complete extension   | Submento-bregmatic    | 9.5             |

Also transverse (bi-parietal diameter) is 9.5

### Female pelvis
(hold at 55 degrees from horizontal plane)

|                     | Inlet | Cavity | Outlet |
| ------------------- | ----- | ------ | ------ |
| AP diameter         | 11    | 12     | 13     |
| Oblique diameter    | 12    | 12     |        |
| Transverse diameter | 13    | 12     | 11     |

#### Also see Conjugates(AP diameter from midpoint of promontory till)
*Anatomical Conjugate* (till Upper border of Symphysis) = 11cm
*Obstetric Conjugate* (till Middle of symphysis) = 10cm
*Diagonal Conjugate* (Till Lower Border of symphysis) = 13cm

OC is the narrowest diameter of inlet but cannot be measured directly. Calculated as $DC - 2cm$ which is in turn, measured PV
