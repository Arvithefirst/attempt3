Spontaneous rupture of membranes anytime beyond 28th week but before onset of labour. 
- Before 37 weeks - Pre-term PROM
- After 37 weeks - Term PROM

## Causes
Increased membrane friability, decreased tensile strength seen in
1. Prior [[OBGY Notes/Preterm Labour\|Preterm labour]], Low BMI
2. [[OBGY Notes/Polyhydramnios\|Polyhydramnios]]
3. Cervical incompetence
4. [[OBGY Notes/Twin Pregnancy\|Multiple Pregnancy]]
5. Infections

## Clinical features
1. Hydrorrhoea Gravidarum
2. Incontinence

#### Confirmation of diagnosis
1. Per Speculum examination
2. Fluid pH, Cytology (ferning, Nile blue stain)
3. USG

## Investigations
1. CBC, Urinalysis and culture
2. Vaginal swab
3. Phosphatidyl glycerol estimation
4. [[OBGY Notes/Mannings Biophysical Score\|Mannings Biophysical Score]]
5. [[OBGY Notes/Non-stress test\|Non-stress test]]

## Management
In *term PROM* wait 48 hours for spontaneous labor else induction of labour or Cesarean section

In *preterm PROM*, balaced use of Prophylactic antibiotics(Amoxicillin, Ampicillin, Erythromycin) and Glucocorticoids
