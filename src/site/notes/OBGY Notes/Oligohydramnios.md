Liqour amnii <200ml. Or sonographically with maximum pocket <2cm/ AFI <5cm

## Etiology
Fetal
1. Chromosomal./structural abnormalities
2. Renal agensis
3. IUGR
4. Drugs
Mathernal
1. Hypertension
2. Uteroplacental deficiency
3. Dehydration

## Clinical features
1. Smaller uterus, "full of fetus"
2. Commonly in breech with IUGR

## Complications
1. Abortion
2. Deformity
3. Cord compression
4. Prolonger labour

## Treatment
1. Treat cause
2. Fluid therapy
3. Amnioinfusion
