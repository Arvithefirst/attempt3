Enveloped, Linear, dsDNA viruses

Most commonly latent in *trigeminal nerve*. Most common cause of sporadic encephalitis. Transmission by respiratory secretions
### Clinical Features
1. Gingivostomatitis, oesophagitis
2. Keratoconjuntivitis
3. Herpes labialis, Herpetic whitlow
4. Temporal lobe encephalitis
5. Erythema Multiforme
