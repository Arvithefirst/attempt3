# Lymph Nodes
Secondary lymphoid organ

![Screenshot 2026-09-15 at 5.06.02 PM.png\|1000](/img/user/Screenshot%202026-09-15%20at%205.06.02%20PM.png)

#### Follicle
- Site of B-cell localisation and proliferation.
- Primary follicles are dense and quiescent
- Secondary follicles have pale centres and are active

#### Medulla
1. Consists of meullary cords (lymphocytes and plasma cells) and medullary sinuses(reticular cells and macrophages)
2. Communicate with efferents

#### Paracortex
1. Contains T-cells
2. Enlargres in extremem cellular response ([[EBV\|EBV]], other viral infections) -> Lymphadenopathy
3. Underdeveloped in [[DiGeorge Syndrome\|DiGeorge Syndrome]]
