# Folic Acid
## Vitamin $B_9$ (Folate)
Converted to Tetrahydrofolic(THF) acid for single carbon transfer/methylation in [[Nucleotide synthesis\|Nucleotide synthesis]]. Found in leafy vegetables. Small amounts stored in liver


### Deficiency
Can be caused by many drugs (Trimethoprim, Phenytoin, Methotrexate.) Supplementation from 1 month before conception can prevent neural tube defects
1. *Macrocytic, Megaloblastic Anemia*
2. Hyper segmented Polymorphonuclear cells
3. Glossitis, no Neurological symptoms (unlike [[Cobalamin\|Cobalamin]] Deficiency)
4. &uarr; Homocysteine. Normal methylmalonic acid
