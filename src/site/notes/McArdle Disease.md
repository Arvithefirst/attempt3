# McArdle Disease
Type V [[Glycogen Metabolism\|Glycogen Storage Disorders]]

$$ \text{Deficiency of Skeletal muscle Glycogen Phosphorylase} $$

## Clinical Features
1. Increased glycogen in muscle -> Painful cramps
2. Myoglobinuria
3. Arrhythmias
4. *Second phenomena during exercise*
5. Flat venous lactate curve whit rise in ammonia during exercise
