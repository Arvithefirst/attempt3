# Rett Syndrome
Mutation of MECP2 on X chromosome. Seen in females (embryonically lethal in males)

## Clinical Features
1. Normal development till 6-18 months followed byb regression in Motor/cognitive/verbal abilities
2. Ataxia seizures
3. Scoliosis
4. Hand wringing
