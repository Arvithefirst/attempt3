## Pathophysiology
- Defects in ATM gene
- Failure to detect DNA damage and halt cell cycle
- Accumulation of mutations

## Clinical Features
Triple A triad
1. Cerebellar *A*taxia and atrophy
2. *A*ngiomas
3. Ig*A* deficiency (&uarr; [[Antibodies#IgM\|IgM]], AFP &darr; [[Antibodies#IgA\|IgA]], [[Antibodies#IgG\|IgG]], [[Antibodies#IgE\|IgE]])

// Increased sensitivity to radiation
// Increased risk of lymphomas and leukemia
