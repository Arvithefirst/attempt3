# Homocystinuria

Disorder of [[Methionine Cycle\|Methionine Cycle]]
## Pathophysiology
1. Deficiency of Cystathione-$\beta$-synthase l
2. &darr; affinity of Cystathione synthase for pyridoxal phosphate [[Pyridoxine\|Pyridoxine]]
3. Methionine Synthase deficiency
4. Methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase(MTHFR) deficiency

## Clinical Features
Not to be confused with [[Marfan Syndrome\|Marfan Syndrome]]. differentiated by AR inheritance, thrombosis, intellectual disability, and Downward/nasal lens dislocation
1. Tall stature with elongated torso/limbs
2. Pectus excavatum, Scoliosis, Kyphosis, Osteoporosis
3. Arachnodactyly, Hypermobile joints, hypreelastic skin
