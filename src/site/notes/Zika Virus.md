A +ve sense RNA [[Viruses\|Virus]]. Transmitted by *Aedes mosquito*

## Clinical Features
Typically seen in tropical/sub-tropical climates
1. Low grade fever
2. Itchy rash
3. conjunctivitis
May be complicated by [[Guillane-Barre Syndrome\|Guillane-Barre Syndrome]]
