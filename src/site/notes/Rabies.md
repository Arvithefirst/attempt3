-ve Sense RNA [[Viruses\|Virus]] 

## Etiology
1. Commonly transmitted by mammal bites (eg: bats, dogs, racoon,  skunk)
2. Aerosol transmission also possible form bat caves

## Pathophysiology
1. Binds to AchE receptors
2. Retrograde travel via *Dynein*
3. Cytoplasmic inclusions(*Negri bodies*) seen in purkinje cells of cerebellum and hippocompus

## Clinical Features
Incubation period is in wekks to months long
1. Fever malaise
2. Agitation *Photophobia, Hydrophobia*, Hypersalivation
3. Paralysis, coma
4. death

PEP with killed vaccine(if status -ve or unknown) + IVIG
PrEP with vaccine *(0,3,7,14)*
