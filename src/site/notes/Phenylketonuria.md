# Phenylketonuria
## Etiology
Disorder of [[Tyrosine Metabolism\|Tyrosine Metabolism]]
1. Autosomal recessive deficiency of Phenylalanine Hydroxylase(PAH)
2. Tetrahydrobiopterin deficiency also leads to decreased PAH activity


## Clinical Features
Screening occurs 2-3 days after birth
1. Intellectual disability
2. Microcephaly, Seizures
3. Hypopigmented skin, eczema, Musty body odor

## Treatment
1. Restrict Phenylalanine in diet, Increased Tyrosine intake (Sot, chicken, fish, $BH_4$)
2. Avoid Aspartame products as they are metabolised into Phenylalanine
