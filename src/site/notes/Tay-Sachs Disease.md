# Tay-Sachs Disease
[[Lysosomal Storage Disorders\|Lysosomal Storage Disorders]]

$$ \text{Defieincy of Hexosamindase A, Accumulation of }GM_2 \text{ ganglioside}$$
## Clinical Features
1. Progressive Neurodegeneration
2. Developmental delay/regression
3. Hyperreflexia, Hyperacusis
4. *Cherry Red spots on macula*
5. Onion skin lysosomes
