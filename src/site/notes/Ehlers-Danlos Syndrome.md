# Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome

Group of genetic connective tissue disorders caused by mixed inheritance mutations of
- *COL5A1* and *COL5A2* causing faulty synthesis of Type V [[Collagen\|Collagen]]
- *COL3A1* causing faulty synthesis of [[Collagen#Type III\|Collagen#Type III]] 
- *Procollagen Peptidase*

## Clinical Features
### Classical Type (Type V Collagen Mutation)
- Hypermobile joints
- Thin/Translucent hyperextensible skin
- Joint discoloration

### Vascular Type (Type III Mutation)
- Bleeding tendency
- Fragile tissues (Aorta, muscles, organs eg uterus)
- Berry aneuysms
