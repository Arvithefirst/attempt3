# Niacin
## Vitamin $B_3$ (Niacin)
Derived from tryptophan using [[Riboflavin\|Riboflavin]] and [[Pyridoxine\|Pyridoxine]]. Used in NAD and NADP cofactors in [[TCA cycle\|TCA cycle]]

### Deficiency
Pathognomic 3 Ds (Diarrhoea, Dermamtitis, Dementia)
- Glossitis
- Diarrhoea
- Dementia and hallucinations
- Dermatitis on sun-exposed skin, C3/C4 'Broad collar' Rash of 'Casal rash'
- Hyperpigmentation of limbs
// Similar seen in [[Hartnup Disease\|Hartnup Disease]], [[Malignant Carcinoid Syndrome\|Malignant Carcinoid Syndrome]], Isoniazid use (Pyridoxine deficiency)

## Excess
- Facial flushing
- Pruritus
- Hyperglycemia
- Hyperuricemia
