# Myotonic Dystrophy
CTG trinucleotide repeat expansion in the DMPK gene resulting in abnormal myotonin protein kinase leading to percussion myotonia

## Clinical Features
1. Progressive Myotonia
2. Muscle wasting
3. Cataract
4. Testicular atrophy
5. Frontal balding
6. Arrhythmia
7. Muscle biopsy shows ring fibres and central nuclei
