Enveloped, Linear, dsDNA viruses

Sexual/perinatal transmission. More common than HSV at [[Viral Meningitis\|Viral Meningitis]]. Stays latent in sacral 
### Clinical Features
Same as [[Herpes Simplex Virus-1\|HSV-1]] but more genital stuff and in neonates
1. Herpes labialis, Herpetic whitlow
2. Temporal lobe encephalitis
3. Erythema Multiforme
