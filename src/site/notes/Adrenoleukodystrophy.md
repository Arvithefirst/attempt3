Disease of the [[Peroxisome\|Peroxisome]]

## Pathophysiology
1. X linked recessive mutation of ABCD1 gene
2. VLCFA build up in *adren*al glands, White(*leuko*) matter of brain and testes
3. Progressive adrenal and neurological failure
