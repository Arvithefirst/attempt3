# Essential Fructosuria
$$ \text{Fructokinase Deficiency}$$

## Pathophysiology
Disorder of [[Fructose Metabolism\|Fructose Metabolism]]
1. Fructose unable to be trapped into cells -> Fructosuria
2. Dependant of Hexokinase for fructose metabolism

## Clinical Features
1. Asymptomatic Fructosuria
