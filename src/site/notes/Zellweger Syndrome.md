# Zellweger Syndrome
Disease of the [[Peroxisome\|Peroxisome]]

## Pathophysiology
1. Autosomal recessive Mutated PEX genes
2. Accumulation of VLCFA and pipecolic acid

## Clinical Features
1. Hypotonia, Seizures
2. Jaundice
3. Craniofacial dysmorphia
4. Hepatomegaly
5. Early death
