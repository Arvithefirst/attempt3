# Panthotenic Acid
## Vitamin $B_5$ (Panthotenic Acid)
Component for Coenzyme A(CoA) in [[TCA cycle\|TCA cycle]] and Fatty acid Synthase

#### Deficiency
1. Dermatitis
2. Enteritis
3. Alopecia
4. Adrenal insufficiency
5. Burning feet sensation, Distal parasthesia, dysthesia
