## Pathophysiology
- Defect in WAS gene
- Leukocytes and platelets unable to reorganise actic cytoskeleton
- Defective Antigen presentation 

## Clinical Features
1. Thrombocytopenia (Smaller and fewer platelets)
2. Eczema
3. Recurrent Pyogenic Infections (Normal or &darr; [[Antibodies#IgM\|IgM]], [[Antibodies#IgG\|IgG]], &uarr; [[Antibodies#IgA\|IgA]], [[Antibodies#IgE\|IgE]])

//Increased risk of autoimmune diseases and malignancy
