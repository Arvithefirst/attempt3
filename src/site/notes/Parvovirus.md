The smallest and only ssDNA [[Viruses\|Virus]] 

#### Pathophysiology
1. Infects RBC precursors and endothelial cells
2. RBC destruction -> Hydrops fetalis/pure RBC aplasia

## Clinical Features
1. Hydrops fetalis in newborns
2. *"Slapped cheek"* rash in children
3. Pure RBC aplasia in adults
4. Aplastic crisis in [[Sickle cell Disease\|Sickle cell Disease]]
