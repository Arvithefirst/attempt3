# Collagen
Most abundant protein in the human body. Organise and strengthens the extracellular matrix. 

## Synthesis
1. Preprocollagen synthesised in fibroblast.
>[!info] Preprocollagen
>Usually Glycine-X-Y where
>X: {Proline, Lysine}
>Y: {Hydroxyproline, Hydroxylysine}
2. *Hydroxylation* of hydroxylysine residues (Requires vitamin C, impaired in [[Vitamin C\|Scurvy]])
3. *Glycosylation* leads to formation of disulphide and hydrogen bonds leading to triple helix formation of Procollagen (impaired in [[Osteogenesis Imperfecta\|osteogenesis Imperfecta]])
4. *Exocytosis* into extracellular space
5. Cleavage of terminal disulphide-rich regions to form tropocollagen
6. Self assembly and aligment
7. Staggered tropocollagen form lysine-hydroxylsine covalent bonds using copper containing lysyl oxidase (Impaired in [[Menkes Disease\|Menkes Disease]])

## Types
There are like 4 common ones but the numbering goes upto XXVIII
#### Type I Collagen
- 90% of all. Found in bone, skin, tendons, dentin, fascia, cornea, late wounds
- Decreased formation in [[Osteogenesis Imperfecta\|osteogenesis Imperfecta]]

#### Type II Collagen
- Found in Cartilage, Vitreous body, nucleus pulposus

#### Type III
- Found in Blood vessels, Skin, uterus, Fetal tissue, Early wounds
- Secreted by myelofibroblasts. Deficient in [[Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome\|Ehlers-Danlos syndrome]]

#### Type IV
- Basement membranes, basal ganglia, lens
- Defective in [[Alport syndrome\|Alport syndrome]]. Targeted in [[Goodpasture Syndrome\|Goodpasture Syndrome]]
