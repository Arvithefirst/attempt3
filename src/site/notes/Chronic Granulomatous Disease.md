## Pathophysiology
1. Defect in NADPH Oxidase
2. Reduced superoxide formation
3. Impaired oxidative burst in neutrophils

## Clinical Features
1. Increases susceptibility to Catalase +ve organisms (Staphylococci spp)
2. Recurrent infections and granulomas
