# Prader-willi Syndrome

Deletion or Mutation of allele on Paternal Chromosome 15 whose maternal copy is normally silenced

## Clinical Features
1. Hyperphagia, Obesity
2. Intellectual disability
3. Hypogonadism
4. Hypotonia
//25% of cases are due to maternal uniparental disomy
