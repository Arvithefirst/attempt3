1% of all tumours in men however are 99% malignant

## Classification
1. [[Surgery Notes/Seminoma\|Seminoma]](50%)
2. [[Teratoma\|Teratoma]](30%) #incomplete
3. Mixed
4. Interstitial
5. Lymphoma

## Clinical Features
1. 20-30 in Teratoma, 30-40 in Seminoma
2. Testicular swelling with *Vas sparing*
3. Infertility, Haemospermia
4. Gynaecomastia
5. Weight loss, fatigue, nausea, etc etc
6. Metastasis - Lymphadenopathies, Distension

## Investigations
1. *NO BIOPSY* to prevent spread
2. Chest X-Ray
3. USG Abdomen, Testis
4. CT abdomen
5. Urine and serum HCG
6. Serum a-fetoprotein
7. Serum LDH
8. Serum Placental alkaline phosphatase

## Staging
I - No metastases
II - Nodes below diaphragm only
III - Nodes above diaphragm
IV - Pulmonary or hepatic masses

## Treatment 
1. Radical Orchidectomy
2. Radiotherapy/Chemotherapy if metastasis
