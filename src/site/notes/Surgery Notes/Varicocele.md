Varicose dilation of the veins draining the testis

## Etiology
1. More common in adolescence or early adulthood
2. More common on the left side*
// Left testicular vein drains in to renal vein at a sharp angle. More prone to obstructions, hypertension etc

## Clinical features
1. Usually aymptomatic. May have swelling, redness, dragging discomfort
2. "Bag of worms" on palpation. Cough impulse may be seen
3. May cause infertility

## Treatment
1. Ligation through inguinal/retroperitoneal aapproach
2. Embolisation
