Non-scrapable White patch appearing in the mouth

## Causes
1. Smoking
2. Spices
3. Spirits
4. Sharp teeth, sepsis, poor oral hygeine
5. Sunlight
6. Syphillis
7. Betel nut

## Stages
1. *Keratosis* - White milky blush
2. *Acanthosis* - Elongation of Rete pegs. Smooth, white, dry patch
3. *Dyskeratosis* - Keratin layer formation in deeper epidermis
4. *Speckled leukoplakia* - Multiple small white patches on erythemous base
5. Carcinoma in-situ ([[Surgery Notes/Oral Cancer\|Oral Cancer]])

## Treatment
Usually irreversible
1. Try isoretinoin
