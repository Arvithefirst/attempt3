## Types
1. Tuberculouur papillary ulcer
2. Cavernous form
3. [[Surgery Notes/Hydronephrosis\|Hydronephrosis]]
4. Pyonephrosis
5. Tubercular perinephric abscess
6. Calcified Tuberculous aarea
7. Caseous kidney (also called putty kidney or cement kidney)
8. Miliary kidney

## Clinical features
1. Common in males and common on right side
2. Sterile pyuria
3. Painful micturation with haematuria, haemospermia
4. Renal/suprapubic/pelvic pain
5. Rarely palpable
6. Enlarged prostate, thickened vas/epididymis, Infertility, impotence
7. Urinary stones, recurrent UTI, Hypertension, Renal failure
8. Dyspareunia, menstrual dysfunction, discharge
9. Fever, weight loss

## Investigations
1. CBC, TLC
2. 3 Early Morning Samples of Urine(EMSU) for ZN stain
3. Tuberculin test
4. Xray chest, abdomen
5. Xray KUB
6. CT abdomen
7. [[Surgery Notes/Intravenous urography\|Intravenous urography]]
8. [[Surgery Notes/Retrograde pyelography\|Retrograde pyelography]]
9. Cystoscopy - Multiple tubercles (golf hole ureter)
10. PCR
11. Voiding cystourethrography

## Treatment
HRZE for 1 year
1. Hanleys renal cavernostomy (to drain ptocalyx)
2. Abnderson hynes operation or stenting for [[Surgery Notes/Hydronephrosis#Treatment\|Hydronephrosis#Treatment]]
3. Ureteral stenting
4. Dilatation, cystoplasty for thimble bladder
5. Nephroureterectomy
