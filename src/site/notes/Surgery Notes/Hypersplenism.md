*Overactivity of spleen* causing pancytopenia even with hypercellular bonemarrow

## Causes
1. Primary hypersplenism
2. Portal hypertension
3. Malaria, Kala azar
4. Schistosomiasis
5. TB
6. Myelooproliferatve disorders
