Surgical removal of gallbladder

## Indications
- Symptomatic [[Surgery Notes/Gallstones\|Gallstones]]
- [[Surgery Notes/Acute Cholecystitis\|Acute Cholecystitis]], Chronic cholecystitis
- Empyema gallbladder
- Mucocele gallbladder
// May also be done *prophylactically in Diabetics*, patients with *congenital haemolytic anemia*, or who have underwent *bariatric surgrey*

## Approaches
1. *Right subcostal* (Kochers)
2. Right paramedial
3. Horizon incision
4. Mayo-robson
5. Laprascopic Cholecystectomy

## Techniques
#### Duct first method 
Goes through calots triangle. Cystic artery is identified and ligated. Duct is ligated close to the bladder

#### Fundus first method 
Done when there are adhesions. Starts with fundus and dissected proximally. Ending with Duct and cystic artery

## Complications
1. Infection
2. Bleeding
3. Injury to CBD or hepatic duct
4. Bile leak
5. Biliary stricture
6. Injuries to colon
