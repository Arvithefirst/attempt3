Can be classified as
- *Primary(first intention)* - Clean incised wounds, Less fibrosis, complete closure with linear smooth scar
- *Secondary(Second intention)* - Extensive tissue loss, Heals slowly with mostly fibrosis, may leave hypertrophied/keloid scar or disability

## Stages of wound healing
1. Inflammation
2. Granulation tissue formation
3. Epithelialisation
4. Scar formation and resorption
5. Maturation

## Factors affecting wound healing
#### Local
1. Infection
2. Foreign body
3. Poor Perfusion
4. Site
5. Size
6. Mechanism
#### General
1. Age, obesity, smoking
2. Malnutrition, Vit C deficiency, Anemia
3. Diaabetes, Hypertension, systemic illnesses
