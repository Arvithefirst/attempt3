Involves abolition of all senses in a state of reversible loss of consciousness. Comprised of 
1. Analgesia
2. Hypnosis
3. Musclerelaxation
4. Amnesia

## Drugs used
#### Anaesthetics
1. Volatile - Ether, Halothane
2. Gaseous - Nitrous oxide, Cyclopropane
3. IV - Thiopentone, Ketamine, Propofol, Fentanyl
#### Muscle relaxants
1. Depolarising - Suxamethonium chloride
2. Non-depolarising - Tubocurarine, Pancuroniium, Gallamine
#### Reversal agents
1. Neostigmine

## Complications
1. Myocardial depression and cardiac arrest
2. Hypertension
3. Laryngeal/Bronchospasm
4. Arrhythmia
5. Respiratory failure
6. Mendelson syndrome
7. Hypoxia
8. Pneumothorax
9. Anaphylaxis
10. Malignant hyperthermia
11. Hypothermia
