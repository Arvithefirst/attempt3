Refers to benign salivary gland neoplasm 

## Pathology
1. Epithelial cells proliferate in strands or arranged as acini/cords
2. Myoepithelium prolkiferates in sheets (spindle shapedcells)
3. Produces mucoid secretions
4. Compresses parotid. Eventually penetrates into parotid

## Clinical features
1. Middle aged females 
2. Painless slow growing swelling in front of ear

1. Non-adherent, rubbery swelling in front of, below and behind ear
2. Raised ear lobule
3. Obliterated retromandibular groove
4. Smooth shiny skin

>[!bug] Suspect m,alignant change when
>1. Rapid growth
>2. Skin infiltration
>3. Facial nerve involvement
>4. Fixed to masseter
>5. red dialted veins
>6. Lymphadenopathy
>7. Stony hard consistency

## Investigations
No biopsy until malignant change suspected because 
1. Danger to facial nerve
2. Further seeding of tumour

1. FNAC
2. CT
3. FNAC lymph nodes
4. Xray bones
5. MRI

## tyreatment
1. Conservative Superficial parotidectomy
2. Total conservative parotidectomy(facial n4erve conserved)
3. Total Parotidectomy with excisionof facial nerve
4. Radical parotidectomy (involves skin, TMJ, Mandible, Petrous bone)
5. Postoperative radiotherapy
