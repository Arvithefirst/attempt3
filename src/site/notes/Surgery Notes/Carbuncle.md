Infective [[Surgery Notes/Gangrene\|gangrene]] of subcutaneous tissue by *Staphylococcus aureus*. Commonly seen in diabetics

#### Pathology
1. Initial lesion is similar to a boil 
2. Grows and results in necrosis of subcutaneous fat 
3. Gives rise to *multiple intercommunicating abscesses* with *"sieve-like"* openings(descibed as *cribiform appearance*)

## Clinical feautes
1. More common in [[Medicine notes/Diabetes Mellitus\|Diabetes Mellitus]]
2. Severe pain and swelling in nape of Neck
3. Constitutional symptoms like ferver with chills and rigors
4. Red indurated surface like "*red hot coal*"
5. Cribiform appearance with central slough

## Complications
1. Worsening of diabetes [[Medicine notes/Diabetic ketoacidosis\|Diabetic ketoacidosis]]
2. Extensive necrosis of skin
3. Septicemia and toxaemia

## Treatment
1. Antibiotics (Penicillins < Methicillin < Vancomycin)
2. Insulin
3. Dressing
4. Cruciate incision for excision and irrigation
