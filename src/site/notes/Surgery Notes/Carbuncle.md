Infective gangrene of subcutaneous tissue by Staphylococcus aureus. Commonly seen in diabetcs

## Pathology
Initial lesion is similar to a boil which grows and results in necrosis of subcutaneous fat which gives rise to *multiple intercommunicatingf abscesses* with "*sieve-like" openings(descibed as cribiform appearance)*

## clinical feautes
1. Diabetic
2. severe pain and swelling in nape of neck
3. Constitutional symptoms liek ferver with chills and rigors
4. Red indurated surface like "red hot coal"
5. cribiform appearance wirth central slough

## Complications
1. Ketoacidosis
2. Extensive necrosis of skin
3. Septicemia and toxaemia

## Treatment
1. Antibiotics
2. Insulin
3. Dressing
4. Cruciate incision for excision and irrigation
