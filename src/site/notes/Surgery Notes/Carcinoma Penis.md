In men >50 years of age. [[Surgery Notes/Phimosis\|Phimosis]] is a predisposing condition

## Etiology
Typically originates from the followign pre-malilgnant lesions
1. Genital warts
2. Erythroplasia of queyrat/Pagets disease of the penis
3. Leukoplakia
4. Bowens disease

## Clinical features
1. Non healing Ulceroproliferative lesion starting over glans which may involve shaft
2. Foul smelling discharge
3. Hematuria

## Staging
*Stage I* - Over glans
*Stage II* - Involving Shaft
*Stage III* - I or II Involving regional metastases
*Stage IV* - Distal metastases or spread further than shaft

## Investigations
1. Routines
2. Wedge biopsy
3. USG/MRI for extent of lesion
4. FNAC inguinal lymph nodes

## Treatment
Depends on the staging
### Stage I
Circumcision if growth is restricted to the prepuce
Partial amputation if it involves glans

### Stage II
Partial amputation leaving minimum 2.5cm for sexual an durinry function
Total penectomy with perineal urethostomy

### Stage III
Partial or total amputation followed by ilioinguinal block dissection of affected lymph nodes (Sentinel lymph node biopsy)

### Stage IV
Radiotherapy(external/interstitial) + Chemotherapy
