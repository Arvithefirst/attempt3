Also called trophic Ulcer. *Defined as tissue necrosis and ulceration due to prolonged pressure

## Common sites
1. Ischial tuberosity 
2. Sacrum
3. Heel
4. Head of metatarsal
5. Buttocks
6. Occiput

## Etiologies
1. Impaired pain sensation in anesthesia / Peripheral neuropathies
2. Nutritional deficiencies
3. Incontinence causing frequent skin soiling

## Staging of Trophic Ulcers
1. Non-blanching erythema
2. Partial thinkness skin loss
3. Full thickness skin loss
4. Full thickness skin loss along with loss of underlying tissue
