also called *Epithelioma; Khangri disease; Chimney cancer* Arises from epidermal keratinocytes

## Etiology
2nd most common of skin cancer. Exhibits male preference.
>[!faq] Usually occurs in pre-existing premalignant lesions
Bowens disease
Chronic scars/burns. Chemically induced skin irritation
Lupus vulgaris
Keratosises
Radiation exposure (risk is proportional to wavelenght)
More common in tobacco users
## Clinical features
- Ulcerative or ulceroproliferative lesion 
- Rasied/everted edge. Indurated base
- Bloody discharge
- Involves regional lymph nodes

## Investigations
1. CBC, etc etc
2. Histology (Broder classsification, Keratin pearls, Cytokeratins 1-10)
>[!info] Broders classification
>Well differentiated - >75% keratin pearls
>Moderately differentiated - >50% keratin pearls
>Poorly differentiated - >25% keratin pearls
>Undifferentiated - <25% keratin pearls
## Managemet
1. Medical
2. Surgical excision
