>[!tldr] Definition
>Plasma potassium concentration >5.5 mmol/L

## Etiology
Decreased loss
1. Renal failure, Tubular disorders
2. Drugs - ACEi's, ARB's, NSAIDs, Heparin
3. Mineralocorticoid deficiency
Increased intake
1. IV potassium
2. Packed Red cell transfusion
Extracellular shift
1. Acidosis
2. Low insulin
3. B-adrenergic drugs, Flouride
Cellular breakdown
1. Tumour lysis syndrome
2. Rhabdomyolysis
3. Crush injury

## Clinical features
1. Weakness / Flaccid paralysis

>[!faq] ECG Changes in hyperkalemia
>1. Tall T waves with short QT interval (6-7 mmol/L)
>2. Wide QRS and loss of P wave (8-10 mmol/L)
>3. Ventricular fibrillation (>10 mmol/L)

## Treatment
1. Calcium gluconate
2. Sodium bicarbonate
3. Dextrose + Insulin
4. Hyperventilation
5. Salbutamol
6. Calcium exchange resins
7. Haemodialysis
