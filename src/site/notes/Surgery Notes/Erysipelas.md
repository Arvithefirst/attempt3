Spreading Strep.pyogenes infection of the skin and subcutaneous tissue.

### Clinical features
1. Toxaemia
2. Rash - "Rose red," Fast spreading, bleaching, raised with sharp margins
3. Serous discharge from lymphangitis
4. Milians ear sign - Sparing of ear lobule

### Treatment
penicillins

//*Erysipeloid disease - "Fish handlers disease" occurs following scratches. It has features of both erysipelas and cellulitis*
