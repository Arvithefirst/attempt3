Excision of skin under microscope to accurately identify the margins of malignancies. Allows for better cosmetics (removes least possible tissue)
#### Indications
1. Centrofacial tumours - BCC, [[Surgery Notes/Squamous Cell Carcinoma\|SCC]]
2. Tumours with ill defined margins
3. Recurrent lesions
4. Tumours at site of previous radiotherapy 
5. Immunosuppressed
