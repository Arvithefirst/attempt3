Bilateral episodic digital ischaemia in upper limb. May
1. Primary (Raynauds disease)
2. Secondary (Artheroclerosis, Cervical rib, Carpal Tunnel, [[Medicine notes/Systemic Lupus Erythematosus\|SLE]])

#### Pathophysiology
Triggered by cold/stress. More common in women
1. Stage of Syncope - Brief arteriole constriction causes pallor 
2. Stage of Asphyxia - Upon dilating, the returning blood is deoxygenated(cyanotic)
3. Stage of recovery/Rubor - Full relaxation of arterioles causes return of blood 

## Clinical features
1. Bilateral, episodic, digital, pain upon exposure to cold
2. Thumb is usually spared
3. Peripheral pulses normal
4. Gangrenous patches on tips of fingers if long standing

## Treatment
#### Consservcative
1. Avoid cold. smoking, stress
2. Calcium antagonists

### Cervical sympathectomy
- Removal of sympathetic truck from below stellate anglion till 3rd thoracic ganglion
#### Complications
1. Perforation of pleura (pneumothorax)
2. horners syndrome
3. haemorrhage
