Inability to retract prepuce

## Etiology
1. Congenital
2. Balantoposthitis
3. Chancre
4. Carcinoma penis
## Clinical features
1. Inability to retract foreskin
2. Difficulty in cleaning
3. Ballooning or 2nd bladder formation
## Complications
1. Carcinoma penis
2. Infection
3. [[Surgery Notes/Paraphimosis\|Paraphimosis]]

## Treatment
Circumcision
