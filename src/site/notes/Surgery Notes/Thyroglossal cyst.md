Congenital abnormality resulting from incomplete degereation of thyroglossal tract

## Clinical features
1. Smooth, Cystic, non-tender, Swelling in midline that moves with deglutinatyion and protrusion of tongue
2. Tugging sensation felt when protruding tongue

## Investigations
1. Radioisotope study
2. USG neck
3. FNAC

## Treatment
1. Sistruck operation - Excisioin of cyst upto foramen caecum along with removal of central part of hyoid
