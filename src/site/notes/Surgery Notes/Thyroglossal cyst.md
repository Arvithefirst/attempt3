Congenital abnormality resulting from *incomplete degeneration of thyroglossal tract*

## Clinical features
1. Smooth, Cystic, Non-tender, Transillumination negative *swelling in midline*
2. *Moves with deglutination* and protrusion of tongue
3. Tugging sensation felt when protruding tongue

## Investigations
1. Radioisotope study
2. USG neck
3. FNAC

## Treatment
1. Sistruck operation - Excision of cyst upto foramen caecum along with removal of central part of hyoid
