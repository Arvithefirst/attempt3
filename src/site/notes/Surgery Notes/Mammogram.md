Radiological screening tool for [[Surgery Notes/Carcinoma Breast\|Carcinoma Breast]]. Uses selenium coated xray plate to give mediolateral and craniocaudal views

## Indication
1. Coursely nodular breast
2. Fibroadenosis
3. Woman

## Reporting (BI-RADS scoring)
Breast Imaging, Reporting And Data System

0 - Incomplete
1- Negative
2 - Normal but with other abnormal finding
3 - 2% chance of cancer
4 -25-35% chance iof cancer
5 - 95% chance of cancer
6 - Pathologically confirmed cancer
