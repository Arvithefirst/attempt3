Spreading, destructive, *invasive, infection of skin* and subcutaneous tissue including deep fascia, with *relative sparing of muscle* 

Fourniers gangrene - Necrotising fasciitis of Genitalia
Meleneys gangrene - Necrtotising fasciitis of abdominal wall

## Etioloogy
1. Common in lower extremities, genitalia, lower abdomen
2. More common in [[Medicine notes/Diabetes Mellitus\|Diabetes Mellitus]], Malnutrition, Obesity, Immunocompromised

*Type I Necotizing Fasciitis* -> Polymicrobial (Group A Strep. + Anaerobes + coliforms)
*Type II Necrotizing Fasciitis* -> Monomicrobial (ß-hemolytic Strep.)

## Clinical features
1. Sudden pain, with gross, red, erythematous, oedematous swelling
2. Skip lesions of skin necrosis and ulceration
3. Bronze hue, Brawny induration, Blebs, Crepitus
4. Fever, Jaundice, [[Medicine notes/Acute Kidney Injury\|Acute Kidney Injury]]

## Investigations
1. Full thickness Skin biopsy
2. Watery pus

## Treatment
1. Supportive treatment
2. Penicillins, Vacomycin, methicillin
3. Clindamycin(Type II NF)
4. Surgical excision + Skin graft
