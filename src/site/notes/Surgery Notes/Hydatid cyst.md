Meaning "dew drop" or "Watery vesicle." It is seen in *Echinococcus granulosum(dog-tapeworm) infection.* Typically involves [[Surgery Notes/Surgical anatomy of liver#Segmental anatomy\|VIIth segment of liver]]

## Pathology
The cyst has 3 layers:
1. Adventitia - Inseperable from liver tissue
2. Ectocyst - Whitish elastic can be peeled from liver tissue
3. Ectocyst - Secretes *hydatid fluid* with scolises
// *Hydatid fluid* is clear with high specific gravity and shows hooklets/scolises(sand)

#### Mechanism of pathogenesis
Cyst may enlarge and/or rupure causing
- Rupture into biliary channels
- Rupture into peritoneal cavity
- Secondary infection/cysts
- Hepatic dysfunction
- Disseminated abdominal hydatidosis

## Clinical features
1. My be asymptomatic with classic "Hydatid thrill"
2. Jaundice, pain
3. Anaphylactic features
4. Upper right quadrant discomfort, dsypepsia, cachexia in children
5. Splenomegaly, pleural effusion
*// Camelottes sign* - Following intrabiliary rupure, gas enters the cyst causing one wall to collapse

## Investigations
1. USG abdomen shows daughter cysts, double membraned cysts, adn calcifications *(Diagnostic test)*
2. Xray shows calcifications
3. CT shoes cart-wheel like mmultivesicular rosette cysts
4. ELISA, agglutination tests, IF antibody testing, immunoelectrophoresis
5. PCR
6. Liver function tests
7. MRI/ERCP to visualise biliary tree involvement
*//Avoid aspiration as it risks anaphylaxis*

>[!faq] WHO classification of hydatid cyst
>CL - Active no cyst wall, not fertile
>CE1 - Active with cyst wall and sand, fertile
>CE2 - Active with rosette cyst, fertile
>CE3 - Transitional, water lily sign
>CE4 - Inactive, degenerative contents, not fertile
>CE 5 - Inactive, calcified wall, not fertile

## Treatment
### Medical management
Only indicated in patients with unoperable cysts or are unfit for surgery. Also indicated in the days prior to intervention
- Albendazole
- Mebendazole

### Surgical management
Can be in the form of PAIR, open surgery or laprascopic pericystectomy. involves the use of [[Surgery Notes/Scolicidal agents\|Scolicidal agents]]
#### PAIR (Puncture, Aspirate, Inject, Reaspirate)
The above steps are performed *under USG/CT guidance with 22 guage needle.* Radioopaque dye may be used to locate communications. [[Surgery Notes/Scolicidal agents\|Scolicidal agents]] are used for aspiration

May be modified into Double-Puncture Aspiration and Injection (D-PAI) or Percutaneous EVacuation of Cyst(PEVAC)

#### Open surgery - *Gold standard treatment*
- Abdomen in opened and packed with black mops for easy identification of the white scolices to avoid spillage
- Cyst is aspirated and replaced with [[Surgery Notes/Scolicidal agents\|Scolicidal agents]]
- Any communications are closed by suture and drains placed
