>[!quote] Definition
>Abdominal viscera herniating into inguinal canal. Can occur through deep ring(Indirect) or though posterior wall(Hasselbachs triangle) of inguinal canal(Direct)

# Layers of inguinal sac(outwards)
1. Extraperitonial tissue
2. Internal spermatic fascia
3. Cremasteric muscle
4. External spermatic fascia
5. Skin

# Contents of Inguinal canal
1. Round ligament (in females)
2. Ilioinguinal nerve
3. Spermatic cord (in males)
	1. Vas deferens
	2. Artery to vas and its sympathetic plexus
	3. Testicular and cremasteric arteries
	4. Genital branch of genitofemoral nerve
	5. Pampiniform plexus
	6. Remains of process vaginalis

# Hasselbachs Triangle
Bound Medially by<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;"> Lateral border of rectus</mark>, laterally by <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Inferior epigastric artery</mark> and inferiorly by <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;"> Inguinal ligament</mark>

Further divided by obliterated umbilical artery which is used to further classify direct hernias into medial and lateral

# Clinical tests
1. Internal ring occlusion
2. Ring invagination test
3. Ziemans three finger test
4. Silk glove sign
5. Leg raise

# Direct vs Indirect

| Indirect                                            | Direct                     |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Any Age                                             | Common in Elderly          |
| Pyriform/oval                                       | Globular                   |
| Commonly descends into scrotum                      | Uncommon                   |
| Sac is Anterolateral to cord                        | Sac is posterior to cord   |
| Ring occlusion negative                             | Ring occlusion positive    |
| Ziemans felt on 1st finger                          | Ziemans felt on 2nd finger |
| Cough impulse on Invagination felt on tip of finger | Felt on pulp               |

# Surgeries
1. <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Herniotomy </mark>
2. Modified Bassinis Hernioohaphy
3. Lytles repair
4. Shouldice repair
5. Hernioplasty (prime treatment for inguinal)
	1. Onlay/Inlay mesh
	2. Lichtenstein tension free repair
	3. Modified Rives Pre-peritonial mesh repair
	4. Stoppa giant prosthesis reinforcement of visceral sac
	5. Gilbert mesh repair
	6. <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Transabdominal preperitonial laprascopic mesh repair (TAPP)</mark>
	7. <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Totally extraperitonial laprascopic mesh repair (TEP)</mark>
