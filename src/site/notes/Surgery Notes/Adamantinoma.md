*Benign tumour arising from the ameloblasts(enamel forming cells.)* Also called ameloblastoma, Eve's disease

## Clinical features
1. Usually arises from mandible. Can also arise from tibia or Pituitary
2. 30-40 year olds
3. As it grows undergoes cystic degeneration forming *multiple cystic spaces
4. Presenting complain is falling teeth
5. Local spread. Late stages may spread to lungs

*Diagnosed by Xray* - Honey comb appearance of one large and many small cystic spaces

## Treatment
Curettage or enucleation usually results in recurrence
1. Segemntal excision of mandible
2. Hemimadibulectomy
