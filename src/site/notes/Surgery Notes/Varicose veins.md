>[!quote] Definition
>Dilated tortuous elongated veins in the leg. There is reversal of blood flow due to faulty valves

# Venous drainage of lower limb
1. Deep veins (90%)
	1. Tibial,  Popliteal, Femoral ("Conduits")
	2. Venous sinuses aka Musculovenous pumps aka periperal heart
2. Superficial veins (10%)
	1. Long Saphenous
	2. Short Saphenous
3. Perforators
	1. Ankle (May or Kuster)
	2. Lower leg (Cockett I, II, III)
	3. Gastronemius (Boyd)
	4. Mid thigh (Dodd)
	5. Thigh (Hunters)

# Aetiology
1. Primary
	1. Congenital incompetence/lack of valves
	2. Weakness/wasting of muscle
	3. Stretching of deep fascia
2. Secondary
	1. Recurrent thrombophlebitis
	2. Long standing
	3. Obstruction of venous flow (Tumour, lymphadenopathy)
	4. Pregnancy

# Clinical findings
1. Dragging pain
2. Oedema and Heaviness
3. Late night cramps
4. Scarring of dermal/subdermal tissue - Lipodermatosclerosis
5. Narrowing of ankle - Champagne bottle appearance
6. Pale atrophic skin - Atrophic blanche

# Clinical tests
1. Brodie - Trendelenburg test I and II
2. Perthes and Modified Perthes
3. Three tourniquet test
4. Schwartz
5. Pratts
6. Morrisey cough impulse
7. Fegans
8. Ian-aird Test

# Treatment
1. Conservative
	1. Crepe bandage
	2. Unna boots
2. Medical
	1. Calcium Dobesilate
	2. Diosmin
	3. Sclerosing agents (ethanol )
3. Surgical
	1. <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Trendelenburg operation</mark>
	2. <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Stripping of vein</mark>
	3. Subfascial ligation of cockett and bodd
	4. Ligation of Short saphenous vein
	5. Hook phlebectomy
	6. Lintons vertical approach
	7. Stab avulsion
	8. Subfascial endoscopic perforator ligation
	9. <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Radiofrequency Ablation</mark>
	10. Endovenous Laser ablation
