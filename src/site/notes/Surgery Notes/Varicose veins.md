>[!quote] Definition
>Dilated tortuous elongated veins in the leg.

## Venous drainage of lower limb
1. Deep veins (90%)
	1. Tibial,  Popliteal, Femoral ("Conduits")
	2. Venous sinuses aka Musculovenous pumps aka periperal heart
2. Superficial veins (10%)
	1. Long Saphenous
	2. Short Saphenous
3. Perforators
	1. Ankle (May or Kuster)
	2. Lower leg (Cockett I, II, III)
	3. Gastronemius (Boyd)
	4. Mid thigh (Dodd)
	5. Thigh (Hunters)

## Aetiology
1. Primary
	1. Congenital incompetence/lack of valves
	2. Weakness/wasting of muscle
	3. Stretching of deep fascia
2. Secondary
	1. Recurrent thrombophlebitis
	2. Long standing
	3. Obstruction of venous flow (Tumour, lymphadenopathy)
	4. Pregnancy
	5. [[OBGY Notes/Oral Contraceptive Pills\|Oral Contraceptive Pills]] alter blood viscosity
#### Risk factors
Height, weight, occupation, left side more affected

## Clinical findings
1. Aching pain
2. Oedema and Heaviness
3. Late night cramps
4. Scarring of dermal/subdermal tissue - Lipodermatosclerosis
5. Narrowing of ankle - Champagne bottle appearance
6. Pale atrophic skin - Atrophic blanche

#### Clinical tests
1. *Brodie - Trendelenburg test I and II* - SF and Perforator incompetence respectively
2. *Perthes and Modified Perthes test* - Deep vein thrombosis
3. *Three tourniquet test* - Perforator site
4. *Schwartz test*(fluid thrill when standing) 
5. *Pratts test* - Bandage applied bottom up(milking). tourniquet. Bandage upplied up to down (perforators blow out)
6. *Morrisey cough impulse*
7. *Fegans test* - Bulges are marked in standing. After milking in supine the incompetence is felt with finger
8. *Ian-aird Test* - Milked, two fingers at sfj, relesae top one. feel pressure

#### Complications
1. Exzema, Dermatitis, Lipodermatosclerosis
2. Haemorrhage
3. Thrombophlebitis, Periostitis
4. Venous ulcer, [[Surgery Notes/Marjolins ulcer\|Marjolins ulcer]]
5. Calcifications, Equinovarus deformity
# Investigations
1. Color doppler - Biphasic signal
2. Duplex scan - Mickey mouse sign, Saphenous eye sign
3. Venography
4. Plethysmography

# Treatment
1. Conservative
	1. Crepe bandage
	2. Unna boots
2. Medical
	1. Calcium Dobesilate
	2. Diosmin
	3. Sclerosing agents (ethanol )
3. Surgical
	1. [[Surgery Notes/Trendelenburg operation\|Trendelenburg operation]]
	2. <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Stripping of vein</mark>
	3. Sclerotherapy
	4. Subfascial ligation of cockett and bodd
	5. Ligation of Short saphenous vein
	6. Hook phlebectomy
	7. Lintons vertical approach
	8. Stab avulsion
	9. Subfascial endoscopic perforator ligation
	10. <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Radiofrequency Ablation</mark>
	11. Endovenous Laser ablation
