Closed injuries cause haematoma formation which in turn compresses majors vessels causing
- Pallor
- Pulselessness
- Pain
- Paresthesia
- Diffuse swelling
*// typically seen in calves and forearms*
>[!info] Injuries with high chance of developing compartment syndrome
>1. [[Ortho notes/Supracondylar fracture of Humerus\|Supracondylar fracture of humerus]]
>2. Forearm bone fracture
>3. Closed tibial fracture
>4. Crush injuries to leg or forearm
## Complications
1. Infection
2. Chronic ischaemic contracture (see [[Ortho notes/Volkmann's ischaemic contracture\|Volkmann's ischaemic contracture]], Disability
3. [[Surgery Notes/Gangrene\|Gangrene]]
4. Renal failure (due to muscle necrosis releasing myoglobin)

## Treatment 
Compartment pressure >30mmHg is an indication for *fasciotomy*
