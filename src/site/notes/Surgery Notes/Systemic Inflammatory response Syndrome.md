SIRS is the bodies systemic response to an infected wound

Any *two* of 
1. Hyperthermia(>38C) / Hypothermia(<36C)
2. Tachycardia(>90bpm) or tachypnea(>20/min)
3. White cell count $>12*10^9$ or $<4*10^9$
