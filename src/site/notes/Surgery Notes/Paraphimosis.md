Inability to return retracted prepuce to normal position

## Causes
1. Improper catheterisation

## Clinical features
1. Pain
2. Swelling

## Complications
1. Ulcer glans
2. [[Surgery Notes/Gangrene\|Gangrene]]

## Management
1. Sedation
2. Hyaluronidase injection and gentle manipulation
3. Circumciusion
