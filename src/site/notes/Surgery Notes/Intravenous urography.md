Short note for smoe reason (abbreviatee IVU). Its the consecutive xrays one

## Indications
1. Congenital abnormality
	1. Horseshoe kidney - "Flower vase"
	2. Double ureter
	3. Ureterocele - "Cobra head"
	4. Polycystic kidney - "Spider leg"
	5. Retrocaval ureter - Reverse J sign
2. [[Surgery Notes/Hydronephrosis\|Hydronephrosis]] - Clubbing of calyces
3. Renal cell carcinoma - Irregular filling defect
4. Bilateral kidney function test
5. Renal injury
6. After urosurgery for outcome assessment

## Procedure
Done at 8hours of fasting only after KUB
1. 1ml test dose of Urograffin (wait 5 minutes for adverse reaction)
2. 1 ml/kg Urograffin
3. Xray at 1-5 minutes
4. Xray 15 minutes later
5. Xray 20-30 minutes later
6. Continue if necesary upto 72 hours

## Contraindications
1. Iodine sensitivity/Thyrotoxicosis
2. Multiple myelome
