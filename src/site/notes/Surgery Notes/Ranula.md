Mucous extravasation cyst that ariuses from the sublingual salivary gland

## Pathology
1. Initially there is a blockage of the duct causing retention cyst formation. Increased pressure ruptures the acini leading to the formation of an extravasatoin cyst

## Clinincal features
1. Blue, smooth, soft, fluctant, brilliantly transilluminant swelling in the lasteral aspect of the mouth 
2. May extend into the submandibuklar reagion "Plunging ranula"

## Treatment
1. Marsupialisation
2. Excision and drainage if small
// recurrence likely in excision
