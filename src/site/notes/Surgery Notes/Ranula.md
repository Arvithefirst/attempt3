*Mucous extravasation cyst* that arises from the *sublingual salivary gland*

#### Pathology
- Blockage of the duct causing retention cyst formation. 
- Increased pressure ruptures the acini leading to the formation of an Extravasation/Retention cyst

## Clinincal features
1. *Blue, smooth, soft, fluctant, brilliantly transilluminant* swelling in the lasteral aspect of the mouth 
2. May extend into the submandibular reagion "Plunging ranula"

## Treatment
1. Marsupialisation
2. Excision and drainage if small
*// recurrence likely in excision*
