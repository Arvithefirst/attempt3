Well differentiated [[Surgery Notes/Squamous Cell Carcinoma\|Squamous Cell Carcinoma]] that occurs in scar tissue. Most common in burn or venous scars with chronic irritation

## Clinical features
1. Slowly growing (Scar tissue is avascular)
2. Locally malignant (Lacks lymphatics to spread)
3. Painless (Scar tissue has no nerve endings)
4. Indurated, raised edge iwth normal surrounding skin and marked fibrosis

## Investigations
1. Wedge biopsy

## Treatment
1. Excision
2. Amputation
