Intermittent "Crampy" kind of pain seen in the libmbs due to arterial occlusion. 

Pain typically develops during exercise due to accumulation of substance P and metabolites. Further exercise opens up collaterals and washed away the metabolites

## Boyds classification of claudication
*Grade 1* - Pain on walking some "claudication distance" which relieves on its own after walking longer
*Grade 2* - Paon perissts on walking but patient cant still walk
*Grade 3* - Patient has to sake rest

## Types of Claudication
1. Arterial - Classic claudication pain
2. Neurogenic - Pain disappeasr almost immediately aftyer beginning to walk.
3. Venous - Rarely occurs
