Telescoping or invaginatio of one segment of bowel in to the adjacent one. Can be ileo-ileal, Ileo-colic(75%), colocolic

## Aetiopathology
Usually seen in children. Typically *after weaning or URTI due to inflammation of peyers patches.*

1. Invaginating part is called the apex
2. Outer sheath is i*ntussuscipiens*. Inner in *intussusceptum*
3. Intussusceptum becomes ischaemic gangrene leading to passign of Red-currant jelly per anum

## Clinical features
1. Sudden onset colicky pain with distension vomitting, *Red currant jelly stool*
2. *Sausage shaped mass* felt near umbilicus. Mobile, resonant, doesnt move with respiration.
3. Empty right iliac fossa
4. Peritonitis

## Investigations
1. Routines
2. Xray Abdomen - Multiple air levels
3. Barium enema - *Coiled spring sign*
4. USG abdomen - *Target sign*
5. Doppler - *Doughnut sign* 

## Treatment
1. Non operative reduction in hydrostatic pressure using saline inserted anally through foleys
2. Copes Method - Laprascopic milking to reduce intussusception adn removal of gangrenous tissue + Appendicectomy
3. Resection
