Collection of excess serous fluid within the Processus Vaginalis usually the tunica

## Etiology
May be *Congenital* or *Acquired*. 
### Congenital Hydrocele
Occur in totally or partially patent Tunica Vaginalis
1. Vaginal
2. Infantile
3. True Congenital
4. Encysted hydrocele of cord
5. Hydrocele en Bissac
6. Hydrocele of canal of Nuck

### Acquired Hydrocele
May be Primary(idiopathic) or Secondary.
#### Primary Acquired Hydrocele
1. Defective absorption
2. Excess production
#### Secondary acquired Hydrocele
1. Filarial - Milky white fluid
2. TB - Cold abscess , "Craggy" Epididymis, "Beaded" Vas
3. Testicular tumours
4. Pyocele
5. Haematocele

## Clinical Features
1. Large, Soft, Cystic, *Fluctuating, Transilluminating*, Painless Swelling
2. Not reducible, No cough impulse. Possible to get over swelling

## Investigations
1. CBC (Routine and Eosinophilia)
2. USG Testis, Chest, Pre-para aortic lymphnodes
*// NO ASPIRATION. Causes spread if malignancy*

## Treatment
1. Lord Plication - Sac is plicated to tunica albuginea causing crumpling of vaginalis decreasing production capacity
2. Jaboulays Operation - Sac is everted and sutured behing the testis causing secretory cells to die off 
3. Sharma And Jhawers Operation - Testis is placed in a newly created pocket

## Complications
1. Pyocele, Hematocele
2. Rupture
3. Hernia
