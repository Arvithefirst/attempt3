Uncommon due to normal achoring of testis. Typically requires any of the following abnormalities
- Inversion
- High investment of tunica vaginalis
- Separation of epididymis

## Clinical features
- Sudden, intense pain associated with nausea, vomitting
- Redness
- Orchitis, Thickening of cord may be present
- Dysuria, UTI

## Investigations
1. CBC etc etc
2. Doppler

## Management
1. Within the first hour or so, gentle manipulation may be attempted
2. Surgical exploration and untwisting followed by fixing
3. Excision of infarcted testis, prosthesis
