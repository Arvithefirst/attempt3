defined as the *acute non-bacterial inflammatory condition caused by the actuvation, liberation and auto-digestion of the pancreas.*

## Etiology
1. *Alcohol  (40%)* - Causes obstruction due to stimulatin of protein release
2. *Biliary tract disorder (30%)*
3. Collagen vascular disorders
4. Drugs - Tetracycline, Corticosteroids, Oestrogen
5. Endoscopic procedures
6. Genetics
7. Hyperparatyroidism(hypercalcemia)
8. Hyperlipidemia/hypertriglyceridemiea
9. Hypothermia/Hypertension
10. Trauma
11. Infection - *Mumps,coxsackie*
12. Ductal
13. Post-operative
14. *Idiopathic (15%)*

### Pathophysiology
Autodigestion is usually the result of *Obstruction/Reflux/Sepsis* which causes conversion of Trypsinogen -> Trypsin which stimulates relase of 
1. Lipase
2. Elastase
3. Lysolecithinase
4. Phospholipase A

## Clinical features
### Symptoms
1. Severe epigastric pain radiating to back triggered by food *(relieved by bending forward // mohammedan prayer sign)*
2. Bluish discolouration of abdomen*
3. Vomiting 
4. Fever
5. Hematemesis / Malena
### Signs
1. Febrile, Cyanosis, Jaundice
2. Features of shock
3. Tender epigastrium with guarding/rigidity
4. Abdominal distension / Mass
>[!bug] Named signs
>*Gray turners sign* - Bluish discoloration of *flanks*
>*Cullens sign* - Bluish discoloration of *periiumbilicus*
>*Kochs sign* - Bluish discoloration of *inguinal region*

## Investigations
1. CBC, TLC, LFT, RFT
2. Serum Amylase
3. BSL
4. Serum calcium
5. X-ray abdomen - *Calcifications, sentinel loop sign, colon "cut-off" sign*
6. USG Abdomen
7. CeCT / Guided FNAC (Balthazar CT score)

Used top classsify by [[USMLE/Ranson Score\|Ranson Score]], [[Glasgow scale\|Glasgow scale]] or [[APACHE II score\|APACHE II score]]
