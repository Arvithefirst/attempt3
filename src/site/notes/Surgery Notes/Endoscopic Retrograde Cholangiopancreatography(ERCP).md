Under sedation and anesthesia dye is injected into the sphinter of oddi to visualise the biliary tree

## Indications
### Diagnostic Uses
1. Malignancy
2. Chronic pancreatitis
3. Congenital abnormality
4. Stones
5. Biliary tree stricture
6. Choledochal cyst
7. Sampling of bile/ pancreatic juice, brush biopsy for analysis

### Therapeutic uses
1. Retraction of stones
2. Nasobiliary drainage
3. Stenting of tumour
4. Dilation of biliary stricture
5. Endoscopic papillotomy

## Complications
- Pancreatitis
- Duodenal injury
- Cholangitis
- Bleeding

## Relative contraindications
- Acute pancreatitis
- h/o gastrectomy
