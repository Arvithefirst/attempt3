Goitre is defined as  *Diffuse enlargement of the thyroid gland*
### Euthyroid
1. Diffuse hyperplastic (Physiological, Pubertal, Pregnancy related)
2. [[Surgery Notes/Multinodular Goitre\|Multinodular Goitre]]
### Toxic
1. Diffuse([[Medicine notes/Graves Disease\|Graves Disease]])
2. Multinodular
3. Toxic adenoma
### Neoplastic
1. Benign
2. Malignant
### Inflammatory
Autoimmune
1. Hashimotos
2. Chronic lymphocytic thyroiditis
Granulomatous
1. De quervains thyroiditis
Fibrosing
1. Reidels Thyroiditis
Infectious
1. Acute (Bacterial, viral, "subacute")
2. Chronic (Tubercular, Syphillitic)
Other
1. Amyloid
