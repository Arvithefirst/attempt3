>[!quote] Courvoisiers law
>In a patient with jaundice, if there is palpable gallblaadder it is not due to stones

This is because in obstruction due to CBD stones the *gallbladder does not distend*. Rather it is fibrotic and contracted due to chronic inflammation. The palpable mass may *instead be a malignancy*
