*500-1500 shock waves* are sent thorugh a *double J stent* to crush the stone and allow it to pass.

- [ ] Painless and done without incision
- [ ] Expensive and requires equipment

>[!faq] Steinstrasse ("Stone street")
>A condition seen after ESWL that occurs when the stone fragments obstruct the distal ureter
