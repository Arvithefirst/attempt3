*asymptomatic, typically solitary, cholestero*l stones of the gallbladder. Often discovered accidentally on USG. They need not be treated unless
1. Diabetic/Immunocompromised patient
2. High risk of malignancy
3. Multople stones or stone >2.5cm
4. Thickened gall bladder wall
