*Functional obstruction at the level of LES due irregular parasympathetic activity*. It is a precancerous condition

## Aetiopathology
Caused by thickening of circular muscle fibres and myenteric ganglion cell inflammation
1. Idiopathic
2. Stress
3. Thiamine deficiency
4. Chagas disease

## Clinical Features
Commonly seen in *females* and in *ages 20 - 40* 

Clinical Triad of Achalasia Cardia
>1. Progressive dysphagia which is worse for liquids
>2. Regurgitation
>3. Weight loss

>[!faq] Staging of Achalasia Cardia (Based of proximal dilation)
>1. $x < 4cm$
>2. $4cm < x < 7cm$
>3. $x > 7cm$

## Investigations
1. Barium swallow shows
	- Bird Beak appearance (narrowing of LES)
	- Dilation of proximal oesophagus
	- Absense of Fundic gas bubbble
	- Sigmoid oesophagus
2. Chest X-ray shows signs of pneumonia
3. Oesophagal Manometry *Gold Standard Test*
4. Oesophagoscopy


## Complications
1. Recurrent pneumonia
2. Malnutrition
3. Lung abscess formation

## Treatment  
Medical management is with *Endoscopic botulinum* but recurrence is high. *Sublingual GTN* can also be given. Surgical interentions are
1. Modified Hellers operation - Laproscopic cuts are made on the circular fibres
2. Negus Hydrostatic dilation (endotrach. balloon)
3. Laproscopic cardiomyotomy
