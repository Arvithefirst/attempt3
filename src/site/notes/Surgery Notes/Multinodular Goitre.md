End stage diffuse hyperplastic goitre.

## Aetiopathogensis
Common with excessive metabolic demand (Women, Puberty, Pregnancy) Can arise from the following physiological goitres
1. Puberty/Pregnancy goitre
2. Iodine deficiency Goitre
3. Goitrogens
4. Dyshormonogenesis
>[!faq] Stages of goitre function
>Stage of hyperplasia and Hypertrophy
>Stage of TSH fluctuation
>Stage of nodule formation

## Clinical features
1. Mostly females mostly in 20-40 year range
2. Long history of nodular, firm swelling in front of neck. Dyspnea, Dysphagia
3. Sudden increase in size or pain is indicative of intrenodular. hemorrhage
4. Features of Hyperthyroidism
>[!summary] Clinical examination of Multinodular goitre
>*PIzzilos method* - Hands behind neck asked to swallow 
>*Laheys method* - Push with one hand feel wit hother
>*Criles method* - Thumb one
>*Kochers test* - Stridor on pressing (scabbard trachea)
>*Berry's sign* - Carotid pulsations shifted
>*Pembertons sign* - Hands up face turns red +ve for retrosternal extension causing Thoracic outlet syndrome

## Compliucations
1. Calcifications
2. Sudden hemorrhage
3. Secondary thyrotoxicosis with CVS involvement (Plummer's Disease)
4. Follicular carcinoma

## Investigations
1. CBC, TFT
2. Xray neck 
3. Laryngoscopy with flexible laryngoscope
4. USG neck
5. FNAC (USG guided)
6. CT

## Treatment
1. Thyroxine
2. *Subtotal thyroidectomy*  - leaves behind little tissue in tracheo-oesophageal groove to protect recurrent laryngeal nerve and parathyroid)
3. *Total thyroidectomy* - No chance of recurrence
4. *Dunhill procedure* - Preserves some tissue to avoid endocrone disorder
