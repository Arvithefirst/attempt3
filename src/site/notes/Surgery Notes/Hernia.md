> [!Quote] Definition
> An<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;"> abnormal protrusion of viscera</mark> or parts of viscera <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">through an opening</mark>, artificial or natural, <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">along with the sac</mark> covering it
{ #b8ce0e}


# Aetiology
1. Increased strain
	1. <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Heavy lifting</mark>
	2. Chronic cough
	3. Constipation
	4. Urinary problems (Stricture, BPH)
2. Weakened Abdominal wall
	1. Obesity
	2. Pregnancy
	3. Ascitis
	4. Smoking
	5. <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Previous Surgery</mark>
	6. Familial collagen disorders

# Classification
- Clinical
	1. Reducible
	2. Irreducible
	3. Obstructed
	4. Inflamed
	5. Strangulated
- Congenital / Acquired
- According to contents
	1. Omentocele
	2. Enterocele
	3. Cystocele
	4. Meckels diverticulum
- Based on site
	1. [[Surgery Notes/Inguinal Hernia\|Inguinal Hernia]]
	2. Femoral
	3. Obturator
	4. Diaphrammatic
	5. Umbilical
	6. Epigastric
