## Treatment
>[!summary] Surgery is only indicated in the case of
>1. Malignant nodule
>2. Follicular neoplasm
>3. Toxic Nodule in young
>4. Symptoms of obstruction
>5. Recurrent cystic nodule
>6. Cosmetic

1. Iodine radiotherapy (in toxic nodule)
2. Hemi-Thyroidectomy (in non-toxic)
3. Subotal thyroidectomy (In papillary carcinoma)
4. Total thyroidectomy (in follicular carcinoma)
