ALso called malignant oedema. Seen in infections *caused by gas-producing(Clostridium spp.)* organisms 

## Aetiopathogenesis
Clostridium produce the following toxins responsible for pathological features
1. Alpha, Beta, Epsilon, Iota, Phi, Kappa Toxins
2. Lecithinase - Causes extensive myositis
3. Hemolysin - Causes extensive hemolysis
4. Hyaluronidase - Helps in spread
5. Proteinase

Exotoxins causes necrosis of infected tissue and produce gas within the wound. Infection of the liver causes *"Frothy liver"*

## Clinical features
1. Fever, tachycardia, Pallor, toxaemia
2. Wound is under tensio with *"sickly sweet smelling"* discharge
3. *Khaki brown* overlying skin due to hemolysis
4. Crepitus *felt* over wound
5. Jaundice, oliguria

>[!faq] Clinical types of gas gangrene based on presentation
	1. Fulminant
	2. Massive(whole limb)
	3. Group(whole compartment)
	4. Single muscle
	5. Subcutaneous

## Investigations
1. Xray shows gas formation
2. LFT, Urea, Creatinine, PO2
3. CT scan

>[!info] Prophylaxis of gas gangrene
	1. Proper debriding of crush wounds and delayed suturing
	2. Peroxide cleaning
	3. Prophylactic penicillin
## Treatment
1. Benzyl penicillin
2. Fresh blood treansfusion
3. Polyvalent anti-serum
4. Rehydration, electrolyte balance, Hyperbaric oxygen
5. Prper debridement of wounds
6. Amputation (withot closing stump)
