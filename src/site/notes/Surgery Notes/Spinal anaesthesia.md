Injection of local anaesthetic into the subarachnoid space to cause loss of sympathetic tone/sensation/motor loss

Sympathetic block is 3 segments above Sensory block which is 3 segments above motor block

### Drugs used
1. Lignocaine 5% in 6% dextrose 2ml
2. Bupivacaine 0.5% in 5% dextrose 3ml

## Advantages
1. Cheap
2. Hypotensive
3. Less respiratory complications

## Disadvantages/Complications
1. CSF leak
2. [[Medicine notes/Meningitis#^c0695d\|Meningism]]
3. Infection
4. Paraplegia

## Contraindications
1. Caridac patient
2. High ICP
3. Sepsis
4. Spinal disease/kyphosis
