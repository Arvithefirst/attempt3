Persistantly recurring limb pain for 2 weeks or ulceration/[[Surgery Notes/Gangrene\|gangrene]] of foot/toes with ankle systollic bp <50mmHg or Toe systolic BP <30 mmHg

## Fontaines classification for limb inchaemia
Stage 1 - No Clinical pain
Stage 2 - Intermittent [[Surgery Notes/Claudication\|Claudication]]
- Stage 2a - Well compensated
- Stage 2b - Poorly Compensated
Stage 3 - [[Surgery Notes/Rest Pain\|Rest Pain]]
Stage 4 - [[Surgery Notes/Gangrene\|gangrene]], Ischaemic [[Surgery Notes/Ulcer\|ulcer]]
