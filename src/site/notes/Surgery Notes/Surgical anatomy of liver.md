Divided into 4 anatomical lobes (right, left, caudate, quadrate) abd supported by 3 ligaments (right/left triangular and falciform)

### Blood supply
80% by portal vein(tributary of Sup.mesenteric+splenic) + 20% by hepatic artery(Branch from celiac trunk)

Drained by right/left/middle hepatic veins which drain into IVC

### Segmental anatomy
- Falciform ligament divides liver into "functional left/right lobes
- Further divided into segments
