## Pathophysiology
Defect in BTK tyrosine kinase gene. Impaired B-cell Activation
1. Absent B-cells
2. Decreased [[Antibodies\|Immunoglobulins]] of all classes
3. Absent/scanty tonsils/lymph nodes

## Clinical Features
1. Recurrent bacterial and enteroviral infections
2. After 6 months og age (breastfeeding)
