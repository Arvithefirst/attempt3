# Vitamin K
Includes Phytomenadine, Phylloquinone, Phytonadione, Menaquinone. Synthesised by gut microbbiome

## Function
Reduced by Epoxide reductase into a cofactor for $\gamma$-carboxylation of glutamic residues on *proteins required for clotting factors* II, VII, IX, X, Proteins C and S

## Deficiency
Seen typically in newbornes,prolonged hepatocellular disease, or prolonged use of broad-spectrum antibiotics
1. Neonatal hemorrhage
2. &uarr; PT and &arr; aPTT with normal bleeding time
