# Riboflavin
## Vitamin $B_2$ (Riboflavin)
Component of FAD and FMN cofactors in redox reactions in [[TCA cycle\|TCA cycle]]

#### Deficiency
1. Cheilosis
2. Magenta tongue
3. Corneal vascularisation
