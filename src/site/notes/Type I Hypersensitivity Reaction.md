Type I is anaphylactic 

## Pathophysiology
First exposure sensitises mast cells against antigen. Following exposures results in anaphylaxis

Has 2 phases
- *Immediate (minutes):* pre-formed [[Antibodies#IgE\|IgE]] crosslinks Antigen to Mast cells -> Degranulation of Histamine, Tryptase, Leukotrienes
- *Late (hours)*: [[Interleukins\|Chemokines]] attract eosinophils and other mediators causing tissue damage

## Examples
- Allergic reaction to food/drugs/Bee stings
- Allergic asthma
