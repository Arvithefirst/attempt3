# Menkes Disease

## Pathophysiology
1. Defective ATP7A Protein
2. &darr; Lysyl oxidase activity (see [[Collagen#Synthesis\|Collagen#Synthesis]])
3. Defective cross-linking


## Clinical Features
1. Brittle kinky hair
2. Growth and developmental hair
3. Hypotonia
4. Cerebral aneurysms
