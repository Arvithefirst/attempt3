# Von Gierke Disease
Type I [[Glycogen Metabolism\|Glycogen Storage Disorders]]

$$ \text{Deficiency of Glucose-6-Phosphatase} $$
## Clinical Features
1. &uarr; Glycogen in liver, adn kidneys
2. &uarr; Lactate, Triglycerides, Uric acid(gout)
3. Hepatomegaly, Renomegaly

## Treatment
1. Frequent oral glucose, Avoid fructose/galactose
