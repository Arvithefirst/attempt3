Enveloped, Linear, dsDNA viruses

## Pathophysiology (Just Random Facts Actually)
Spreads through respiratory secretions, also known as *"Kissing disease" or Mononucleosis*
1. Infects CD-21 on B-cells
2. Reactive cytotoxic T-cells seen as abnormal lymphocytes on Peripheral smear
3. Positive Monospot test

## Clinical Features
1. Fever
2. *Hepatosplenomegaly*
3. Pharyngitis*
4. Lymphadenopathy
//* Use of Amoxicillin for presumed strep may cause maculopapular rash

>[!info] Associated with
>1. Splenic laceration during contact sports
>2. [[Burkitt Lymphoma\|Burkitt Lymphoma]]
>3. Nasopharyngeal carcinoma
>4. Lymphoproliferative disease in transplant patients
