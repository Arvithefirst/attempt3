May be familial or sporadic 

## Pathophysiology
Most common promary immunodeficiency. May be secondary to some diseases
- &darr; [[Antibodies#IgA\|IgA]], Normal [[Antibodies#IgG\|IgG]] and [[Antibodies#IgM\|IgM]] 
- &uarr; susceptibility to giardiasis


## Clinical Features
1. Majorly asymptomatic
2. Airway/GIT infection
3. Atopy, [[Type I Hypersensitivity Reaction\|Anaphylaxis]] to blood products
