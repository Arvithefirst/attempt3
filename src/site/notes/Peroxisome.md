# Peroxisome
Membrane-enclosed organelle

## Functions
1. $\beta$-oxidation of very long chain fatty acids (VLCFA) 
2. $\alpha$-oxidation of branched chain fatty acids
3. Catabolism of amino acids and ethanol
4. Synthesis of bile acids and plasmalogens

#### Disorders
1. [[Zellweger Syndrome\|Zellweger Syndrome]]
2. Refsum disease
3. Adrenoleukodystrophy
