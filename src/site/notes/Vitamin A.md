# Vitamin A
Vitamin A includes *Retinol*, Retinal and retinoic acid. Found in liver and leafy vegetables

## Functions
1. Part of visual pigments
2. Essential for normal differentiation of epithelial tissue
3. Anti-oxidant

## Deficiency
1. *Night blindness*
2. *Bitot spots*, dry eyes, corneal degeneration
3. Dry skin
4. Immunosuppression

## Excess
1. Acute toxicity -> Nausea, vomitting, &uarr; ICP
2. Chronic toxicity -> Alopecia, hepatotoxic, arthralgias

#### Clinical Uses
1. Supplemented 100,000 IU at 6 months and 200,000IU every 6 months after
2. Improves measles outcomes
3. Oral ir=soretinoin treats severe cystic acne
4. all-trans retinoic acid treats Acute promyelocytic leukemia
