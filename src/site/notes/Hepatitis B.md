A circular dsDNA Hepednavirus
1. Spread through Blood, Bedroom, Birth
2. Carries risk of [[Hepatocellular carcinoma\|Hepatocellular carcinoma]]

## Clinical Features
1. Initial serum sickness-like symptoms (non-specific fever arthralgia rash)
2. Granular eosinophillic *ground glass appearance* on H&E stain due to accumulated $HB_SAg$
#### Extrahepatic Manifestations
- Aplastic anemia
- [[Membranous Glomerulonephropathy\|Membranous Glomerulonephropathy]], [[Membranoproliferative Glomerulonephropathy\|Membranoproliferative Glomerulonephropathy]]
- Polyarthritis nodosa
