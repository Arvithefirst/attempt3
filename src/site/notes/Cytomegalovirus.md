Enveloped, Linear, dsDNA viruses. Part of [[TORCH infections\|TORCH infections]]

## Pathophysiology
Spreads by shared fluids, causes *"Mononucleosis-like" disesase*. 
1. Intracellular "Owl-eye" inclusion bodies in peripheral smear
2. Latent in mononuclear cells
3. Negative monospot test

## Clinical Features
More common in immunocompromised patients
1. Pneumonia
2. Esophagitis, Colitis
3. AIDS Retinitis - Cotton wool spots, hemorrhage, vision loss
