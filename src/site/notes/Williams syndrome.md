# Williams Syndrome
Congenital deletion of long arm of chromosome 7(includes [[Elastin\|Elastin]] gene)

## Clinical Features
1. *Elfin facies*
2. Intellectual disabilities
3. Hypercalcemia
4. Well developed verbal skills, Extreme friendliness with strangers
5. Supravalvular aortic stenosis, pulmonary artery stenosis, renal artery stenosis
