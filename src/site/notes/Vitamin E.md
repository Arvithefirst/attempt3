# Vitamin E
Antioxidant 

## Deficiency
Looks like [[Cobalamin\|Cobalamin]] deficiency without Megaloblasts or hypersegmented PMNs or serum markers. Closely mimics [[Friedreich Ataxia\|Friedreich Ataxia]]
1. Hemolytic anemia, Acanthocytosis
2. Muscle weakness
3. Demyelination of posterior columns and spinocerebellar tract

## Excess
1. May cause enterocolitis
2. High doses affects [[Vitamin K\|Vitamin K]]-dependant coagulation factors enhancing coagulation
