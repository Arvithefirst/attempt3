## Drugs
#### 1. Calcineurin Inhibitors
- Cyclosporine/Tacrolimus
- Inhibits Calcineurin which promotes [[Interleukins#IL-2\|IL-2]] transcription
- Decreased [[Interleukins#IL-2\|IL-2]] -> Decreased proliferation of [[T-cells\|T-cells]]
