# Down Syndrome
Most common cause of genetic intellectual disability. Most common viable chromosomal disorder

## Pathophysiology
Trisomy of chromosome 21
1. 95% due to Meiotic nondisjunction in Meiosis I (&uarr; in advanced maternal age X60 times)
2. 4% due to unbalanced Robertsonian translocation between 14 and 21
3. 1% due to postfertilisation mitotic error

## Investigations
First trimester shows
1. Increased nuchal tranlucency, hypoplastic nasal bone
2. Increased hCG and inhibin. Decreased PAPP-A

Second Trimester
1. Decreased Estriol and AFP
2. Umbilical hernua
[[Trimester screening for Trisomies\|Trimester screening for Trisomies]]

## Clinical Features
1. Intellectual disability
2. Flat facies, Prominent epicanthal folds, Low set ears, short neck
3. Upslanting palpebral fissures, Brushfield spots in iris
4. Single palmar crease, Incurved 5th finger, saddle toe deformity
5. Duodenal Atresia, [[Hirschsprung disease\|Hirschsprung disease]]
6. Congenital heart disease
7. Early onset Alzheimers
8. &uarr; risk of AML/ALL
