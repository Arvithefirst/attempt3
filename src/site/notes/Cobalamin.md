# Cobalamin
## Vitamin $B_{12}$ (Cobalamin)
Cofactor for
- Methionine synthase as Methylcobalamin which provides 1-carbon units for DNA methylation
- Methylmalonyl-CoA mutase in odd chain fatty acid breakdown
### Methylation Cycle and Fatty Acid Oxidation
![Screenshot 2026-09-04 at 12.44.45 PM.png](/img/user/Screenshot%202026-09-04%20at%2012.44.45%20PM.png)

## Deficiency
Very large stores are held in the liver (several years)
### Aetiology
1. Malabsorption syndromes (Sprue, enteritis, Bacterial overgrowth, Alcohol overuse)
2. Chronic Pancreatitis
3. Lack on Intrinsic factor ([[Pernicious anemia\|Pernicious anemia]], Gastric bypass surgery)
4. Absence of terminal ileum (Surgical resection, [[Crohns disease\|Crohns disease]])
5. Certain drugs (Metformin)
6. Vegetarian diet

## Clinical Features
Caused by abnormal myelin in CNS
1. Macrocytic, Megaloblastic anemia
2. Hypersegmented PMNs
3. Paresthesias
4. *Subacute Combined Degeneration*
