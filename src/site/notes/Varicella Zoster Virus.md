Enveloped, Linear, dsDNA viruses

Latent in dorsal root or trigeminal ganglia. Involvement of $CN\space V_1$ can cause *Herpes Zoster Ophthalmicus*. Most commonly complicates into herpetic neuralgia

### Clinical Features
1. Shingles(D)/Chickenpox(E)
2. Encephalitis
3. Pneumonia
