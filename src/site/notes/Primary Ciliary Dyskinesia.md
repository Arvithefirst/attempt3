# Primary Ciliary Dyskinesia
Broad spectrum of diseases including [[Kartagener Syndrome\|Kartagener Syndrome]]

## Pathophysiology
1. Defective dynein arm
2. Immotile cilia
3. Developmental abnormalities

## Clinical Features
1. Situs inversus
2. Hearing loss
3. Recurrent infections
4. Bronchiectasis
5. Infertility, increased chances of ectopic
