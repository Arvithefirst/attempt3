# G6PD Deficiency
Most common human enzyme deficiency, more prevalent in *Malaria-endemic regions* (cool reason)

## Pathophysiology
- Decreased availability of G6PD enzyme leads to inability to form NADPH from [[HMP Shunt\|HMP Shunt]]
- &darr; NADPH leads to reduced action of Glutathione reductase which regenerates glutathione peroxidase to combat Oxygen free radicals in RBC
- Poor RBC defence against oxidation -> Hemolytic anemia
![Screenshot 2026-09-04 at 11.43.01 PM.png](/img/user/Screenshot%202026-09-04%20at%2011.43.01%20PM.png)

## Clinical Features
Hemolytic anemia precipitated by oxidative stress of
- Infections
- Fava beans, sulfonamides, nitrofurantoin, *Primaquine*
- *Heinz bodies* (Denatured globin chains due to oxidative stress)
- *Bite cells* (Phagocytic removal of heinz bodies)
