# Hardy-Weinberg Principle
Formula for expected frequency of Alleles p and q assuming that 
- No mutations occur at the locus
- Natural selection is not occurring
- Completely random mating
- No net migration
- Large population

$$ p^2 + 2pq + q^2 =1 $$
$$ AND $$
$$p + q = 1$$

Where
$P^2$ = Frequency of Homozygote allele P
$2pq$ = Frequency of Heterozygote pq
$q^2$ = Frequency of Homozygote allele q
