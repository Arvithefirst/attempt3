# Angelman Syndrome
Mutation or Deletion of Maternal UBE3A genes on chromosome 15 while paternal copy is silenced(normal)

## Clinical Features
1. Hand flapping, Ataxia
2. Intellectual disability
3. Inappropriate laughter
4. Seizures
