# Osteogenesis Imperfecta

Genetic bone disorder caused by mutations in the COL1A1 and COL2A2 genes resulting in decreased production of [[Collagen#Type I Collagen\|Collagen#Type I Collagen]]

## Clinical Features
May be confused with an abused child
1. Multiple fractures and bone deformities after minimal trauma
2. Blue sclerae
3. Tooth abnormalities due to lack of dentin
4. Hearing loss

## Treatment
1. Biphosphonates
