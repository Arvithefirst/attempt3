*Antibody mediated Cytotoxic reactions* 

## Pathophysiology
Autoantibodies or transplanted antibodies against self cell-surface antigens *opsonise* their targets leading to 3 outcomes
1. Cellular destruction (Via Phagocytosis, NK cells, or [[Complement\|Complement]] activation)
	- Autoimmune Hemolytic Anemia
	- Immune thrombocytopenia
	- Transfusion reactions 
	- Hemolytic disease of Newborn
2. Inflammation (via activation of [[Complement\|Complement]] system and Fc receptor activation)
	- [[Goodpasture Syndrome\|Goodpasture Syndrome]]
	- [[Rheumatic fever\|Rheumatic fever]]
	- [[Transplant Rejection Reactions\|Hyperacute and Chronic transplant reactions]]
3. Cellular dysfunction 
	- [[Myasthenia Gravis\|Myasthenia Gravis]]
	- [[Graves disease\|Graves disease]]
	- Pemphigus Vulgaris
