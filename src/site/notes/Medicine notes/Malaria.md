Plasmodia parasite infection

## Aetiology
*Agent* - P. falciparum, P. vivax, P. ovale, P. malariae spread by Female Anopheles mosquito - Rests indoors, bites outdoors usually early morning hours
*Host* - Man. May be protected by HbS trait, G6PD deficiency, Thalassemia trait
*Environment* - Temperate climates(SEA, Africa)

## Clinical features
1. Fever - Quaternary(Pf), Tertiary(Pm), Alternate days(Pv, Po)
2. Malaise, Rigor, Cold/Hot flush phases, Vomitting
3. Tachycardia, Anemia, Jaundice
4. Splenomegaly

## Investiagtions
1. Routines
2. Giemsa stained Thick and thin smears
3. Immunochromatographic Rapid Diagnostic tests (OptiMal, ParaSight-F)
4. Quantitative Buffy Coat
5. DNA PCR tests

## Treatment
Artemisinin Combination Therapy(ACT)
#### Mild
Artemether + Lumefantrine(preferred) OR
Quinine + Doxycycline OR
Atovaquone + Proguanil
#### Severe
Artesunate(preferred) OR
Quinine + Doxycycline 
*// Avoid chloroquine in G6PD deficiency*
