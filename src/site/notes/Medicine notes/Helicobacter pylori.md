Curved Gram -ve bacilli associated with [[Medicine notes/Peptic Ulcer disease\|Peptic Ulcer disease]] and Gastric carcinoma

## Microbiological factors
H.pylori is uniquely adapted to the gastric environment due to certain *features allowing colonisation of gastric mucosa* and
- High mobiliity due conferred by 4-8 unipolar flagella
- Acid-resistance due to urease breaking down urea to ammonia in its immediate surroundings
- Adhesins(BabA)
- Innate resistance to oxidative stress
Other *features inducing pathological change*
- Vacuolating Cytotoxin (VacA)
- Cytotoxin-associated gene A(CagA)
- Bacterial Lipopolysaccharide shows Molecular mimicry with the Lewis blood group antigen on Gastric parietal cells
- LPS helps intereferes with gastric mucus production

>[!quote] Protective role of H.pylori
>Infection has an inverse realation with diseases caused by hyper-acidity
>- GERD
>- Barretts oesophagus
>- Adenocarcinoma oesophagus

## Laboratory Diagnosis
1. Urea breath test - labelled urea is broken down to labelled CO2 by urease enzyme(if present) and expired
2. Fecal Antigen assay
3. IgG antibody detection
4. Histopathological staining with Warthin starry silver
5. Blood-agar culture, anaerobic culture
6. Biopsy urease test

## Eradication Therapy
Done with short term(10-14 days) intensive use of PPI + Antibiotic + H$_2$ receptor antagonists
