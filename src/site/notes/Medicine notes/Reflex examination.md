1. Corneal (Trigeminal, Facial)
2. Conjuctival (Trigeminal, Facial)
3. Pharyngeal (Glossopharyngeal, Vagus)
4. Palatal (Trigemnial, Vagus)
5. Abdominal (Dermatome related)
6. Cremasteric (Femoral, Genitofemoral)
7. Plantar (Tibial, Tibial)

1. Jaw jerk - Trigeminal
2. Bicep - C5, C6
3. Supinator - C5, C6
4. Tricep - C7, C8 
5. Knee - L2, L3, L4
6. Ankle - S1
