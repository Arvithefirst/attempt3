Clinical syndrome characterised by loss of previously acquired intellectual function

## Types
1. Vascular
	1. Diffuse small vessel disease
	2. Amyloid angiopathy
	3. [[Medicine notes/Systemic Lupus Erythematosus\|Systemic Lupus Erythematosus]]
2. Inherited
	1. [[Medicine notes/Alzheimers Disease\|Alzheimers Disease]]
	2. Huntingtons disease
	3. Wilsons disease
	4. Lewy Body dementia
3. Neoplastic
4. Inflammatory
	1. Multiple sclerosis
	2. Sarcoidosis
5. Traumatic
6. Toxic/Nutritional
	1. Alcohol abuse
	2. Thiamin dificiency, B12 deficiency
	3. heavy metal / Carbon monoxide poisoning
7. Prion disease
