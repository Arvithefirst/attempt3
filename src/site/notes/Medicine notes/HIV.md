Human Immunodeficiency Virus is an STD we got from Monkeys :) 

## Etiology
AIDS can be caused by
1. HIV - 1
	1. Group M(Major)
		1. Subtypes A-K (mainly C in India, B in Europe)
	2. Group O(Outlier)
	3. Group N(Non-major, Non-outlier)
2. HIV - 2

## Pathogenesis
HIV ssRNA is surrounded by a lipid membrane and contains
1. env genes - Envelope proteins - gp 120, gp41
2. gag genes - Structural proteins - p24
3. pol genes - Reverse Transcriptase, Integrase, Protease
plus 6 regulatory genes

#### Events
1. *gp120* attatches to CD4 cells and binds to CCR5
2. *gp41* responsible for insertion of virion
3. *Reverse Transcriptase* turns ssRNA to dsDNA
4. *Integrase* integrates dsDNA into CD4 cell nucleus creating proviral DNA
5. CD4 T-helper cell creates about $10^{10}$ virions before being killed off by immune system
6. *Protease* cleaves polypeptides and allows viral release

In response to Primary HIV infection there is a dramatic B-cell Immune response resulting in reversal of CD8:CD4 ratio. This kills of actively replicating infected cells but fails to reach latently infected cells. Over time CD4 cell counts fall low enough to allow opportunistic infections leading to death

## Clinical features
Staged by WHO(for poors) or CDC(For rich-ards)
Disesase has 2-4 weeks of incubation followed by upto 2 weeks of primary infection followed by median 9 years of latency
### WHO stage 1 / CDC Category A
- *(CDC only)* Primary HIV Infection
- Asymptomatic
- Generalised Lymphadenopathy

### WHO Stage 2 / CDC Category B
- Unexplained weight loss <10%
- Recurrent URTI
- [[Medicine notes/Herpes Zoster\|Herpes Zoster]], Angular cheilitis, Papular pruritic eruptions, Seborrhoeic dermatitis
- Recurrent Oral Ulcers
- Fungal nail infections

### WHO Stage 3 / Also CDC Category B
- Unexplained weight loss >10%, Unexplained fever / [[Medicine notes/Chronic Diarrhoea\|diarrhoea]] >1month
- Persistant oral candidiasis, Hairy Oral Leukoplakia
- Pulmonary TB, Severe bacterial infections
- Acute necrotising mucosal ulcers
- Unexplained Anemia, Neutropenia,Thrombocytopenia
- *(CDC only)* PID, Cervical dysplasia, Vaginosis
- *(CDC only)* Peripheral neuropathy

### WHO Stage 4 / CDC Category C
- Respiratory system Candidiasis
- Cryptococcus, Cryptosporidiosis, other nosocomial mycosis
- Cytomegalovirus(encephalitis and retinopathy), Herpes Simplex (see [[Medicine notes/Herpes Family Tree <3\|Herpes Family Tree <3]])
- Kaposi Sarcoma, [[OBGY Notes/Carcinoma Cervix\|Cervical carcinoma]], Lymphoma
- TB, Non-TB Mycobacterial infection
- Recurrent Bacterial Pneumonia, Toxoplasmosis, Sepsis
- HIV Wasting syndrome, HIV Encephalopathy

May also have
- Hepatitis B/C (needle sharing)
- Cholangiopathy (Due to CMV or endogenous bacteria)
- [[Medicine notes/Acute Kidney Injury\|Acute Kidney Injury]] (Due to drugs)
- Cardiomyopathy
- [[Medicine notes/Stroke\|Stroke]]
- Peripheral neuropathy
- Rheumatic disease
- Psychiatric disorders
- HIV-associated neurocognitive disorders(HAND)

## Investigations
#### For diagnosis
1. p24 screening
2. Antibody testing, Western blot
3. ELISA
4. HIV RNA PCR
#### After diagnosis
1. CBC, LFT, RFT
3. HbsAG, Hep B, Syphillis, Cervical smear, STD screening, PPD
4. *CD4 count (>500 is normal, <200 is Severe AIDS)*
5. Viral load

## Management
### Prevention of opportunistic infections
- Minimise risk through food/water safety, Vector control, safe sex, Pet testing
- Prevent common nosocomial infections with Co-trimoxazole
- ATT if necessary
- Cryptococcus prophylaxis if CD4 count <100
- Vaccinate for Hep A, HPV, Chickenpox, DTaP, MMR, IPV

### Antiretroviral Therapy
Should be started immediately after confirmation of diagnosis. *Typically 2 NRTIs + 1 NNRTI/PI/ Integrase inhibitor*

*Nucleotide Reverse Transcriptase Inhibitor(NRTI) 1* - Abacavir, Emtricitabine

*Nucleotide Reverse Transcriptase Inhibitor(NRTI) 2*-Lamuvidine,Tenofovir,Zidovudine

*Non-Nucleotide Reverse Transcriptase Inhibitor(NNRTI*) - Efavirenz

*Protease Inhibitors(PIs)* - Atazanavir, Darunavir (taken with ritonavir)

*Integrase inhibtor* - Raltegravir, dolutegravir

See - [[Medicine notes/HIV Drugs for losers\|HIV Drugs for losers]]
#### Complications of ART
1. Immune reconstitution syndrome
2. Lipodystrophy
3. Hypersensitivity
4. Insomnia, Dysphoria, Kidney/Bone/Heart toxicity

#### Prophylaxis
- PrEP - Emtricitabine + Tenofovir
- PEP - Within 72 hours(6-8 ideally) Emtricitabine + Tenofovir + PI/Integrase inhibitor
