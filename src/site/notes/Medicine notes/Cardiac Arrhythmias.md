An abnormality in rate or rhythm of contraction of the heart

## Classification
1. [[Medicine papers/Tachyarrhythmias\|Tachyarrhythmias]]
	1. Supraventricular Tachycardia(SVT)
		1. [[Atrial Fibrillation\|Atrial Fibrillation]]
		2. [[Atrial flutter\|Atrial flutter]]
		3. [[Paroxysmal Supraventricular Tachycardia\|Paroxysmal Supraventricular Tachycardia]]
	2. Ventriclar Tachycardia
		1. Ventricular fibrillation
		2. Ventricular ectopic beats
		3. Brugada syndrome
		4. Torsades des pointes
2. Bradyarrhythmias
	1. AV block
	2. Bundle Branch Block

## Principles of management
Anti-arrhythmics are potentially toxic so they shoul de used with care
1. Many arrhythmias are benign
2. Causal factors should be corrected where possible
3. Electrophysiological study can aid treatment
4. Prophylactic treatment is preferred
5. Long term patients should be reviewed regularly for withdrawal
6. Surgical management may be preferrred

### Anti-arrhythmic drugs
There are 4 main classes based on mechanism of action. Therapy should be decided according to type of arrhythmia

#### Class I - Sodium channel blockers
 Subdivided into *Ia, Ib, Ic based on their ability to prolong, reduce or not affect action potential time* respectively. Avoided in [[Medicine notes/Cardiac failure\|Cardiac failure]]
	*Ia* -> Quinidine, disopyramide
	*Ib* -> Lidocaine
	*Ic* -> Propafenone
#### Class II - B-blockers
Used to *depress SA node function, decrease myocardial excitability*. Used in VT, SVT, to decrease chances of arrhythmic death in patients of [[Medicine notes/Acute Coronary Syndrome\|Acute Coronary Syndrome]] and [[Medicine notes/Cardiac failure\|Cardiac failure]]
	Non selective - Propanolol, carvedilol
	Cardioselective - Metoprolol, Bisoprolol
	Racemic mixture of the 2 - Sotalol *(causes Torsades des pointes)*
#### Class III - QT Prolonging drugs
Very potent at preventing tacharrhythmias. May predispose patients to VT and torsades des pointes
1. Amiodarone
2. Dronedarone
#### Class IV - Calcium channel Blocers
Prevention of SVT and rate control in AF by blocking AV node
1. Verapamil
2. Diltiazem

>[!faq] Other drugs used to manage arrhythmias
>*Atropine sulphate* - increases sinus rate at AV and SA nodes. Used for severe bradyarrhythmias
>*Adenosine* - Blocks AV node temporarily used to terminate SVT
>*Digoxin* - Slows conduction but may cause ectopic beats
