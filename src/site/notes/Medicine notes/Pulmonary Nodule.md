*Well or poorly defined rounded mass seen in a fully inflated lung*. Incidental pulmonoary nodules have become more cmmon since the advent of the CT
Typically benign unless
- >3cm
- Spiculated
- Upper lobe
- History and age suggestive of malignancy

## Causes
#### Common
1. Lung cancer
2. Tuberculoma
3. Single Metastasis
4. Localised Pneumonia
5. Pulmonary Infarct
6. Abscess

#### Uncommon
1. Benign tumour
2. Lymphoma 
3. AV malformtion
4. [[Surgery Notes/Hydatid cyst\|Hydatid cyst]]
5. Rheumatoid nodule
6. Hamartoma
7. Aspergilloma
8. Cryptococcus infection
