[[Medicine notes/Inflammatory Bowel Disease\|Inflammatory Bowel Disease]] more common in females, ashkenazi jews and smokers

## Pathophysiology
Typicallly involves (in order) *terminal ileum + rt colon > colon alone > terminal ileum alone > ileum + jejunum > peri-anal only*

- *Lead Pipe Appearance*
- *Cobblestone appearance* of mucosa (thickened, oedematous, ulcerated)
- *"Patchy" skip lesions* with intervening healthy tissue
- Transmural non-caseating granulomas

## Clinical features
The main symptoms(3) of Crohns are
1. Abdominal pain
2. Diarrhoea (watery, non-blod tinged)
3. Weight loss, anemia, malabsoption symptoms

// Physical examination may also reveal palpable bowel loops or perianal skin tags in 50% of cases

## Treatment
It is a progressive disease that may develop into fistulae if improperly treated. Therefore the goals of treatment are to 
- Induce remission
- Maintain remission with glucocorticoid therapy

#### Induction of remission
1. Budesonide(glucocorticoid) - 6mg OD x6weeks
2. Prednisolone *(if not responsive)*
3. Enteric nutrition *(alternative #1, more palatable for children)*
4. Anti-TNF drugs + Thiopurine(immunosuppresant) *(for severe cases)*

#### Maintenance threapy
1. Immunosuppresants *(mainstay)*
2. Methotrexate (*alternative*)
3. Vedolizumab (*integrin monoclonal antibody for non-responsive*)
4. STOP SMOKING MOTHEFUCKER
