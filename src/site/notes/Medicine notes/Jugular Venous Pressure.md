It is the reflection of the phasic changes in the Right Atrium

#### 'a' wave - Atrial contraction - increases JVP
Absent 'a' wave - Atrial Fibrillation
Prominent 'a' wave - Tricuspid/Pulmonary stenosis, Pulmonary hypertension
Cannon 'a' wave - Complete heart block, Multiple ectopics

#### 'c' wave - Closing of Tricuspid - slight bump in JVP
No known abnormalities

#### 'x' descent - Atrial filling - Steep decrease in JVP
Prominent x descent - Constrictive pericarditis

#### 'v' wave - Ventricular systole - JVP increases
Prominent v wave - Tricuspid regurgitation

#### 'y' descent - Ventricular filling - JVP falls
Absent 'y' descent - Cardiac tamponande
Slow 'y' descent - Tricuspid stenosis
Fast 'y' descent - Tricuspid regurgitation


## Causes of raised JVP
1. Cardiac

	2. Cardiac Tamponade

2. Cor pulmonale
3. Hepatic failure
4. Pregnancy
5. Iatrogenic - Excess iV fluids
