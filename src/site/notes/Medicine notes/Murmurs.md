Prolonged vibrations arising from turbulent blood flow in the heart due to constricted or irregular orifices. 

# Description
1. Systolic/diastolic
2. Grade
3. Area
4. Radiation
5. Pitch and quality

>[!faq] Grades of Murmur
>Grade 1 - Only audible in ideal conditions
>Grade 2 - Mild
>Grade 3 - moderate
>Grade 4 - Fluid Thrill felt
>Grade 5 - Very loud
>Grade 6 - Heard without Stethoscope
