<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Causes</mark>
1. Erythropoietin deficiency
2. Uremia toxicity on Marrow cells
3. Reduced RBC lifespan
4. Capillary fragility and impaired platelet function
5. Reduced intake, absorption and utilisation of dietary iron

<mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Management</mark>
1. Iron Supplement
2. Human recombinant Erythropoietin
