Autoimmune condition directed against platelet membrane glycoprotein IIb/IIIa 
#### Pathology
1. Glycoprotein IIb/IIIa is responsible for sensitisation of platelets
2. Autoantibodies are responsible for early removal of platelets

## Clinical features
Old age and Female preference. Usually associated with connective tissue disorders, [[Medicine notes/HIV\|HIV]], certain drugs, B-cell lymphomas. *Spontaneous bleeding occurs at platelet counts $>2*10^{10}$* 
1. Easy bruising
2. Epistaxis
3. [[OBGY Notes/Menorrhagia\|Menorrhagia]]

## Management
Required when platelet count $>3*10^{10}$ or when surgery
1. Glucocorticoids
2. IV immunoglobulins
3. Thrombopoietin Receptor Agonists
4. Splenectomy
5. [[USMLE/Immunosuppressants for losers\|Immunosuppressants]]
