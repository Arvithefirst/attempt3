Rare autoimmune connecctive tissue disorder. Most common in females 20-30

#### Pathophysiology
Poorly understood
- Strong genetic connection to mutations on HLA locus(chromosome 6)
- Autoantibody production with wide range of specificity
- May be due to defective apoptotis pathways. Supported by aggravation by environmental oxidative stress

## Clinical features
Fever, Weight loss, Lymphadenopathy
1. Arthritis(90%) - with early morning stiffness, Joccouds arthropathy
2. [[Surgery Notes/Raynauds Phenomenon\|Raynauds phenomenon]]
3. Classic Malar Rash - Raised, red, erythemous, over nasolabial folds
4. Discoid, hyperkeratinised rash which may cause scarring alopecia
5. Diffuse non-scarring alopecia
6. Urticarial eruptions, Livedo reticularis

#### Systemic complications
1. Progressive glomerulonephritis
2. Myocarditis / Libman sachs endocarditis
3. Serositis, Atelectasis, Pulmonary Fibrosis 
4. Headache, Hallucinations, Chorea, lymphatic meningitis
5. Hemolytic anemia, Neutropenia, Thrombocytopenia
6. Mouth ulcers, Hepatitis(rarely) 

## Investigations
1. CBC
2. ANA testing
3. Anti dsDNA antibody testing, 
>[!faq] American Rheumatology Association Criteria for Lupus
>Any 4 of the following 11
>1. Malar Rash
>2. Discoid Rash
>3. Photosensitivity
>4. Oral ulcers
>5. Arthritis
>6. Serositis
>7. Renal disorder - Persistant proteinuria or Cellular casts
>8. Haematological disorer - Haemolytic anemia, Leukopenia or thrombocytopenia
>9. Neurological disorder - Seizures or psychosis without metabolic cause
>10. Immunological disorder - Anti-DNA titres
>11. ANA titres

## Treatment
#### Mild to moderate disease
- NSAIDs + Hydroxychloroquine
- Glucocorticoids(Prednisolone) + Immunosuppresants(Methotrexate/Mycophenolate mofetil/Azathioprine)
- Monoclonal antibody - Belizumab

#### Severe disease 
- Methylprednisolone + Cyclophosphamide
- Mesna
- Rituximab
