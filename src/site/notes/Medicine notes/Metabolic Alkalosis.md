Increase in plasma HCO$_3$ 
- Primary change -> *HCO$_3$ rises* above 24mmol/L
- Compensatory change -> *PCO$_2$ rises* above 5.33kPA
$$ 𝚫PCO_2 = 𝚫HCO_3 * 0.08 $$
#### Causes
1. Primary hyperaldosteronsim
2. Cushings syndrome
3. Glucocorticiod therapy
4. Antacid overuse


## Management
1. Diagnose with Blood pH and Serum elecctrolytes
2. Treat with IV potassium
