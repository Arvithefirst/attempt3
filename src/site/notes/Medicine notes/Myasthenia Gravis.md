Most common cause of acutely evolving fatiguable .

## Pathology
1. *Autoantibodies to acetylcholine receptors*
2. May also be imitated by autoantibodies to Muscle Specific Kinase(MuSK)
3. 15% have thymoma
4. May be triggered by drugsh like penicillamine

## Clinical features
Seen in 15-50 years. Usually females. Shows characteristic weakness that gets worse with the day. 
1. Intermittent ptosis, Diplopia
2. Weakness chewing, swallowing, speaking.
3. Limb weakness, most commonly in shoulder girdle

## Investigations
1. Short acting Anticholinesterase (Edrophonium bromide) produces temporary relief (*Tensilon test)*
2. Repetitive nerve stimulation studies
3. Antibody detection tests
4. Thoracic CT

## Treatment
Physiostigmine/Neostigmine (Anticholinesterases) given
- Overdose produces "Cholinergic crisis" with similar symptoms to "Myesthenic crisis" differrentiate by *Tensilon test*

Immunological treatment options are
- Acute
	1. IV immunoglobulins
	2. Plasma exchange
- Chronic
	1. Glucocorticoids
	2. Immunosuppressants
	3. Thymectomy
