A psychiatric disorder chartacterised by delusions, hallucinations and lack of insight.

#### Aetiopathology
1. Strong genetic component attributed to copy number variations in C4 gene on chromosome 6
2. Associated with obstetric complications
3. Associated with decrease in brain size specifically in temporal lobe
4. Acute attack may be triggered y cannabis use

## Clinical features
Diagnosis is usually clinical but organic causes and other psychoses have to be ruled out
### First Rank symptoms
1. *A*uditory Hallucinations - typically voices "echo de la pense"
2. *B*roadcasting or insertion/withdrawal of thoughts
3. *C*ontrolled feelings or passivity of actions
4. *D*elusions - Bizarre interpretations of phenomena
#### Other symptoms
- Blunted affect
- Autism
- Alogia
- Poor self-care


## Treatment
Anti-psychoti therapy eg:
1. Haloperidol 8-10mg/day
2. Chlorpromazine - 500mg/day
3. Olanzapine - 10-15mg/day
4. Clozapine - 300-600mg/day
