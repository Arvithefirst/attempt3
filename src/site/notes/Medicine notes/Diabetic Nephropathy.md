Complication seen in 30% of all pateitns with [[Medicine notes/Diabetes Mellitus\|Diabetes Mellitus]] Accounts for 20-50% of all patients on [[Medicine notes/Renal Replacement Therapy\|Renal Replacement Therapy]]

#### Risk factors
- Race
- Weight 
- Glycemic control
- Smoking
#### Pathology
Pahtogenesis is not fully understood. Postulated to be toxic effects of hyperglycemia and prolonged activation of RAAS leading to 
- Microalbuminurea
- Thickening of glomerular membrane
- Characteristic nodular deposits - Kimmelstein-Wilson nodules

## Investingations
1. Microalbinurea screening by early morning Urine Albumin:Creatinine ratio
2. Urine Dipstick can only detect overt macroalbuminurea > 300

## Treatment
Aims to reduce BP, CVS risk factors and improve glycemic control
1. Salt restriction diet
2. ACEi's, ARB's
3. Aldosterone antagonist
4. [[Medicine notes/Renal Replacement Therapy\|Renal Replacement Therapy]]
