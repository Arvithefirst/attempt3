Occurs due accumulation of acids other than carbonic acid
- Primary change -> *HCO$_3$ falls* <24mmol/L
- Compensatory change -> *PCO$_2$ falls* <5.33kPa$^2$
$$ 𝚫PCO_2 = 𝚫HCO_3 * 0.16 $$

## Causes
Divided into based on the presence of anion gap
#### Normal anion gap
1. GIT loss -> Diarrhoea, Small bowel fistula
2. Renal Tubular Acidosis 
#### Increased Anion gap
1. Endogenous acid production - [[Medicine notes/Diabetic ketoacidosis\|Diabetic ketoacidosis]], Starvation, Alcoholism, Kidney disease([[Medicine notes/Acute Kidney Injury\|AKI]])
2. Exogenous acid load - Poisoning (Asprin, methanol, Ethylene glycol)

## Clinical features
History, Symptoms and features of underlying disease

## Investigations
1. Arterial Blood Gas
2. Serum Electrolytes
3. Kidney function tests
4. Serum Calcium
5. Blood Glucose levels

## Treatment
1. Identify and treat cause
2. Avoid rapid correction (induces [[Surgery Notes/Hypokalemia\|Hypokalemia]], hypocalcemia)
3. Sodium and Potassium supplementation
4. Diuretics, thiazides, etc if RTA
