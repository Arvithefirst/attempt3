Complication of DM II characterised. by 
- Severe hyperglycemia >600mg/dl
- Hyperosmolality >320 mOsm/kg
- Severe Dehydration

## Clinical features
Insidious onset 
- Dehydration
- CNS manifestations 
- Predisposition to Infarcts
- Infections

## Management
- Rehydration
- Insulin
- Potassium supplementation
- Bicarb
- Anticoagulant (prophylactic low molecular weight Heparin (LMWH))
- Antibiotics
