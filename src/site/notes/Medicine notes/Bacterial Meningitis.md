*Same as Pyogenic meningitis*. Usually part of bacteraemic illness but may also spread from local foci of infection

## Aetiopathogenesis
May be of the following causative agents
1. Gram -ve bacilli (E.coli, Proteus)
2. Neisseria meningitis (*Most common in West*)
3. Staphylococcus aureus
4. Streptococcus pneumoniae (*2nd Most common in India*)
5. Hemophilus Influenzae B (*Most common in India*)
6. Mycobacterium Tuberculosis

Infection causes inflammation and congestion of Pia-arachnoid membrane. Combined with formationof adhesions this causes *increased CSF pressure* leading to symptoms and possible damage to the cranial nerves. *S.pneumoniae is associated with purulent pus which may cause secondary brain infections*

## Clinical Features
Patients usually present with symptoms of [[Medicine notes/Meningitis#^c0695d\|Meningism]], altered consciousness and possible a rash. In pneumococcal or Haemophilus infection threre may be accompanying *otitis media*

## Complications
1. Shock
2. Renal Failure
3. Pancarditis
4. Arthritis
5. Peripheral [[Surgery Notes/Gangrene\|gangrene]]

## Investigations
1. CT brain to rule out Hydrocephalus/Cerebral edema where clinically suspected
2. Lumbar puncture (*only if no contraindications*)
	- Cloudy appearance
	- Many neutrophils
	- Significantly raised proteins, reduced glucose
	- Culture positive
	- PCR for bacterial DNA
3. Blood cultures may be positive

## Treatment
Untreated bacterial meningitis has an 80% mortality rate so empirical treatment should be started as soon as possible

Empirical treatment
1. IV Cefotaxime 2g QID or IV Ceftriaxone 2g BD
2.  + IV Vancomycin 1g BD or IV Rifampicin 600mg BD (*If suspected penicillin resistance*)
3. + IV Chloramphenicol 25mg/kg QID AND IV Vancomycin 1g BD (*If allergic to penicillins*)
4. + Dexamethasone 0.15mg/kg QID adjunctive therapy

If culture reveals N.meningitis switch to
- IV Benzylpenicillin 2.4g 6x/day

>[!faq] Post-exposure Prophylaxis for Bacterial meningitis
>Done using oral Rifampicin or Ciproloxacin
>Recommended for those
>- Who contacted oral secretions within last 7 days
>- Household contacts
>- Adjacent plane passengers (>8hours)
