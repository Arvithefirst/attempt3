Acute inflammation of the meninges presents with a classic triad of Pyrexia, Headache and *Meningism*

>[!help] Meningism consists of
	1. Headache
	2. Photophobia
	3. Neck stiffness
	4. Kernigs sign
	5. Brudzinskis sign 
{ #c0695d}


## Aetiology
Many infective and non-infective causes

[[Medicine notes/Bacterial Meningitis\|Bacterial Meningitis]]
1. Gram -ve bacilli (E.coli, Proteus)
2. Neisseria meningitis
3. Staphylococcus aureus
4. Streptococcus pneumoniae
5. Mycobacterium Tuberculosis

[[Medicine notes/Viral Meningitis\|Viral Meningitis]]
1. Enteroviruses (Cocksackie, Polio)
2. Mumps
3. Herpes viruses (Simplex 1/2, Zoster, EBV)
4. Influenza
5. HIV

Protozoal
1. Amoeba
2. Cysticerci

Fungal
1. Cryptococcus neoformans
2. Candida
3. Histoplasma

Malignancies
1. Breast cancer
2. Leukemia, Lymphoma
3. Bronchial cancer

Other
1. Sarcoidosis
2. SLE
