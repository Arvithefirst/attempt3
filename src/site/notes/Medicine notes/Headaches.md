## Tension Headache
- Most common type. Typically dull, banded, pain that may be episodic. Unknown etiology(not tension)
- Treat with "reassurance"(lol), stress management, low-dose amitryptilline

## Migraine 
- Affects 20% of all females and 6% of men. Severe throbbing pain maybe with visual disturbances(aura)
- Unknown etiology. Affected by genetic makeup, sex, stress levels

>[!Quote] Treatment of Migraine
>1. Analgesia with Paracetamol, NSAID
>2. Anti-emesis with domperidone, metaclopromide
>3. Severe attacks can be stopped with 5-HT agonists (Triptans)
>Prophylaxis with b-blockers, Anti-depressants and anti-epileptics. Avoid OC pills

## Medication overuse headache
Most frequent culprits are opiate analgesics and triptans. Typical complication of medications given for tension headaches and migraines
