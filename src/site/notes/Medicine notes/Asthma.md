Chronic inflammatory diseaseof the airways associated with Airway Hyper-Resonsiveness and elevated IgE

## Pathophysiology
Genetic related
1. Airway-Hyperreactivity (AHR)
2. Increased propensity for IgE production

*Allergen triggered mediator release* 

*In Exercise induced asthma* there is Hyperventilation -> water-loss from pericellular lining of respiratory mucosa - Release of Mediators

## Clinical features
1. Wheeze
2. [[Medicine notes/Breathlessness\|Breathlessness]]
3. Chest pain/thightness
4. Cough

>[!summary] Diagnosis of Asthma
>Clinical History + any of
>- $FEV_1$ increases by >12% after administering glucocorticoid/bronchodilator
>- $FEV_1$ Decreased >15% after 6 minutes of exercise
>- Atleast 20% variation for >3 days a week for 2 weeks

## Investigations
1. Routines + ABG
2. Spirometry
3. PEF dairy maintenance
4. Skin prick tests
5. Chest Xray - CT
6. Corticosteriod trial

## Treatment
Use short acting ß-agonists as required - Salbutamol, Terbutaline

Ramping up control measures
1. Low dose Inhaled corticosteroid(ICS)
2. Inhaled Long acting ß-agonist(LABA) + Low dose ICS
3. Increase ICS dose and/or consider Muscarinic antagonist/Leukotriene Antagonist/Theophylline
4. High dose + add from above group
5. Daliy oral steroid + High dose ICS
