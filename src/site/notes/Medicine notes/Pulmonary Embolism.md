Typically arise from DVT may also come from septic emboli, tumours, fat emboli, Amniotic emboli

## Clinical features
#### Acute massive PE
Causes major haemodynamic events -> Decreased cardiac output, Right heart failure
1. Faintness, Crushing pain, Dyspnea
2. Signs of Shock, Raised [[Medicine notes/Jugular Venous Pressure\|JVP]], Loud P$_2$ 
3. Marked decease PaO$_2$, [[Medicine notes/Metabolic Acidosis\|Metabolic acidosis]]

#### Acute small/medium PE
Occlusion of segmental pulmonary artery
1. Pleuritic chest pain, haemoptysis
2. Tachycardia, pleural rub, crackles, fever 
3. Pleural effusion on xray
4. ABG may be normal

#### Chronic PE
Occlusion of pulmonary microvasculature
1. Exertional dyspnea
2. Signs of pulmmonary hypertension, Loud P$_2$, Signs of Right heart failure
3. Enlarged pulmonary trunk. RVH on Xray

## Investigations
1. Assessment of risk factors
2. Chest Xray
3. ECG
4. Arterial Blood Gas
5. D-dimer
6. CT Pulmonary Angiogram (CTPA)
7. Ventilation Perfusion Scanning (VQ scan)
>[!faq] Xray findings in Pulmonary Embolism
>Non-specific. More useful in ruling out other diagnoses
>1. Pulmonary opacities on any size/shape
>2. Wedge opacituies
>3. Horizontal line opacities
>4. Pleural effusion
>5. Oligemia
>6. Enlarged pulmonary artery 
>7. Elevated hemidiaphragm

## Treatment
- Oxygen
- Prevention of shock with IV fluids/plasma expander
- Opiates
- Anticoagulation
- Thrombolytic surgery
- Vena caval filters
