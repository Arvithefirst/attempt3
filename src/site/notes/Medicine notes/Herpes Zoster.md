Also called shingles 

Re-infection by Varicella Zoster Virus that has been lying dormant within gaglionic roots. You can get chicken ox from shingles but not the other wway around

## Clinical features
1. Pleomorphic rash along a single dermatome(typically thoracic)
2. May have ophthalmic, Auditory, Bowel, Bladder complications bassed on ganglion affected

## Management 
1. Acyclovir
2. Treatment of neuralgia with Amitryptiline, Gabapentin etc
