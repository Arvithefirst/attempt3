Acute autoimmune response directed against ADAMST-13

#### Pathology
1. ADAMST-12 normally cleaves von WIllebrand Factor to produce normal functional units
2. Deficiencty of ADAMST-13 causes build up of vWF cross linked with platelets

## Clinical features
1. Microvascular occlusions affecting mainly brain and kidneys
2. May occur in association with shiga toxin, [[Medicine notes/HIV\|HIV]], or malignancy

## Treatment
1. Emergency plasm aexchange
2. Glucocorticoids
3. Aspirin
