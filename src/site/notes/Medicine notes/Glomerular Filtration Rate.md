A measure of Renal function that correlates to rate of filtration of substrates. Can be measured by %excretion of inert compounds like Inulin/EDTA or endogenous creatinine by the formula
$$ GFR = 120ml/min/1.73m^3 * \frac{\text{Urine concentration(umol/L) } * \text{ Volume(mL)}}{\text{Plasma concentration(umol/L) } * \text{ Time(min)}}$$
*//Constant adjusts for body surface area

# eGFR
Estimated GFR is more practical as it can be done without 24 hour unrine collection, however it has the following drawbacks
1. Is just an estimate
2. Innaccurate in low muscle mass patients / Individuals taking creatine supplements
3. Innaccurate in Elderly
4. Not applicable for minors, pregnant women
5. Doesnt adjust for racial differences
