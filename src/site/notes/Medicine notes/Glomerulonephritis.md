*Umbrella term for all glomerular diseases. 

>[!faq] Poor prognostic factors for glomerular disease
	1. Male
	2. Hypertension
	3. Persistant and severe proteinuria
	4. Elevated ceratinine
	5. Rapid decline in renal function
	6. Tubulo-interstitial Fibrosis
