May be required temporarily for patients of [[Medicine notes/Acute Kidney Injury\|Acute Kidney Injury]] or permanently by [[Medicine notes/Chronic Kidney Disease\|Chronic Kidney Disease]] Patients. *Refers to 4 main modes of treatment: Hemodialysis, Hemodiafiltration, Peritoneal Dialysis and Renal transplantation

# Hemodialysis
Most common form of RRT for ESRD but is also used in [[Medicine notes/Acute Kidney Injury\|AKI]]
- Occurs through a Central catheter or an AV fistula
- May require use of anti-coagulant 
- Usually carried out for 3-5 hours x 3 times/week - can be escalated 

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Indications</mark>
1. Fluid overload
2. Hyperkalemia
3. Uraemia
4. Metabolic Acidosis
*Non-renal indications are non-responsive pleural effusion, Pericarditis, Cardiac tamponade etc*

<mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Complications</mark>
1. Hypotension
2. Arrhythmias
3. Haemorrhage
4. Air embolism
5. Hypersesnsitivity
6. Calcium Oxalate Crystal formation in joints

# Peritoneal Dialysis
2L of sterile isotonic fluid is pumped into the peritonial cavity and left for 4-6 hours. metabolic waste diffuses through peritoneal capillaries into it.

Can be of 2 types: 
1. Continuous Ambulatory Peritoneal Dialysis - Patient is mobile
2. Automated Peritoneal Dialysis - Done at night by machine

<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Complications</mark>
1. Peritonitis, Cathetier associated infection
2. Fluid overload
3. Sclerosing peritonitis

>[!faq]
>Haemodialysis vs Peritoneal Dialysis
>CAPD is less efficient,slower, and has to be done more often but can be done at home by patient independantly adn doesnt require as much dietary restriction.
>(Ideal for children)

# Hemofiltration
(not bidirectional dialysis)
- Higher rates of filtration, suitable for AKI
- Requires replacement fluid

# Hemodiafiltration
Combines both haemodialysis and haemofiltrtion. Has advantages of both but is more expensive

# Renal Transplant
Best and most cost effective treatment for ESRD ohwever requires ABO and HLA-DR matching. Transplant patients requier life-long monitoring and face immunosuppression

Immuno suppression via Triple therapy of
1. Prednisolone
2. Ciclosporin/Tacrolimus
3. Azathioprine/Mycophenolate Mofetil
*IL-2 receptor antagonist Basiliximab may also be given
