Affects nearly 30% of all the population

## Aetiologies
1. Can occur in healthy individuals especially those with high intake of dietary fats, alcohol or coffee
2. Hiatus hernia
3. Lower oesophageal sphincter abnormalities
4. Delayed oesophageal clearance
5. Increased stomach acidity
6. Defective gastric clearance
7. Increased abdominal pressure
8. Genetic factors


## Clinical features
1. Retrosternal burning pain worsened on lying down. 
2. Dysphagia, Odynophagia, Bleeding
3. Hoarseness of voice (Acid laryngitis)

## Complications
1. Barrets oesophagus
2. Oesophagitis
3. Anemia
## Investigations
1. Ambulatory pH monitoring
2. Endoscopy *Gold standard*

## Treatment
"Step down" approach. Start with 
1. Antacids + Full dose PPI's
2. If theres good response step down to "maintanence dose PPI's" then H2 receptor antagonists
3. If not reconsider diagnosis and do fundiplication if necessary
