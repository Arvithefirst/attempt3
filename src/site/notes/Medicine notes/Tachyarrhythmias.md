Defined as *heart rhythms with a rate > 100bpm* they can be classified based on origin of the pathology as
- Supraventricular Tachycardias(SVT)
- Ventricular
or by morphological changes to ECG as
- Narrow QRS complex (<120ms/3small square)
- Wide QRS complex

>[!faq] Rule of 100s for Tachycardias
>Dictates the possible range of bpm in pathologies
>- Atrial/Ventricular tachycardia = $200 \pm 50$
>- Atrial Fibrillation = $300 \pm 50$
>- Atrial flutter = $400 \pm 50$

## Management of SVT
Section for common management of [[Atrial Fibrillation\|Atrial Fibrillation]], [[Atrial Flutter\|Atrial Flutter]], and [[Paroxysmal Supraventricular tachycardia\|Paroxysmal Supraventricular tachycardia]]. The goals of management are
- Restoration of sinus rhythm
- Thromboprophylaxis
- Treatment of underlying cause

#### Acute management
1. Ventricular rate control by AV blocking drugs
	- Adenosine *(drug of choice)*
	- Metoprolol, CCB, Digoxin, Amiadarone
2. Cardioverion - either electrical or chemical([[Medicine notes/Cardiac Arrhythmias#Class I - Sodium channel blockers\|Class Ic drugs]])
#### Long term Mangement
1. Rhythm control - Cardioversion + Anticoagulation
2. Rate control with
	- AV node slowing agents
	- Calcium channel blockers
	- B-blockers
	- Digoxin
3. *Radiofrequency ablation* is definitice long term treatment for PSVT

## Manegement of VT
Common section for Ventricular Fibrillation, Torsades des pointes, Brugada syndrome, Ventricular ectopic beats. 
1. Restore Sinus rhythm
2. Procainamide/Amiadarone
3. Correct acid-base balance
4. Prevention of recurrence(b-blockers)
5. Implantable Cardiac defibrillator
