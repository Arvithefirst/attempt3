Autoimmune cause of Hyperthyroidism

## Aetiology
More common in women 15-25, has genetic risk factors. May be trigggered by
- Post-partum
- Iodine excess
- Lithium therapy
- Bacterial/viral infections

### Pathogenesis
1. TSH Receptor antibodies
2. Thyroid stimulating immunoglobulins(TSI)
3. Long acting thyroid stimulators(LATS)
4. Exophthalmos producing substance

Continuous stimulation -> Acinar hypertrophy -> Acinar dysplasia = Small follicles with hyperplastic columnar epiuthelium

## Clinical features
1. Heat intolerance, Sweating, weight loss, Diarrhoea, Oligomenorrhoea, fine tremors
2. Uniformly enlarged gland, soft, warm with bruit
3. Hypoerkinetic movements, 
4. Tachycardia, palpitations, extra systoles
5. Thyroid eye signs
