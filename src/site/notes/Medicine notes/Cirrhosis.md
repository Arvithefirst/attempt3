Diffuse hepatic fibrosis and nodule formation. *Histological diagnosis* of long term fibrosis and hepatocyte loss leading to d*istortion of normal hepatocyte architecture* causing *portosystemic shunts* in the vasculature

### Pathophysiology
Liver injury activates stellate cells in the space of Disse into myofibro-blast like cells that are capapble of producing inflammatory markers and collagen. Cann be classified into 
 - Micronodular(>1mm)
 - Macronodular(>1mm)
 - Mixed

## Etiology
1. Alcohol
2. Chronic hepatitis
3. Non-alcoholic steatorrhic hepatitis
4. Immune - Primary sclerosing cholangitis, auto immune liver disease
5. Biliary - Primary biliary cholangitis, Cystic fibrosis
6. Genetic - Haemochromatosis, Wilsons disease
7. Cryptogenic
8. Venous outflow obstruction
9. Chronic liver conditions

## Clinical Features
1. Hepatomegal, or not who knows
2. Jaundice
3. [[Medicine notes/Ascites\|Ascites]]
4. [[Medicine notes/Signs of Hepatic Encephalopathy\|Signs of Hepatic Encephalopathy]]
5. Hemorrhagic tendency
6. [[Medicine notes/Portal Hypertension\|Portal Hypertension]]

## Investigations
1. Routines
2. LFT, pt ptt, Proteins
3. Bilirubin

Investigations aim to calculate [[Medicine notes/Child-Pugh score\|Child-Pugh score]] or [[Medicine notes/MELD score\|MELD score]] used for prognosis
