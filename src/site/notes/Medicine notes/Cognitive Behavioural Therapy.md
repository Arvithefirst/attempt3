Combined cognitive therapy and behavioural therapy

#### Cognitive therapy
Attempts to recognise and challenge systematic errors in patients thought processes. 

For example depressed patients are encouraged to challenge the *cognitive triad of depression*. This can also be used in patients of anxiety, eating and somatoform disorders
>[!faq] Cognitive triad of depression
>1. Negative view of self - "I am no good"
>2. Negative view of the weorld - "The world sucks"
>3. Negative view of the future - "The future is hopeless"

#### Behavioural therapy
Practically oriented form of therapy where patients are assisted in eliminating unhelpful behaviours.

For example exposure therapy
