Inflammatory arthritis with female reference

## Etiology 
Multifactorial genetic and Envinronmental causes
1. Over 100 loci HLA B, HLA-D
2. May be triggered infections, drugs, smoking, pregnancy

#### Pathology
1. Immune response triggered against synovium
2. Autiantibodies cause vasculitis
3. RANKL from activated Tcells cause bone erosions
4. ADAMTS and MMP responsible for cartilage breakdown
5. NO and Prostaglandins responsible for inflammation and Pain
*// RANKL - receptor activator of Nuclear Kappa B Ligand*
*// ADAMTS - Aggrecanase*
*// MMP - Matrix metalloproteinase*

## Clinical features
[[Medicine notes/ACR Diagnostic criteria\|ACR Diagnostic criteria]]
1. Pain, inflammation, swelling of small joints symmetrically 
2. Early morning stiffness, pitting edema
3. May be palindromic in onset
4. Popliteal "bakers" cysts
>[!info] Characteristic deformities of Rheumatoid Arthritis
>1. *Spindling of fingers*(inflammation of procimal but not distal joints)
>2. *Ulnar deviation* - Subluxation of Metacarpopharyngeal(MCP) joints
>3. *Swan neck deformity* - Hyperextension of PIP and flexion of DIP
>4. *Button-hole deformity* - Extension of PIP and flexion of DIP
>6. *Z-deformity of thumb* - Extension of first Interpharyngeal and flexion of MCP
>7. Carpal tunnel
>8. *Broadening of forefoot* - due to swelling
>9. *Cock-up deformity of toes* - Subluxation of toes at MTP joints 
>10. *Hallux valgus deformity*

#### Extra-articular manifestations of Rheumatoid Arthritis
1. Systemic - fever, fatigue, weight loss
2. Musculoskeletal - Wasting, Osteoporosis, Bursitis
3. Haematological - Anemia, Thrombocytopenia
4. Lymphatic - Felty syndrome, Splenomegaly
5. Ocular - Episcelritis, Scleritis, Keratoconjunctivitis sicca
6. Vasculitis - Ulcers, Digital arthritis
7. Cardiac - Pericarditis, Myocarditis
8. Pulmonary - Pleural effusion, [[Medicine notes/Pulmonary Nodule\|Pulmonary Nodule]]
9. Neurological - Peripheral neuropathy, Compression
10. Amyloidosis

## Investigations
1. Routines - ESR CRP
2. Rheumatoid Factor(RF) estimation
3. Anti Citrullinted Peptides Antibodies (ACPA)
4. Xray small joints
5. MRI bakers cysts
6. Disease Activity Score - 28 (DAS28)

## Treatment
Depends DAS28 score

1. Treatment with Prednisolone gradually tapering off and Methotrexate gradually increasing dose uptil 12 weeks
2. If DAS28 > 2.6 add Sulfasalazine/Hydroxychloroquine
	1. If No improvement - Change DMARD or add low dose prednisolone
	2. If DAS28 > 5.1 add Biologic

Also 
- Physical/occupational therpy
- Intraarticular injections
- Joint replacement surgeries
- Joint excision surgeries
- Neurosurgery for pain relief
