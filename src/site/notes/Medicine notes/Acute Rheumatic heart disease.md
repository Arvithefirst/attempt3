Usually affects children 5-15 post streptococcal infection

## Aetiopathogenesis
Certain Group A streptococci can trigger cross-immunisation against cardiac myosin and sacrolemmal membrane proteins causing
- Inflammation of heart tissue and formation of *Aschoff nodules
- Fibrinoid degeneration of collagen and connective tissue
*// Pathognomic sign. Multinucleated giant cells surrounded by Macrophages and T cells

## Clinical Features
Multisystem involvement presenting with Fever, Anorexia, Lethargy 2-3 weeks after Streptococcal pharyngitis

## Investigations
1. ESR/CRP 
2. Anti-streptolysin O antibody titres
3. Echocardiogram (typically shows mitral regurgitation)
4. Chest X-ray and ECG

## Treatment
1. Bed rest
2. Treatment of heart failure
3. Benzathine Penicillin 1.2 Million units IM
4. Aspirin 60mg/kg/day divided into 6 doses/day
