Thrombotic Microangiopathy particularly affecting renal circulation

## Aetiopathogenesis
Most commonly seen in infections of organisms that produce enterotoxins called *"Shiga-like toxins."* May also be genetic or drug use related 
1. Enterohemorrhagic E.coli, Shigella Dysenteriae
2. Complement mediated (genetic)
3. Drugs (Quinine, VEGF-A inhibitors)

*// Shiga +ve HUS is the most common cause of [[Medicine notes/Acute Kidney Injury\|Acute Kidney Injury]] in children*

## Clinical features
Classical triad of HUS
1. Thrombocytopenia
2. Microangiopathic hemolytic anemia
3. [[Medicine notes/Acute Kidney Injury\|Acute Kidney Injury]]

## Treatment
1. Supportive therapy until infection is over
2. [[Medicine notes/Renal Replacement Therapy\|Renal Replacement Therapy]]
3. Cessation of offending drug
4. C5 complement inhibitor - Eculizumab
