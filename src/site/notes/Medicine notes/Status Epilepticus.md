Seizure activity that doesnt either resolve spontaneously or recurs without prior recovery of consciousness 

# Management

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Initial steps</mark>
1. Ensure Airway, Breathing, Circulation and Monitor Pulse, BP, RR
2. Secure IV access
3. Send Investigations
	1. BSL
	2. Urea and electrolytes
	3. LFTs
	4. CBC, coagulation testing
	5. Drug testing

<mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">At 5 minutes</mark> give any of
- Midazolam 10mg Buccal
- Lorazepam 4mg IV
- Diazepam 10mg Rectal

<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">At 30 minutes</mark> give IV infusion of
- Phenytoin 15mg/kg at 50mg/min
- Valproate 20-30mg/kg at 40mg/min
- Phenobarbital 10mg/kg at 100mg/min
*Monitor cardiac and neurological function

<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">At 60 minutes</mark>
- Move into intensive care -> Intubation, general anesthesia with propofol

<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">After episode </mark>start longer term treatment with any of
- Valproate 10mg/kg IV over 3-5 minutes followed by 800-1000mg/day
- Phenytoin loading dose(above) followed 300mg/day
- Carbamazapine 400mg by NG tube followed by 400-1200mg/day
