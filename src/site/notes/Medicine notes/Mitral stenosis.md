Almost always rheumatic. Rarely congenital

5 $mm^3$(normal) > 2 $mm^3$(asymptomatic) > 1 $mm^3$(symptomatic)

## Clinical features
1. Fatigue, breathlessness, palpitations, Chest pain
2. Thrombotic events
3. Dilated Left atrium, atrial fibrillation
4. Giant "v" wave JVP
5. Loud S1, Opening snap, Mid diastolic murmur

## Investiagtions
1. ECG - Tall R waves, P mitrale
2. Chest Xray - Dilated left atrium, Pulmonary congestion
3. Echo - Immobile smaller valves, Slower diastolic filling
4. Doppler - Pressure gradient

## Management
Treat [[Medicine notes/Acute Rheumatic heart disease\|Acute Rheumatic heart disease]]
1. ß-blockers
2. Diuretic
3. Valvuloplasty/ Valve replacement
