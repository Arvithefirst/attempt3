Heterogenous group of immune mediated conditions. Most commonly
- Acute Inflammatory Demyelinating Polyneuropathy (AIDP)
- Acute Motor Axonal Degeneration (AMAN)
- Acute Motor and Sensory Axonal Degeneration (AMSAN)

## Clinical features
1. Distal parasthesia
2. Ascending paralysis more pronounced in proximal muscles
3. Facial > Bulbar > Respiratory weakness

## Investigations
1. Things to rule out
2. Proteins in CSF
3. GM1 ganglioside Antibodies

## Treatment
1. Plasma replacement
2. IV immunoglobulins
