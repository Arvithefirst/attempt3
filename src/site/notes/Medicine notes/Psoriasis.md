Chronic, inflammatory, hyperproliferative disease characterised by well defined red scaly plaques particularly on extensor surfaces

## Etiolgy
1. Whites > Asians
2. Bimodal age distribution early adults and 50s
3. Strong genetic association with HLA-C mutation (Chromosome 6)
4. Associated with environmental stressors - Alcohol, stress, Sunlight, Trauma, Drugs

## Clinical features
Typically red scaly plaques over extensor surfaces. May haev bleeding. poiunts, may be white/silvery. May present as
1. Guttate psoriasis - Typically in children, seen over belly. Responds well to UV-B
2. Pustular psoriasis - Can be localised or generalised. May be reaction to steroids
3. Erythrodermic -Generalised psoriasis is a medical emergency 
4. Arthropathy - 5-10% complication ivolves nails

## Investigations 
1. Infection screening
2. Skin Biopsy

## Treatment 
1. Emollients, Vitamin D, Retinoids
2. Topical glucocorticoids
3. UV-B Phototherapy
4. Immunosuppressants - Methothrexate, Cyclosporin
5. Biologics
