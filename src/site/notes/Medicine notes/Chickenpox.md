Infection by HHV - 3 or Varicella Zoster Virus(VZV.) Part of the [[Medicine notes/Herpes Family Tree <3\|Herpes Family Tree <3]]
Typically a benign illness of children (aged 5 - 9)
## Mode of Transmission
1. Droplets
2. Direct contact with ruptured lesions

## Clinical features
1. Centripetal Maculopapular pleomorphic rash appearing on 2nd day
2. New crops of lesions every 2-4 days
3. Enanthem rash on mucoius membranes

## Investigations
1. Detection of antigen by IF or DNA PCR
2. Serology
3. Isolation of virus from pustules

## Treatment 
1. Hygeine, avoid complications
2. Avoid Symptomatiuc treatment but avoid Aspirin(Reyes)
>[!faq] Antiviral therapy
>Not indicated for healthy children
>- Acyclovir 1g TDS
>- Valacyclovir 250mg TDS
>- Famiciclovir 500mg TID


## Prevention
1. Live attenuated vaccine
2. Zoster Immunoglobulin(ZIG) or Varicella-Zoster Immunoglobulin(VZIG)
3. Antiviral therapy as prophylaxis

## Complications
1. Secondary bacterial infection of skin lesions
2. CNS (Ataxia, Encephalitis, Aseptic meningitis, Gullain-Barre syndrome)
3. Acute [[Medicine notes/Glomerulonephritis\|Glomerulonephritis]]
4. Bleeding disorders
5. [[Medicine notes/Herpes Zoster\|Herpes Zoster]]
