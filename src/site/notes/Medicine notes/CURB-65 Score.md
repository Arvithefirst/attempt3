Tool used to assess severity of pneumonia. Each parameter is worth 1 point
1. *C*onfusion <8 on Abbreviated Mental Test (AMT score)
2. *U*rea > 7mmol/L
3. *R*espiratory rate >30/min
4. *B*lood pressure < 90/60mmHg
5. Age > *65*

Score 0-1 eligible for at home care
Score 2 hospital supervised treatment - OPD or Short Admission
Score >3 ICU admission(especially for 4 or 5)
