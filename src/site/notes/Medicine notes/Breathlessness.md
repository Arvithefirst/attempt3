## MMRC classification
Modified Medical Research Council. Used in Respiratory Pathologies
*Grade 0* - Only on Exertion
*Grade I* - Only on Walking uphill
*Grade II* - On walking less than someone else of similar age
*Grade III* - On walking 100m
*Grade IV* - On regular activity

## NYHA Classification
New York Heart Association. Used in cardiac Pathologies
*Grade I* - No limitation of physical activity
*Grade II* - Slight limitation of physical activity
*Grade III* - Marked limitation in physical activity
*Grade IV* - At rest
