*Albumin:Creatinine excretion ratio in urine(x) < what can be detected by dipstick but greater than normal*

$$ 2.5 _\text{(in females) } \text{ or } 3.5 _\text{(in males) } < x < 30$$
May indicate early glomerular pathology. 

- Indicates use of RAS inhibitors to protect against loss of renal function.
- Persistant microalbuminuria is associated with increased cardiovascular mortality
