Most common form of dementia
#### Pathogenesis
1. May be autosomal dominant type or Polygenic type
2. Macroscopic atrophy of brain particularly cerebral cortex/Hippocampus
3. Histologically - Senile plaques and neuropibrillary tangles
4. Histochemically amyloid plaques

## Clinical features
1. Memory loss (first short term then long term)
2. Apraxia, Aphasia, Visuo- spatial impairment
3. Anosognosia - Inabiility to recognise something is wrong
4. Depression, Loss of cognitive function

## Investiagations
1. Rule out treatable cuases of [[Medicine notes/Dementia\|Dementia]]

## Treatment
1. Centrally acting anti-cholinesterases
2. NMDA receptor antagonist
3. Anti-depressants [[Medicine notes/Amazon Wishlist\|Amazon Wishlist]]
