also called Thyroid storm, Thyrotoxicosis, Thyrotoxic crisis. It is a medical emergency that is a complication of Thyrotoxicosis

## Etiology
1. Severe infection in inadequately treated hyperthyroidism
2. Following subtotal thyroidectomy
3. Following radiotherapy with Iodine dye
4. [[Medicine notes/Stroke\|Stroke]], [[Medicine notes/Diabetic ketoacidosis\|Diabetic ketoacidosis]], [[Medicine notes/Acute Coronary Syndrome\|Acute Coronary Syndrome]], Stress, Trauma

## Clinical features
1. Hyperpyrexia (104-106F)
2. Tachycardia
3. CNS - Agitation, restlessness, delirium
4. GIT - Nausea, vomitting, diarrhoea, jaundice

## Management
1. Control of hyperpyrexia without Salicylates*
2. Propanolol
3. Antithyroid Thionamine drugs (Propylthiouracil/Carbimazole)
4. Lugoils iodine
5. Benzodiazepine
6. Corticosteroids
7. Bile acid sequestrants
8. Digoxin
