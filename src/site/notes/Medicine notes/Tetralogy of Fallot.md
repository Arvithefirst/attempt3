Most common cause of cyanosis in infancy after 1st year of life. 1 in 2000 prevalence

# <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Components of Tetralogy of Fallot</mark>
1. Pulmonary Stenosis
2. Over-riding of Aorta
3. Ventricular septal defect
4. Right ventricular hypertrophy

# Clinical Features
Signs
1. <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Fallot Spells</mark> (Crying or cyanosis particularly after feeding)

Symptoms
1. Stunting
2. [[Medicine notes/Clubbing\|Clubbing]]
3. Polycythemia
4. <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Fallots Sign</mark> (Symptoms relieved on squatting)
5. Loud ejection systolic murmur in pulmonary region

# Investigations
1. Chest Xray - <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">"Boot shaped" Heart</mark>
2. ECG - Right ventricular hypertrophy
3. Echo - over-riding of Aorta

# Treatment
Total surgical correction can be done in children <5 years of age.
If pulmonary arteries are too hypoplastic then Blalock-Taussig shunt is performed (Pulm. artery in anastomosed with subclavian)
