Obsessive compulsive disorder is a psychiatric disrder characterised by *recurrent 'obsessions' that are anxiety-inducing and unwanted;* typically resulting in 'compulsive' acts that are repeated to relieve said anxiety

### Clinical features
Typically the obsessions take the form 
- thoughts of contamination leading to excessive hand washing
- thoughts of having forgotten something leading to repeated, time consuming checking
