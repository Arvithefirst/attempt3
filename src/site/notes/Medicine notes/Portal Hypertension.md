>[!Summary] Definition
Prolonged elevation of the portal venous pressure >30cm saline *OR* elevation of Hepatic Venous Pressure Gradient(HVPG) more than 7mmHg

## Classification
By site of obstruction
#### Prehepatic Presinusoidal 
- Portal vein thrombosis
- Coagulopathies
- Abdominal trauma
#### Intrahepatic presinusoidal
- Schistosomiasis
- Congenital hepatic fibrosis
- Drugs
- Sarcoidosis
#### Sinusoidal
- Cirrhosis
- Polycystic liver disease
- Malignancy
#### Intrahepatic Postsinusoidal
- Veno Occlusive Disorders
#### Posthepatic Postsinusoidal
- Budd Chiari Syndrome (Narrowing of hepatic veins)
- Constrictive pericarditis

## Clinical features
Often asymptomatic
1. History of Alcoholism, Past hepatitis, Distension
>[!bug] Triad of Portal hypertension
>1. [[Medicine notes/Ascites\|Ascites]]
>2. Splenomegaly
>3. Dilated tortuous Abdominal veins

2. Bleeding from esophageal varices. Associated symtpms and signs
3. [[Medicine notes/Signs of Hepatic Encephalopathy\|Signs of Hepatic Encephalopathy]]

## Investigations
Usually clinical but see also
1. Barium swallow - (Bag of worms in eosophageal varices)
2. Upper GI scopy (cherry red spots indicate impending rupture of varices)
3. USG
4. Portal venography
5. Transghepatic venous poressure
6. Proctoscopy
7. LFT

## Treatment
>[!faq] Some numbers I found in SRB
>30% of patients with cirrhosis develop portal hypertension. 30% of them will have variceal bleed within 2 years. 70% of them will rebleed
>
>30% of patients with varices will experience bleed. 30% of those will die from bleed
#### Decrease portal blood flow
1. B-blockers
2. Somatostatin
3. Vasopressin
#### Decrease intrahepatic resistance
1. a1 blockers
2. Angiotenisn receptor Blockers
3. Nitrates
## Complications
1. Variceal bleeding from [[Medicine notes/Areas of Portocaval anastomosis\|Areas of Portocaval anastomosis]] specifically [[Surgery Notes/Oesophageal varices\|Oesophageal varices]]
2. Hepatic encephalopahty
3. [[Medicine notes/Ascites\|Ascites]], Subacute Bacterial peritonitis
4. Hepatorenal syndrome
5. Hypersplenism
6. Iron deficiency anemia
