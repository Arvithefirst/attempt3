#### Tricyclic Anti-depressants(TCA's)
*Amitriptiline, Imipramine, Clomipramine*
Inhibit reuptake of 5-HT and Noradrenaline

Side effects - sedation, Anticholinergic effects, postural hypotension, lowers seizure threshold, Cardiotoxic.
+++ Overdose potential

#### Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors(SSRI's)
*Fluoxetine, Sertraline*
Inhibit only 5-HT reuptake(less anticholinergic/sedative effects)

Side effects - QTc prolongation, Nausea, anorexia, Sexual dysfunction, 'serotonin syndrome' with other drugs

#### Selective Noradrenaline Reuptake inhibitors(SNRI's)
*Mirtazapine, Venlafaxine, Duloxetine*
Inhibit Noradrenaline reuptake, latter 2 are also SSRIs

Side effects - same

#### Monoamine Oxidase inhibitors(MAO's)
*Phenelzine, Moclobemide*
Inhibit 5-HT and noradrenaline synthesis

Side effects - "Cheese reaction" to foods with Tyrosine or Amphetamines or Anesthetics
