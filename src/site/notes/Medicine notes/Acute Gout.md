The most common inflammatory arthritis in men and older women. Caused by deposition of *Monosodium Urate Crystals*

## Aetiopathogenesis
Crystal formation occurs due to Hyperuricaemia which may be caused by

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Diminished renal excretion of uric acid</mark>
1. Genetic pre-disposition
2. Renal failure
3. [[Medicine notes/Lead poisoning\|Lead poisoning]]
4. Lactic Acidosis
5. Alcoholism (predominantly beer)
6. Drugs (Thiazides, NSAIDs, Ciclosporin)

<mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Increased Intake </mark>
1. Seafood
2. Red Meat
3. Offal

<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Increased Production</mark>
1. Myelo/lymphoproliferative disease
2. [[Medicine notes/Psoriasis\|Psoriasis]]
3. [[USMLE/Glycogen Storage Diseases\|Glycogen Storage Diseases]]
4. Genetic conditions ([[USMLE/Lesch-Nyhan Syndrome\|Lesch-Nyhan Syndrome]])

## Clinical Features
Typically presents as monoarthritis of 1st Metatarsophalangeal joint(big toe lol)
- Rapid onset typically in the early morning
- Severe pain reaching peak in 2-6 hours
- Extreme tenderness
- Red, shiny overlying skin
- Self limiting in 5-14 days

>[!fail] Complications
> - Crystals in joints form firm, white nodules called *tophi* typically on the extensor surfaces, Helix of ear, or Achilles Tendon. In chronic disease, these tophi may ulcerate
> - Chronic hyperuricaemia may cause crystal deposition in kidneys leading to renal stone formation and eventually *Interstitial nephritis*
>   
 
## Investigations
1. Arthrocentesis
2. Renal Function tests
3. BSL and lipid profile
4. CRP and ESR
5. X-ray may show degenerations in Chronic gout

## Treatment
1. Rest and Ice pack
2. Oral Colchicine 0.5ml x 2-3 times/day (*First line drug*)
3. Oral NSAID
4. Oral Prednisolone
5. Joint Aspiration
6. Intra-articular glucocorticoid
*// Colchicine is an anti-mitotic that inhibits CRP and therefore inflammatory response*

>[!info] Prophylaxis of gout - Urate-lowering Therapy
> Given to individuals with >1 attack in 12 months with complications such as tophi. 
> 1. Xanthine oxidase inhibitors -> Allopurinol, Febuxostat
> 2. Uricosuric drugs -> Probenecid
> 3. Biological Uricase -> Pegloticase
> Also lifestyle changes to avoid beer, sea food etc
