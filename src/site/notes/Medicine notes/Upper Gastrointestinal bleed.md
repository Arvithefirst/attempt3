Most common Gastrointestinal emergency

## Causes
1. Peptic ulcer
2. Gastric Erosions
3. [[Gastro-oesophageal Varices\|Gastro-oesophageal Varices]]
4. Oesophagitis
5. Vascular malformation
6. Mallory-Weiss tear
7. Malignancy
8. Aorto-duodenal fistula

## Clinical features
1. Coffee-ground hematemsis
2. Hypotension, Syncope, Pallor
3. Malena

## Management
Initially
1. IV access
2. Clinical circulatory/hepatic assessment, Identify comorbidities
3. CBC, LFT, Urea, Electrolytes, Blood typing
*//these steps allow for risk assessment via Blatchford score*

After rescucitation
	1. Endoscopic diagnosis and hemostasis.
	2. 24h Monitoring of pulse, BP, Urine output
	3. Surgery if endoscopic hemostasis fails
	4. Eradication of H.Pylori infection if present
