Examination by Naffzigers method

*Stellwags sign - Infrequent blinking - First to appear*
von Gaefes sign - Lid lag - Mild disease
Joffroys sign - Absence of wrinkling - Moderate disease
*Moebius sign - Loss of convergence - Last to appear*

Dalrymples sign - Upper eyelid retraction
Enroth sign - Eyelid/conjunctiva oedema
Gifflord sign - Difficulty everting upper eyelid
Kocher sign - Upper eyelid moves up faster than eyebrows
Jellineks sign - Increased pigmentation around eyelid margins
Rosenbachs sign - Tremor of closed eyelids
Loewis sign - P{upil dilates with weak adrenaline soluttion
Knies sign - Unequal dilation 
Cowens sign - Jerky pupillary contraction
