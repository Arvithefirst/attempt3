Most common cause of meningitis. *Usually self-limiting* much less severe unless accompanied by encephalitis

## Causative agents
1. Enteroviruses (Cocksackie, Polio)
2. Mumps
3. Herpes viruses (Simplex 1/2, Zoster, EBV)
4. Influenza
5. HIV

## Clinical Features
Typically seen in children or young adults. Sudden onset high pyrexia, irritability and [[Medicine notes/Meningitis#^c0695d\|Meningism]]. Focal neuropathies are rare

## Investigations
1. Lumbar Puncture - CSF lymphocytosis with normal glucose/proteins

## Treatment
Self limiting within a week. Provide Symptomatic treatment
