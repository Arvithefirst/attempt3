Progressive neurtodegenerative movement disorder associated with Bradykinesia, Riugidity, Tremors and loss of postural reflexes

## Causes
1. Idiopathic(80%) - Influenced by genetics, Age
2. Drugs
	1. Anti-psychotics (more commonly atypical or older ones)
	2. Metoclopramide. Valproate
3. Other degenerative diseases
	1. Lewy Body Dementia
	2. [[Medicine notes/Alzheimers Disease\|Alzheimers Disease]]
4. Genetic
	1. Huntingtons
	2. Wilsons disease
	3. Fragile X syndrome
5. Anoxic Brain injury

#### Pathology
1. Depletion of dopaminergic receptors in Substantia Nigra
2. Presence of ⍺-synuclein and other proteins

## Clinical Features
1. Bradykinesia (Micrographia, trouble tying shoes, buttons)
2. Unilateral rest tremors
3. Slow to start gait, with short strides, reduced arm movements and Impaired balance
4. Cogwheel rigidity of upper limbs. Also Lead pipe rigidity
5. Hyposmia, Mood disorders, Sleep disturbances, Constipation

## Investigations
Clinical diagnosis
1. CT/MRI usually normal
2. Functional dopaminergic scan(SPECT, PET)
3. Genetic testing

## Management
1. *Exogenonus dopamine precursor - Levadopa*
2. *Dopa decarboxylase inhibitors - Carbidopa*
3. Dopamine Agonists - Bromocriptine, Apomorphine
4. MAO-inhibitors - Selegiline
5. Catechol-O-Methyl Transferase (COMT) inhibitors - Entacapone
6. Amantidine
7. Anti-cholinergics
8. Stereotactic surgery
9. Physiotherapy, Speech therapy
