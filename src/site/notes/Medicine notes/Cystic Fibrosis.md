Autosomal recessive disorder affecting *Cystic Fibrosis Transmembrane-conductance Regulator (CFTR) gene* on the long arm of Chromosome 7

#### Pathology
CFTR gene is responsible for regulating salt and water movement across cell membranes. *Relative dehydration of airways predisposes to Bronchiectasis, Chronic infections and Ciliary dysfunction*. Also effects GIT epithelium, pancreas, liver and reproductive tract

## Clinical features
1. Bronchiectasis
2. Repeated LRTI

### Complications
Respiratory and otherwise
#### Respiratory
1. Hemoptysis
2. Progessing obstruction
3. Pulmonary hypertension
4. Respiratory failure
#### GIT
1. Malabsorption, steatorrhoea
2. [[Surgery Notes/Gallstones\|Gallstones]]
3. [[Medicine notes/Portal Hypertension\|Portal Hypertension]], [[Surgery Notes/Oesophageal varices\|Oesophageal varices]], Splenomegaly
#### Others
1. [[Medicine notes/Diabetes Mellitus\|Diabetes Mellitus]]
2. Delayed puberty, [[OBGY Notes/Male infertility\|Male infertility]], Stress incontinence
3. Osteoporosis, Arthralgia

## Investigations
1. Ruling out other stuff
2. Genotyping
3. Finding complications

## Treatment
1. Prophylactic antibiotics
2. Bronchodilators, Glucocorrticoids if asthma
3. CFTR potentiators
4. Supplemental feeding, could be nasogastric, gastrostomy tube
