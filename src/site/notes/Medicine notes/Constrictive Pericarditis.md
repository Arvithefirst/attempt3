Caused by progressing thickening, fibrosis and calcification fo the percardium

## Etiology
1. Post tubercular pericarditis
2. Complication of haemopercardium, viral percarditis
3. [[Medicine notes/Rheumatoid Arthritis\|Rheumatoid Arthritis]]

## Clinical features
Systemic venous congestion
1. Fatigue
2. Low volume pulse, Rapid y descent, Pulsus paradoxus, Kussmauls sign
4. Atrial fibrillation, Pericardial knock
5. Dramatic Ascitis, Hepatomegaly

## Investigations
1. Chest Xray + CT
2. Doppler
3. Cardiac Catheterisation

## Management
1. Loop diuretics
2. Aldosterone antagonists
3. Surgical resection of fibrosed tissue
