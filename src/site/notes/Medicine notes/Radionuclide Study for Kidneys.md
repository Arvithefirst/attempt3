Functional studies involving the injection of gamma ray-emitting pharmaceuticals 

## Dynamic studies 
Done with *Tc-MAG3* (Technetium - Mercaptoacetyltriglycine)
Measures kidney perfusion but not reliable for renal artery stenosis

## GFR Calculation
Done with *Tc-DPTA* (Technetium Diethylenetriamine Penta-acetic acid)

## Static studies
Done with *Tc-DMSA* (Dimercaptosuccinic acid)
Measures cortical scarring and individual kidney function


## Bone studies
Done with *Tc-MDP* (Methylene diphosphate)
Measures extent of bone metastases of prostate cancer
