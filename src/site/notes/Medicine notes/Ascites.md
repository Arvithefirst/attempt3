## Investigations
1. Clinical examination - Puddles sign, Shifting dullness, horseshoe dullness, Fluid thrill. Signs of Cirrhosis, infection, malignancy
2. USG
3. Paracentesis - Proteins, Gram stain, cytology
4. Serum Proteins (Serum Ascitis AG ratio)
5. Serum amylase

## Differential diagnoses
lots a ways but choosing by SAAG ratio
#### High SAAG ratio 
(>1.1g/dl)
- Portal hypertension
- Hepatic outflow obstruction
- Alscoholic hepatitis
- Cardiac failure, Rigth sided heart failure, Constrictive pericarditis
- Myxodema
#### Low SAAG Ratio 
(<1.1g/dl)
- Peritoneal TB
- Carcinoma
- Pancreatitis
- [[Medicine notes/Nephrotic Syndrome\|Nephrotic Syndrome]]
- Bowel infarct

## Management
1. Hospitalisation, serum electrolytes, urine monitoring
2. Fluid and sodium restriction (avoid drugs with sodium too)
3. Diuretics (Spironolactone(100mg/day) > Furesemide (40mg/day))
