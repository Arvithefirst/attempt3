Irreversible deterioration of kidney function over a period of many years. Eventually **leads to loss of excretory, metabolic and endocrine function of the kidneys leading to signs of kidney failure collectively known as Uremia**

# Aetiology
1. [[Medicine notes/Diabetes Mellitus\|Diabetes Mellitus]]
2. Interstitial disease
3. Glomerular disease
4. Hypertension
5. Systemic Inflammatory Response Syndrome
6. Congenital 
7. Unknown

# Clinical Features
<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Symptoms</mark>
1. Nocturia
2. Fatigue, Vomitting, Nausea, Weight loss, Pruritis

<mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Signs</mark>
1. Pallor, Icterus
2. Pulsus paradoxicus
3. Kussmaul Breathing
4. Peripheral Neuropathy

# Investigations
1. Urine urea/[[Medicine notes/Glomerular Filtration Rate\|creatinine]] for monitoring progression
2. Urinalysis
3. Electrolytes
4. Calcium, Vit D, PTH
5. Albumin
6. CBC, Iron Studies, B12 to monitor Anemia
7. Lipid Profile, BSL + HbA1C
8. Renal Ultrasound
9. HbsAg, HIV testing
10. Tests for suspected cause based on history

>[!faq] Grading of CKD (Using GFR values)
>Grade 1 - GFR >90 mL/min
>Grade 2 - GFR <90 mL/min
>Grade 3a - GFR <60 mL/min
>Grade 3b - GFR <45 mL/min
>Grade 4 - GFR <30 mL/min
>Grade 5 - GFR <15 mL/min

# Management
There are 5 main aims in Management of CKD

<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Monitoring</mark>
- RFTs every 6 months upto Stage 3. More frequent in progresssed disease
- Can assess effectiveness of treatment and predict ESRF to allow for timely RRT

<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Slowing</mark>
- Hypertensive therapy to ease load on kidneys and prevent cardiac complications
- Reducing Proteinuria (with ACE's and ARB's)

<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Treat complications</mark>
- Maintain Fluid-electrolyte balance
	1. Dietary Protein / Potassium restriction
	2. Zirconium cyclosilicate/Patiromer (reduce K+ absorption)
	3. Diuretics
- Prevent [[Medicine notes/Renal Bone disease\|Renal Bone disease]]
- Correct [[Medicine notes/Anemia in CKD\|Anemia in CKD]]

Prevent Cardiovascular disease
- CVS disease risk is increased at GFR <60mmol/min
- Treat with lifestyle changes, statins
Prepare for [[Medicine notes/Renal Replacement Therapy\|Renal Replacement Therapy]]
