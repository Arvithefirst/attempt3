Refers either [[Medicine notes/Crohns Disease\|Crohns Disease]] or [[Medicine notes/Ulcerative colitis\|Ulcerative colitis]]

## Pathophysiology
- *Genetic and environmental factors* lead to abnormal inflammation of bowel tissue triggering cascade and causing damage to mucosa
- *Microbial dysbiosis* plays an impolrtant role

## Differential Diagnoses
Infective
- Bacterial
	- Salmonella
	- Shigella
	- E.coli
	- Gonococcal proctitis
- Viral
	- Herpes simplex proctitis
	- Cytomegalovirus
- Protozoal
	- Ameobiasis

Non-infective
- Ischaemic colitis
- Diverticulitis
- NSAID
- Bechets disease

## Complications
1. Life threatnening colonic inflammation
2. Hemorrhage
3. Fistulae *(Typically in CD)*
4. Cancer
5. Extra-intestincal complications
	1. Conjuctivitis, Iritis, Scleritis
	2. Mouth ulcer
	3. Liver abscess, Fatty liver, Autoimmune hepatitis, [[Surgery Notes/Gallstones\|Gallstones]]
	4. Thrombus formation, Portal vein thrombus
	5. Large joint arthritis, Metabolic bone disease, Sacroilieitis

## Investigations
1. CBC, Iron study, B12, folate, ESR, CRP
2. Bacteriology
3. Endoscopy
4. Barium enema, MRI enterography, Xray/USG abdomen

## Indications for surgery
1. Impaired QoL
2. Failure of medical therapy
3. Fulminant colitis
4. Cancer or sever dysplasia
